# Discovery Call Checklist

A discovery call is only as strong as the weakest thing you assumed instead of confirmed. This is how you close one out clean — cold call, referral, or re-engagement alike.

## Lesson Objective

- Run the checklist as a live diagnostic, not just an after-the-fact review
- Close specific gaps with one targeted question, not a full re-interview
- End every call with the candidate stating what happens next, in their own words — including which channel that follow-up will actually happen on

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't let "I'll sort it out later" energy creep into your own tone, and don't let a casual channel make the close feel casual too — the gap still needs a name and an owner.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Asking "how should I actually reach you" alongside "what happens next" confirmed the channel, not just the timing — important when the call itself started on WhatsApp rather than a scheduled call.
</CoachingNotes>

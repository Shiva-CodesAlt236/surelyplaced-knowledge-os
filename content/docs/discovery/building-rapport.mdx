# Building Rapport

You got past the opener — Rahul stayed on the line, gave you thirty honest seconds. Rapport is what happens next: turning "still on the call" into "actually talking to you."

## Lesson Objective

- Respond to a disclosure in a way that proves you were listening, not just waiting your turn
- Use silence on purpose, especially with a candidate who's still deciding if this call is worth it
- Notice the exact moment a guarded outbound candidate's answers start opening up — or closing further

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't reassure with facts before acknowledging the feeling. "It'll be fine" is what everyone else says — that's exactly why it doesn't land as rapport.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
"Can you say more about that" cost nothing and signaled the disclosure mattered. A WhatsApp yes only gets you the call — this is what actually earns the conversation.
</CoachingNotes>

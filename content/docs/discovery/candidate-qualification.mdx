# Candidate Qualification

A referral feels like it's already vetted — a friend sent them, so surely they're a fit. That feeling is exactly what makes referrals the easiest place to skip a check you'd never skip on a cold call.

## Lesson Objective

- Confirm work authorization without it feeling like an interrogation, referral or not
- Get a real, concrete picture of IT background — not just enthusiasm or a friend's endorsement
- Say "this isn't a fit" clearly and kindly when that's the honest answer, even for a referral

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't let a referral's warmth turn "constraints" into "don't worry about it." Priya's friend vouched for the experience, not for skipping the honest parts.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Priya directly names the risk this lesson exists to prevent — that referral warmth might mean getting told what she wants to hear instead of the truth. Running the real check is what avoided that.
</CoachingNotes>

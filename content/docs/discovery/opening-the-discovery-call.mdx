# Opening the Discovery Call

## Start Here

**When do I use this module?** Discovery is your very first real conversation with a candidate — and for most of our calls, they didn't ask for it. They're on LinkedIn, at work, on WhatsApp, mid-day, not expecting to hear from you.

**What will this module help me achieve?** By the end, you'll open any outbound call — cold, warm, referral, or re-engaged — in a way that earns 90 honest seconds instead of an instant hang-up, and turns those seconds into a real discovery conversation.

**When should I move to the next module?** Once you can open a call, get past the first objection, and get a real answer to "why are we even talking" — move to Discussion, where the conversation about SurelyPlaced itself begins.

Most candidates who answer this call have never heard of SurelyPlaced. They didn't apply anywhere. Your name means nothing to them yet. The first 20 seconds decide whether they stay on the line at all.

## Lesson Objective

- Open cold, without assuming any prior awareness of who you are or what SurelyPlaced does
- Use a permission opener and a pattern interrupt to earn real attention, fast
- Handle "I'm busy," "where did you get my number," "who are you," and "not interested" without losing the call

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
"I'm busy" is rarely a hard no — it's a test of whether you'll respect it or argue with it. Shrinking your ask instead of defending it is what actually earns the next thirty seconds.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Naming the cold call directly, answering "where did you get my number" without flinching, and shrinking the ask under "I'm busy" — three separate objections, three honest, un-defensive answers, and Rahul is still on the line.
</CoachingNotes>

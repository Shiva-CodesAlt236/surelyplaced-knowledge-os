# Discovery Calls — Module Overview

## Purpose

Every candidate relationship at Surely Placed starts here, and almost never because the candidate asked for it. This module is about the outbound call — cold, warm, referral, or re-engaged — and the specific skill the whole academy builds from: finding the real pain underneath a vague "I guess I'm open to hearing more."

## When You'll Use This

You'll use Discovery on the very first real conversation with a candidate, before they've decided whether Surely Placed is worth their time. That's most of your calls — someone on LinkedIn, at work, on WhatsApp, mid-day, not expecting to hear from you. Discovery is also the module you return to any time a candidate re-engages after going quiet, since a re-engagement call opens the same way a cold one does.

## Real-World Examples

- Rahul, an F1 OPT candidate approaching his STEM extension deadline, answers a cold call not knowing who Surely Placed is — the opener has to earn the next 90 seconds before anything else can happen.
- Priya, on H1B, gets reached through a LinkedIn message and shows up analytical and slightly skeptical — rapport here means precision, not small talk.
- Emily, a US citizen with a straightforward case, still deserves the same real qualification and goal-finding as every other candidate, not a shortcut because her situation looks simple.
- Akash, recently laid off, is reached through a job board lead already carrying financial strain — Discovery is where that context first surfaces honestly.

## Skills You'll Build

- Opening an outbound call so a candidate who wasn't expecting you decides to keep talking
- Building real rapport quickly, without performing warmth
- Qualifying a candidate honestly, without skipping people who don't fit a narrow mold
- Asking questions that surface a candidate's actual goals, not the goals you'd expect them to have
- Closing a Discovery call with a clear, specific next step

## What's Inside This Module

[Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) covers the first 20 seconds of an outbound call, cold or warm. [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport) covers earning real trust quickly. [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification) covers qualifying honestly, including candidates who don't fit an obvious profile. [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals) covers finding what a candidate actually wants, not what you'd assume. [Discovery Call Checklist](/docs/discovery/discovery-call-checklist) closes the module and confirms the call ended with a real next step.

## What Comes Next

Once a candidate has a real reason to keep talking and you understand what they actually need, the conversation moves into **Discussion** — where you diagnose their situation in detail and introduce what Surely Placed actually does. Discovery finds the pain; Discussion is where you start working with it.

# Objection Handling — Module Overview

## Purpose

An objection is rarely the whole story. This module is about the emotional resistance underneath what a candidate actually says — fear, past disappointment, a spouse's uncertainty, a bad experience with another consultancy — and about staying curious enough to find it instead of arguing with the surface-level version.

## When You'll Use This

Objections don't wait for a scheduled call. You'll use this module's skills anywhere in the funnel — a hesitant "let me think about it" in Discovery, a price concern raised mid-Discussion, or a final objection right before Closing. The framework here is designed to work at any stage, because the underlying skill — getting curious about what's actually behind a stated concern — doesn't change based on where you are in the call.

## Real-World Examples

- Akash, already having spoken with three consultancies, raises price with real skepticism — the module treats his comparison shopping as legitimate information, not a wall to push through.
- Priya asks about guarantees with an analytical edge, wanting to know exactly what can and can't be honestly promised.
- Neha needs to involve her husband given his own H1B-dependent situation — treated as a real decision-making factor, not a stalling tactic.
- Rahul, already applying on his own, gets asked about actual conversion rates rather than being talked out of an approach that might already be working.

## Skills You'll Build

- Using a consistent framework to get curious about an objection before responding to it
- Distinguishing a price objection about the number from one about timing
- Handling "I need time to think" without pressuring or writing the candidate off
- Bringing an absent decision-maker — a parent or spouse — into the process respectfully
- Responding honestly to trust, credibility, and no-guarantee concerns without overpromising

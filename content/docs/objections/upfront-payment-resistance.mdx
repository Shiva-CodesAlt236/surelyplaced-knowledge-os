# Upfront Payment Resistance

Candidate objects specifically to paying an upfront fee before job placement or outcome.

> **Policy Note:** Upfront payment is mandatory for enrollment in SurelyPlaced programs. Installments may be offered for the upfront amount where permitted. Advisors must NEVER offer unauthorized pay-after-placement, zero-upfront arrangements, Income Share Agreements (ISAs), unapproved discounts, or refund/placement guarantees.

**Why this matters:** Candidates often confuse upfront investment in dedicated placement infrastructure with risk, requiring clear explanation of value, mentorship allocation, and structured installment options.
**When to use this:** Any time a candidate states they will only pay after getting a job or resists paying before receiving placement support.
**What skill this builds:** Explaining upfront resource commitment and structuring approved installment plans without violating pricing policies.
**What happens next:** The candidate understands why upfront commitment is required and evaluates approved installment structures.

## Lesson Objective

- Explain why dedicated mentorship allocation requires upfront program commitment
- Offer approved installment options for the upfront fee where appropriate
- Maintain strict policy compliance against pay-after-placement or zero-upfront promises

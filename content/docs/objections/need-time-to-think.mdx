# Need Time to Think

"I need time to think about it" said early in a conversation and said right after a closing ask usually mean different things. This lesson covers the early, general version — for the closing-stage version, see Handling Final Hesitation.

## Lesson Objective

- Ask "what specifically" instead of assuming what's behind the request
- Tell a genuine need for reflection apart from a polite way of disengaging
- Set a specific follow-up, never an open-ended one

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't respond to early hesitation with a longer, more urgent pitch. That reads as pressure exactly when a candidate is deciding whether this conversation is worth their continued time.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Asking "is something sitting with you, or does it just feel early" let Emily answer honestly without being put on the defensive, and the answer changed what the right next move was.
</CoachingNotes>

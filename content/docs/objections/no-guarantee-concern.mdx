# No Guarantee Concern

This question deserves a direct, unhedged yes: there is no guarantee. What comes after that yes is one of the most trust-building moments in the whole funnel.

## Lesson Objective

- Confirm plainly, without hedging, that there is no placement guarantee
- Explain honestly why, grounded in what a genuine guarantee would require
- Respond to a competitor's guarantee claim without disparaging them

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't rush past this question or soften it. A direct, unhedged answer here is worth more than any amount of careful phrasing around it.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Explaining why a guarantee isn't offered — because it would require controlling things no program controls — turned an uncomfortable admission into a credibility-building explanation.
</CoachingNotes>

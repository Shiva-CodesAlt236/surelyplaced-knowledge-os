# Trust and Credibility

Skepticism in this space is earned, not irrational. Meeting it with more enthusiasm almost always backfires — calm specificity is what actually works.

## Lesson Objective

- Respond to direct skepticism without becoming defensive or over-explaining
- Ask what's actually behind the skepticism instead of assuming
- Be honest that one conversation can't fully resolve trust

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't rush to fix this in one answer. The goal of this call isn't to fully resolve Neha's caution — it's to not add to it.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Naming honestly that one call can't fully resolve trust actually built more of it than a confident reassurance would have. It matched what Neha already knew to be true.
</CoachingNotes>

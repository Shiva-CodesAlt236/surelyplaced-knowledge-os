# Already Working With a Consultancy

> **Policy note:** Whether a candidate can be enrolled with SurelyPlaced while working with another consultancy, recruiter, or program at the same time — including any exclusivity, conflict-of-interest, or overlap rule — is not documented anywhere in this repository. Never improvise an answer to a candidate's direct question about simultaneous engagement. Flag it internally and confirm with the appropriate resource. This lesson teaches the conversation itself, not the underlying policy answer.

## Lesson Objective

- Respond to this disclosure with genuine curiosity, never defensiveness
- Never criticize another consultancy, named or unnamed
- Confirm rather than guess when a direct exclusivity question comes up

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
A candidate disclosing another engagement is giving you useful information, not testing your loyalty. Treat it as exactly that.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Not commenting on the other three consultancies, even when Akash volunteered a mildly negative read on two of them, kept the conversation about SurelyPlaced's own fit rather than turning it into a pile-on.
</CoachingNotes>

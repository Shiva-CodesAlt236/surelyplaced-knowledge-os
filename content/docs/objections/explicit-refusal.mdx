# Explicit Refusal

Candidate explicitly asks to stop calling, remove number, or firmly refuses any further communication (e.g. "Please stop calling", "Don't call me again", "Remove my number", "Do not contact me").

> **Mandatory Policy:** When a candidate expresses an explicit refusal or do-not-contact demand, advisors must **IMMEDIATELY END Persuasion**. No secondary questions, no diagnostic probes, no sales pitch, and no pushback are permitted. Acknowledge professionally, wish them well, and end the call.

**Why this matters:** Respecting candidate refusal is essential for compliance, brand integrity, and professional standards. Attempting persuasion after explicit refusal damages reputation and violates sales policy.
**When to use this:** Any time a prospect explicitly asks to stop contact or reiterates firm refusal.
**What skill this builds:** Immediate professional acknowledgment, zero persuasion, clean exit execution.
**What happens next:** The conversation ends immediately. After ending the call, follow the applicable internal do-not-contact or contact-preference process.

## Lesson Objective

- Short-circuit all sales attempts immediately upon receiving explicit refusal
- Provide a clean, professional exit line with zero secondary questions or persuasion
- Maintain absolute compliance with candidate contact preferences

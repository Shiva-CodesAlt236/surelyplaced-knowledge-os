# Interview Intelligence Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist closes the Interview Intelligence module, gathering its eleven articles — overview, six interview formats, a shared framework, red flags, a coaching guide — into one working reference an advisor can run through before a candidate's interview conversation.

## Scope

This checklist verifies that an advisor is applying the module consistently. It doesn't introduce new guidance beyond what the individual articles already cover, and it doesn't define a specific interview-support program this repository leaves undocumented.

## Core Content

**Orientation and framework**
- [ ] Have I identified which of the six formats in this module the candidate's upcoming interview most resembles?
- [ ] Am I applying the three-part approach from [Interview Preparation Framework](/docs/interview-intelligence/interview-preparation-framework) — orientation, communication coaching, honest boundary-setting?

**Format-specific readiness**
- [ ] Have I stayed at the level of format and communication, without assessing the candidate's technical or business readiness?
- [ ] Have I avoided naming a specific assessment duration, structure, or process as fixed for this employer?

**Escalation and honesty**
- [ ] Have I routed any genuinely technical or business question to an internal SME rather than answering it myself?
- [ ] Have I watched for anything matching [Interview Red Flags](/docs/interview-intelligence/interview-red-flags), and escalated rather than resolved it independently?

**Coaching conversation**
- [ ] Did the pre-interview conversation feel like calm orientation rather than last-minute pressure, per [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/interview-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide)?
- [ ] Is any post-interview follow-up genuine listening, not treated as a signal about the candidate's overall prospects?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the closing-checklist pattern used across every other Sales Academy module in this repository, adapted here to this module's eleven articles and its interview-specific scope.

## Advisor Guidance

Run this checklist before any interview-prep conversation as a quick self-check, and return to the specific article behind any item that feels shaky rather than relying on this summary alone.

# Interview Preparation Framework

> This framework describes a general coaching approach, not a specific curriculum, session structure, or program this repository documents. Where a candidate needs an actual interview-support offering, follow the latest approved internal process.

## Purpose

Each format-specific article in this module — technical, behavioral, system design, coding, case study, recruiter screening — applies the same underlying approach to a different interview type. This article states that approach once, so the other six can apply it rather than each defining it independently.

## Scope

This framework covers how an advisor approaches interview preparation in general: orientation, structured communication coaching, and honest acknowledgment of what the advisor can and can't help with. It does not define a specific number of sessions, a fixed curriculum, or any program detail this repository doesn't already document elsewhere.

## Core Content

Preparation conversations work best in three parts. First, orientation: helping a candidate understand the general shape of the interview format ahead of them, reducing uncertainty about what to expect. Second, communication coaching: helping a candidate structure how they'll talk about their own real background and experience clearly, without supplying or inventing content on their behalf. Third, honest boundary-setting: telling a candidate directly when a question needs an internal technical or business SME rather than guessing at an answer to seem helpful.

This structure applies across every format this module documents, adjusted for what that specific format actually asks of a candidate. What stays constant is that an advisor never predicts an outcome, never claims authority over technical or business content outside their role, and never treats one employer's process as representative of every employer's process.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This framework extends the honesty and non-invention standards already established in [Sales Constitution](/docs/sales-constitution/things-we-never-say) and the SME-routing discipline from [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework) into the interview-preparation context specifically.

## Advisor Guidance

Work through orientation, communication coaching, and honest boundary-setting in that order, and treat every format-specific article in this module as an application of this same underlying approach rather than a separate method.

# Recruiter Screening

## Purpose

An employer's recruiter or HR screening call is usually the first conversation in their process, focused on fit, logistics, and basic qualification rather than deep technical or business content. This article covers how an advisor can help a candidate feel prepared for that specific, earlier-stage conversation.

## Scope

This article covers the general shape of a recruiter screening call as distinct from later interview rounds. It does not describe a specific employer's screening questions or process, and it does not promise that a screening call will proceed a particular way.

## Core Content

Because this call sits early in an employer's process and covers ground an advisor already understands well — background, motivation, logistics, timeline — it's often the interview stage where an advisor can offer the most direct, practical coaching. Helping a candidate practice describing their background clearly and concisely translates fairly directly from the discovery-and-discussion skills already built earlier in the Surely Placed process.

Where logistics come up that are specific to visa status or work authorization, route the candidate to the relevant guidance in Visa Playbooks rather than answering from general knowledge, since those details carry real consequences if stated inaccurately.

## Advisor Guidance

Coach clear, concise background description using skills already built during discovery and discussion, and route any visa- or work-authorization-specific question to the relevant Visa Playbook rather than answering informally.

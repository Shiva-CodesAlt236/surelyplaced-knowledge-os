# Interview Intelligence — Overview

> This module covers interview literacy and advisor coaching technique — never a specific Surely Placed interview-support curriculum, session structure, or delivery process, none of which this repository documents. Where a candidate's actual program details are needed, follow the latest approved internal process rather than anything implied here.

## Purpose

Once a candidate is enrolled and moving toward employer conversations, the advisor's job shifts again: from presenting Surely Placed to helping a candidate feel oriented and prepared for what an employer's interview process will actually involve. This module opens that work, giving an advisor a shared vocabulary for the different interview formats a candidate might encounter and a consistent approach to coaching them through it.

## Scope

This module addresses the experience of interviewing — format, structure, pacing, communication — not the technical or professional substance a given interview tests. No article here evaluates a candidate's technical skill, predicts an interview outcome, or treats a specific employer's process as fixed; hiring practices vary by employer, and this module says so explicitly wherever that variation matters.

## Core Content

Six articles document commonly encountered interview formats: [Technical Interviews](/docs/interview-intelligence/technical-interviews), [Behavioral Interviews](/docs/interview-intelligence/behavioral-interviews), [System Design Interviews](/docs/interview-intelligence/system-design-interviews), [Coding Interviews](/docs/interview-intelligence/coding-interviews), [Case Study Interviews](/docs/interview-intelligence/case-study-interviews), and [Recruiter Screening](/docs/interview-intelligence/recruiter-screening). [Interview Preparation Framework](/docs/interview-intelligence/interview-preparation-framework) gives the shared approach behind all six, [Interview Red Flags](/docs/interview-intelligence/interview-red-flags) covers what to route to an internal expert rather than handle directly, [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/interview-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide) covers the coaching conversation itself, and [Interview Intelligence Checklist](/docs/interview-intelligence/interview-intelligence-checklist) closes the module.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This module sits downstream of [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and alongside [Reference Profile: Interview Support](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/interview-support), which already establishes that this repository doesn't define a specific interview-support program — this module extends that same discipline into a fuller set of format-specific articles without introducing the program details that article deliberately leaves undefined.

## Advisor Guidance

Treat this module as a way to help a candidate feel oriented before an interview, not as license to coach them on technical or professional content the advisor isn't positioned to judge. Route any question requiring real technical or industry judgment to an internal subject matter expert, consistent with the standard already established in [Industry Playbooks](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework).

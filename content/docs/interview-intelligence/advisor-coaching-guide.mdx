# Advisor Coaching Guide

> This article is easy to confuse with the Sales Coaching module by name — they cover different things. [Sales Coaching & Case Studies](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation) covers an advisor's own sales-call skill; this article covers how that same advisor coaches a candidate through the candidate's upcoming interview.

## Purpose

The coaching conversation before an interview is where the [Interview Preparation Framework](/docs/interview-intelligence/interview-preparation-framework) actually happens in practice. This article covers how to run that conversation — before an interview, and in the follow-up afterward — as its own distinct skill.

## Scope

This article covers the advisor's own conversational technique in these coaching moments. It does not cover reviewing an advisor's sales-call performance, which belongs to the Sales Coaching module, and it does not describe a specific pre-interview program or session structure this repository doesn't define.

## Core Content

Before an interview, the goal is calm orientation, not last-minute pressure. Ask what the candidate already knows about the format, fill in general gaps using this module's format-specific articles, and be honest about anything the advisor can't answer — a candidate who trusts that honesty tends to trust the orientation that came before it too.

After an interview, a short follow-up conversation gives a candidate room to process the experience and gives the advisor useful, real information about how it actually went — treat this as genuine listening, not a data-collection exercise, and never treat a difficult interview experience as a data point about the candidate's overall prospects.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article puts [Interview Preparation Framework](/docs/interview-intelligence/interview-preparation-framework) into practice as an actual conversation, and stays within the same constructive, non-evaluative tone [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework) uses for its own, differently scoped coaching conversations.

## Advisor Guidance

Keep pre-interview conversations calm and orientation-focused, be honest about what the advisor can't answer, and treat post-interview follow-up as genuine listening rather than data collection or a signal about the candidate's overall prospects.

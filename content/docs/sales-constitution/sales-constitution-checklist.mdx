# Sales Constitution Checklist

A principle that's understood but doesn't show up in a difficult moment isn't a principle yet — it's still an idea. This checklist, and this module, exist to close that gap honestly.

## Lesson Objective

- Run this checklist as genuine, periodic self-examination, not a formality
- Confirm the nine constitutional principles are shaping real behavior, not just understood
- Recognize how five candidates' full journeys demonstrate every principle in this academy at once

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Run this checklist as genuine self-examination, on a slower cadence than any other checklist in this academy. An honest "no" on any item is more valuable than a quick, generous "yes."
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
None of these five outcomes were guaranteed, and none were forced. Each one arrived because the same constitutional standard was actually held, conversation after conversation, long enough for five very different people to reach their own genuine decisions.
</CoachingNotes>

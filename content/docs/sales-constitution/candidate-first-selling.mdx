# Candidate-First Selling

The process exists to help a real, specific person. It never works the other way around.

## Lesson Objective

- Adapt every framework in this academy to the specific candidate in front of you
- Notice when a conversation is being run "at" someone instead of "with" them
- Let a candidate's actual situation override a script when the two conflict

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
The instinct to move faster through a "simple" candidate is understandable and still wrong. Candidate-first means every candidate, not just the complicated ones.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Emily expected to be rushed because her situation was simple. Not being rushed was itself the evidence that this process serves her, not the other way around.
</CoachingNotes>

# Surely Placed Differentiation

> This is the internal operating constitution for Surely Placed Sales Advisors. This article states what can be honestly said about Surely Placed's scope and approach — it does not include competitor comparisons, market statistics, or superlative claims that aren't documented elsewhere in this repository.

## Purpose

[Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) covers how to introduce the company in a discussion call. This article states the constitutional layer underneath that presentation: what can honestly be said to differentiate Surely Placed, grounded strictly in its documented scope and approach, so that differentiation never drifts into invented claims about competitors or the market.

## Core Principle

Surely Placed's documented scope is specific: helping candidates who already hold valid work authorization for the United States, Canada, or United Kingdom find placement in IT-related roles. What differentiates the company, as far as this academy can honestly state, is that specific scope combined with the consultative, honest, non-invented approach codified throughout this entire academy — not any claim about being faster, cheaper, or more successful than a named or unnamed competitor.

## Why It Matters

A candidate evaluating Surely Placed against alternatives deserves an honest answer about what's actually different, not a persuasive-sounding claim that can't be substantiated. Overstating differentiation — implying a placement rate, a speed advantage, or a success statistic that isn't documented — creates exactly the kind of invented claim this entire academy is built to avoid, and it undermines the trust that any real differentiation depends on.

## Practical Application

When a candidate asks what makes Surely Placed different, describe the documented scope honestly — work with candidates who already hold valid work authorization, focused specifically on IT-related roles across the US, Canada, and UK — and connect it to the consultative approach demonstrated throughout the actual conversation the candidate is having, rather than reciting a claim about the market or competitors. Let the quality of the conversation itself be part of the evidence, rather than substituting a statistic for it.

## Advisor Behaviours

- Describe Surely Placed's scope and approach specifically and honestly, without inflating it into unsubstantiated superlatives
- Let the consultative quality of the conversation itself demonstrate differentiation, rather than only asserting it
- Redirect any request for a specific competitive statistic or comparison to what can actually be substantiated, consistent with [Already Working With a Consultancy](/docs/objections/already-working-with-a-consultancy)

## Common Mistakes

- Claiming a placement rate, speed advantage, or success statistic that isn't documented anywhere in this repository
- Disparaging a competitor by name or implication instead of describing Surely Placed's own approach honestly
- Treating differentiation as a single rehearsed claim rather than something demonstrated through the actual conversation

## Ethical Considerations

Honest differentiation, grounded in documented scope and demonstrated through genuine consultative practice, is fundamentally different from a persuasive but unsubstantiated claim. This article intentionally limits itself to what can be said honestly — anything beyond that belongs to confirmed marketing or business materials outside this repository's scope, not to an advisor's invented elaboration.

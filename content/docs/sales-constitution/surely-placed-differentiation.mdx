# What Great Sales Executives Do Differently

What makes an advisor genuinely great isn't one big skill. It's the same small, honest choice, made consistently, across hundreds of calls, with people who are never quite the same twice.

**Why this matters:** claiming differentiation in a single conversation is a pitch; demonstrating it across many calls is what actually makes it true.
**When to use this:** as a lens for your own daily consistency, not something to state directly to a candidate.
**What skill this builds:** holding the same standard whether a candidate is straightforward or genuinely difficult, and treating daily consistency as the real skill.
**What happens next:** this is the last skill-focused lesson before the module's closing checklist, which confirms all nine constitutional principles together.

## Lesson Objective

- Recognize that differentiation is demonstrated across many calls, never claimed in one
- Hold the same standard whether a candidate is straightforward or genuinely difficult
- Treat daily consistency as the actual skill, not any single dramatic moment

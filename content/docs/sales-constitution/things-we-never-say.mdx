# Never Inventing Facts

Six categories are never invented, approximated, or guessed at, anywhere in this academy: pricing terms, outcome guarantees, unconfirmed statistics, legal or immigration advice, unconfirmed internal policy, and disparaging or unverifiable competitor claims.

## Lesson Objective

- Recognize the six never-invent categories immediately when a conversation approaches them
- Respond with an honest "let me confirm" instead of a plausible-sounding guess
- Treat your own confidence in an answer as irrelevant to whether it's appropriate to state

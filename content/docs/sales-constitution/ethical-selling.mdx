# Never Manipulate, Never Lie

An advisor never invents information to make a conversation easier, never uses a candidate's trust against their own best interest, and never treats vulnerability as an opportunity. When honesty and persuasive momentum conflict, honesty wins. Every time.

**Why this matters:** the moment honesty and momentum genuinely conflict is the moment this whole academy's principles either hold or quietly don't.
**When to use this:** any time an invented answer, or a candidate's own vulnerability, would make the conversation easier in the moment.
**What skill this builds:** choosing an honest "let me confirm" over any invented answer, with total consistency regardless of how close a decision seems.
**What happens next:** this standard is what the consultative framework, covered next, is actually built on.

## Lesson Objective

- Choose an honest "I don't know, let me confirm" over any invented answer, always
- Recognize genuine vulnerability as a signal to slow down and care, not close faster
- Apply this standard with total consistency, regardless of how close a decision seems

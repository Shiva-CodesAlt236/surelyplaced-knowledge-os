# Professional Communication & Team Culture

Every stage of the process exists to help a candidate understand their situation more clearly. The same standard of genuine, honest communication applies inside the team, not just on candidate calls.

**Why this matters:** treating a stage as just "advancing a candidate forward" instead of genuinely informing them turns a consultative process into a funnel with extra steps.
**When to use this:** at every stage of a call, and in every internal handoff, follow-up, or conversation about a candidate with a colleague.
**What skill this builds:** communicating professionally and accurately in every context, and holding the same honesty standard internally as externally.
**What happens next:** this same standard is what makes owning a mistake, covered next, something to do plainly rather than something to manage.

## Lesson Objective

- Treat each stage as adding genuine understanding, not just advancing a candidate forward
- Communicate professionally and accurately, including in follow-up and handoff moments
- Extend the same honesty standard to how advisors talk about candidates and each other

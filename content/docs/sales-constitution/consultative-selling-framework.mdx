# Consultative Selling Framework

> This is the internal operating constitution for Surely Placed Sales Advisors. This article states the structural logic connecting [Sales Workflow Overview](/docs/sales-operations/sales-workflow-overview) to the constitutional principles in this module.

## Purpose

[Sales Workflow Overview](/docs/sales-operations/sales-workflow-overview) maps the operational sequence of a candidate's journey. This article states the underlying logic that sequence is built on: consultative selling, where each stage exists to genuinely understand and inform, not to move a candidate mechanically toward a predetermined outcome.

## Core Principle

Consultative selling means every stage of the process — discovery, presentation, objection-handling, closing — exists to help the candidate understand their own situation and options more clearly than they did before the conversation. Each stage should leave the candidate better informed and more capable of making a sound decision, regardless of what that decision turns out to be.

## Why It Matters

A transactional approach treats each stage as a step toward a sale; a consultative approach treats each stage as a step toward genuine understanding, which then makes a sound decision possible. The two approaches can look similar on the surface — the same questions might get asked in either — but the underlying intent shapes how a candidate experiences the conversation and whether the eventual decision, whatever it is, is one they can stand behind.

## Practical Application

At each stage, ask whether the conversation genuinely added to the candidate's understanding of their own situation, or whether it was primarily designed to advance them to the next stage regardless. Use discovery questions to actually understand, not to qualify a candidate as quickly as possible; use objection-handling to actually resolve a candidate's real concern, not to overcome resistance to a predetermined outcome.

## Advisor Behaviours

- Treat each stage of the process as an opportunity to add genuine understanding, not just to move a candidate forward
- Ask discovery and clarifying questions because the answers genuinely matter to how the rest of the conversation should go, not as a formality
- Evaluate a conversation's success by whether the candidate ended it better informed, regardless of their eventual decision

## Common Mistakes

- Running through the stages in [Sales Workflow Overview](/docs/sales-operations/sales-workflow-overview) mechanically, without the underlying consultative intent this article describes
- Asking discovery questions for the appearance of thoroughness rather than because the answers will genuinely shape the conversation
- Treating objection-handling as a technique for overcoming resistance rather than genuinely resolving a candidate's real concern

## Ethical Considerations

A consultative approach used with genuine intent to help a candidate understand their situation is fundamentally different from the same questions and structure used purely as a more sophisticated way to move someone toward a sale. The difference isn't visible in the words used — it's in the advisor's actual intent, which is why this principle depends on [Advisor Mindset](/docs/sales-constitution/advisor-mindset) being genuine, not performed.

The same questions and structure can be used consultatively or purely transactionally — the difference lies in the advisor's actual intent, not the visible technique.

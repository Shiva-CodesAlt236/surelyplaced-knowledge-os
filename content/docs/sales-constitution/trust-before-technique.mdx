# Respect, Trust & Long-Term Reputation

Trust isn't one technique among many. It's the precondition every technique in this academy depends on, and it doesn't end when the sale does.

## Lesson Objective

- Treat every interaction, before and after enrollment, as building or eroding trust
- Recognize that trust built in one conversation doesn't transfer automatically
- Understand that reputation outlasts any individual sale

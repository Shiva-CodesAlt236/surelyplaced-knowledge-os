# Sales Constitution — Module Overview

## Purpose

Every prior module taught a skill. This one is different: it's about identity — who you are as an advisor when no technique is telling you what to say. The Sales Constitution is the set of honest principles that hold whether a call is easy or difficult, whether the candidate is warm or resistant, and whether the outcome is a yes or a genuine not-now.

## When You'll Use This

This module isn't tied to a stage of the call — it's the foundation underneath every stage. You'll draw on it any time a technique alone isn't enough: when you're not certain what's honest to say, when a candidate catches you in an error, when a colleague treats a "difficult" candidate differently than an easy one, or any moment where doing the right thing and closing the call don't obviously point the same direction.

## Real-World Examples

- Advisor Mindset shows matching a candidate's own pace instead of introducing urgency they never asked for.
- Ethical Selling shows committing, before anything goes wrong, to name uncertainty rather than bluff through it.
- Things We Never Say shows declining to cite a statistic that can't honestly be stood behind, even when it would be persuasive.
- Trust Before Technique shows offering a new contact — a candidate's spouse, a referral — the same honest conversation, not a lesser one, because they're not the one already convinced.

## Skills You'll Build

- Naming what Surely Placed actually believes about selling, plainly and specifically
- Holding an honest advisor mindset regardless of how easy or resistant a candidate is
- Practicing candidate-first selling as a real behavior, not a slogan
- Applying a consultative framework and sound decision-making principles under pressure
- Recognizing exactly what should never be said, and why

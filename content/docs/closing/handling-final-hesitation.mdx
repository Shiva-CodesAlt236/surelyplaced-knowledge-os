# Handling Final Hesitation

Not every closing call ends in a clean yes on the first ask — and that's not a sign anything went wrong.

## Lesson Objective

- Ask "what specifically" instead of "why" to surface what's actually behind hesitation
- Tell resolvable-in-the-call hesitation apart from a genuine need for time
- Set a specific follow-up date, never an open-ended one

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't keep probing once Akash has clearly said, more than once, that this is genuinely about time, not a specific unresolved question. Continuing to dig at that point starts to feel like pressure.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
"What would be a reasonable amount of time for you" hands the pacing decision back to Akash instead of Aayush guessing at it. A specific week, confirmed together, is a real commitment — "whenever you're ready" isn't.
</CoachingNotes>

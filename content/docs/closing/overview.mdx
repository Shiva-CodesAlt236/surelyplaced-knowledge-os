# Closing Calls — Module Overview

## Purpose

Discussion diagnosed the situation and covered the investment. Closing is where a candidate actually decides — and where the whole academy's honesty principle gets tested hardest. This module is about commitment: confirming it's real, asking for it plainly, and treating both a yes and a genuine "not now" as complete, honest outcomes.

## When You'll Use This

You'll use this module once Discussion is genuinely finished — the candidate has heard the recommendation, understands the process, and knows roughly what it costs. Closing is where you find out what they actually decided, ask directly if they haven't said, and guide the call into an honest payment and agreement process once the answer is yes.

## Real-World Examples

- Rahul confirms clean readiness after reviewing the cost breakdown from Discussion — the ask here is a single plain sentence, not another round of selling.
- Priya answers the direct ask after a real pause — the silence afterward does real work, and Aayush lets it.
- Neha says yes once her husband is on board, and the call shifts honestly into what Aayush knows for certain versus what needs to come from elsewhere.
- Akash, after sitting with his numbers, gives a genuine "not now" — closed with the same care and a specific two-month follow-up, not treated as a loss.

## Skills You'll Build

- Confirming readiness honestly with an open-ended question, not a closed yes/no
- Making a single, clear ask and holding the silence that follows it
- Reading whether hesitation is resolvable in the call or a genuine need for time
- Handling the payment and agreement stage honestly, without guessing at details you don't know
- Closing every outcome, yes or not now, with the same warmth and specificity

# Closing Call Checklist

A well-closed "not now" is just as much a successful call as a well-closed "yes." This lesson is about closing every outcome with the same care.

## Lesson Objective

- Confirm the ask was clear and the silence after it was held
- Set a specific follow-up for any outcome that isn't a clean yes
- End every call warmly, regardless of the outcome

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't let your own disappointment leak into your tone here. Akash needs to hear that this outcome is genuinely fine, not tolerated.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Naming that "not now" is the right call, given his situation, is what actually kept the relationship intact. A candidate who feels pushed toward a decision they know isn't right for them doesn't come back later — one who feels respected often does.
</CoachingNotes>

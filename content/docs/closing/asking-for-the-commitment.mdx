# Asking for the Commitment

One sentence. Then stop talking. This is the moment a lot of otherwise well-run calls get soft.

## Lesson Objective

- Ask in one clear sentence — never buried, never hedged
- Hold the silence after the ask instead of filling it
- Respond to whatever comes back without treating it as a problem to fix

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Match your tone to the moment — calm and direct, not high-energy. Urgency in your voice can read as pressure even when your words are completely neutral.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
The pause did real work. Priya needed a few seconds to actually decide, not to be rescued from deciding. Filling that silence would have taken the decision out of her hands.
</CoachingNotes>

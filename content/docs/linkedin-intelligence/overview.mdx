# LinkedIn Intelligence — Overview

> This module covers how a candidate's LinkedIn presence communicates and how an advisor coaches around it — never a specific Surely Placed profile-writing service, guaranteed visibility outcome, or LinkedIn algorithm mechanic this repository doesn't document. Where a candidate needs something beyond general coaching, follow the latest approved internal process rather than anything implied here.

## Purpose

LinkedIn is usually where a recruiter or hiring manager forms a first impression before any conversation happens, and it stays relevant well past that first look — a candidate keeps refining it through active outreach, interview season, and beyond. This module gives an advisor a consistent way to look at a candidate's LinkedIn presence and talk through it constructively, without overstating what any advisor can actually know about how the platform behaves behind the scenes.

## Scope

This module addresses how a LinkedIn profile and its surrounding activity present a candidate — clarity, completeness, tone, and general visibility principles. It does not claim insider knowledge of LinkedIn's search or ranking mechanics, does not promise a recruiter will reach out, and does not treat any single tactic as certain to work; platform behavior isn't something this repository can verify, and every article says so wherever it matters.

## Advisor Guidance

Treat this module as a way to help a candidate present their real background clearly and consistently across a platform recruiters actually use, not as a way to promise a specific result the advisor can't control. Route any question about a paid LinkedIn product tier, a specific platform feature, or a claim about how the algorithm works to the latest approved internal process rather than answering from assumption.

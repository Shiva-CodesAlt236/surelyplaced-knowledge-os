# LinkedIn Intelligence Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist gathers the nine articles preceding it — the profile analysis approach, headline through content-strategy guidance, red flags, and the coaching conversation — into a single working reference for a LinkedIn-focused conversation with a candidate.

## Scope

Treat this as a final pass before or during that conversation, not a new source of guidance — each item here points back to the article that actually covers it in depth. Nothing below stands in for a specific profile-writing or optimization service, which this repository doesn't define.

## Core Content

**Whole-profile review**
- [ ] Have I worked through all three passes in [LinkedIn Profile Analysis](/docs/linkedin-intelligence/linkedin-profile-analysis) — coherence, completeness, and consistency?
- [ ] Have I checked the profile against the candidate's resume and prior conversations for material mismatches?

**Section-specific guidance**
- [ ] Does the headline state role and focus clearly, without relying on a single required formula?
- [ ] Does the About section open with direction rather than a chronological recap, in the candidate's own voice?
- [ ] Have I kept any visibility discussion honest about the real limits of what's knowable, without promising a specific outcome?

**Ongoing presence**
- [ ] Have I discussed networking and content options without pressuring the candidate toward either if it doesn't suit them?
- [ ] Have I coached toward genuine, specific outreach rather than generic, high-volume tactics?

**Red flags and coaching conversation**
- [ ] Have I watched for anything in [LinkedIn Red Flags](/docs/linkedin-intelligence/linkedin-red-flags), routing rather than resolving anything touching authorization or authenticity?
- [ ] Did the coaching conversation itself follow [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/linkedin-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide) — asking first, prioritizing impact, and confirming genuine buy-in at the close?

## Cross-Module Alignment

## Advisor Guidance

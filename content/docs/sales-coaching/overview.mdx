# Sales Coaching & Case Studies — Module Overview

## Purpose

Every earlier module taught a specific skill in isolation. This module is about reading a full conversation the way a coach does — noticing what actually worked, what didn't, and why — and building the habit of honest self-review so your own performance keeps improving after the academy ends.

## When You'll Use This

You'll use this module's skills after calls, not during them: reviewing your own conversations, reading composite case studies drawn from real situations, and learning to give yourself the same honest, specific feedback a good coach would give you. It's cross-stage because a full call touches every skill from Discovery through Closing at once.

## Real-World Examples

- A composite case study follows an OPT software engineer candidate through a full call, showing how discovery, rapport, and objection handling combine in real time, not as separate techniques.
- A supply chain case study and a healthcare case study each show the same underlying skills applied to very different candidate situations.
- A price negotiation case study and a consultancy comparison case study show curiosity and honesty holding up under direct pressure.
- The call self-review checklist walks through pausing after your own calls to name, specifically, what worked and what you'd do differently.

## Skills You'll Build

- Reading a full sales conversation and identifying what specifically worked
- Recognizing common sales mistakes before they become habits
- Reviewing your own calls honestly, without either self-criticism or excuse-making
- Applying every prior module's skills together, naturally, instead of as separate techniques
- Coaching yourself the way a good manager would coach you

## What's Inside This Module

[Reading a Sales Conversation](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation) teaches the core skill of analyzing a call. Composite case studies — [OPT Software Engineer](/docs/sales-coaching/composite-case-study-opt-software-engineer), [Supply Chain](/docs/sales-coaching/composite-case-study-supply-chain), and [Healthcare](/docs/sales-coaching/composite-case-study-healthcare) — apply that skill to full, realistic conversations. [Price Negotiation Case Study](/docs/sales-coaching/price-negotiation-case-study) and [Consultancy Comparison Case Study](/docs/sales-coaching/consultancy-comparison-case-study) focus on specific high-pressure moments. [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework) and [Common Sales Mistakes](/docs/sales-coaching/common-sales-mistakes) build the reviewer's mindset. [Call Self-Review Checklist](/docs/sales-coaching/call-self-review-checklist) and [Sales Coaching Checklist](/docs/sales-coaching/sales-coaching-checklist) close the module, leading into the [Complete Sales Call Walkthrough](/docs/sales-coaching/complete-sales-call-walkthrough) capstone.

## What Comes Next

Skill and technique only go so far without a foundation underneath them. **Sales Constitution**, the academy's final module, steps back from technique entirely to address identity — who you are as an advisor, and the honest principles that hold up whether or not any given technique is working in the moment.

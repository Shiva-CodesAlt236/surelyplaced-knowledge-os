# Complete Sales Call Walkthrough

## Start Here

**When do I use this?** Read this after you've been through Discovery, Discussion, Closing, Objection Handling, and Pricing at least once. This isn't where you learn any single skill — it's where you see all of them happen in one place, in order, the way they actually do on a real call.

**What will this help me achieve?** A felt sense of the whole shape of a call — how Opening leads into Rapport, how a goal surfaces in Discovery and gets tested again at Closing, how one honest choice in minute four is still paying off in minute forty.

**When should I move on?** There's nowhere to "move on" to from here — this is the capstone. Come back to this page any time the pieces feel disconnected, and let it remind you they're one conversation, not eleven separate techniques.

This is Rahul's call, start to finish. Not a summary of earlier lessons — the actual, complete thing, in real time, with every phase labeled as it happens.

## Lesson Objective

- See every stage of the sales process happen in one continuous, realistic call
- Notice how an early honest choice (in Opening) is still shaping the outcome much later (at Agreement)
- Walk away with a felt sense of the whole shape of a call, not just its individual parts

</CoachingNotes>

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Read this call again and count the phases where Aayush could have taken a shortcut — skipped the qualification pause, guessed at the price objection, called Rahul on day two instead of day four, recited the agreement terms from memory. Every one of those shortcuts was available. None of them got taken.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
No single moment closed this sale. The cold open on minute one and the honest "let me confirm" on minute thirty-eight are the same choice, made eleven times in a row, in eleven different situations.
</CoachingNotes>

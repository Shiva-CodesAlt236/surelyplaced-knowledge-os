# Sales Coaching Checklist

Selling better isn't one big skill. It's a handful of small, quiet habits, practiced consistently until they stop feeling like effort.

## Lesson Objective

- Confirm the core coaching skills from this module are actually being applied, not just understood
- Know which specific lesson to revisit when a particular skill feels shaky
- Reinforce that these are habits to build, not facts to memorize

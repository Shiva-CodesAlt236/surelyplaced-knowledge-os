# Sales Coaching Checklist

> This is a sales coaching module, not marketing or testimonial content. Every case study referenced below is a Composite Case Study — fictional, built by combining existing modules — never a real candidate or real conversation.

## Case Study Type

Not applicable — this is a module-summary checklist, not a case study.

## Purpose

The ten articles in this module cover a reading framework, five composite case studies, and four coaching and review tools. This checklist gathers them into a single working reference, so an advisor or coach can confirm the module's core skills are being applied without reopening every article individually.

## Learning Objectives

- Confirm familiarity with the four-layer reading framework and its application across all five case studies
- Use this checklist as a periodic self-audit of coaching-related habits, distinct from operational or pricing-specific checklists
- Identify which specific article to revisit when a particular skill needs reinforcement
- Reinforce that every case study in this module is explicitly composite and fictional

## Conversation Walkthrough

Reading framework
- [ ] I can apply the four-layer read — literal content, underlying signal, advisor response, resulting shift — to any conversation (see [Reading a Sales Conversation](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation))

Composite case studies
- [ ] I understand how visa-timeline and industry-specific pressures combine in a single candidate (see [Composite Case Study: OPT Software Engineer](/docs/sales-coaching/composite-case-study-opt-software-engineer))
- [ ] I understand how a process-oriented candidate evaluates both career and immigration questions the same way (see [Composite Case Study: Supply Chain](/docs/sales-coaching/composite-case-study-supply-chain))
- [ ] I recognize how a subtle tone shift, without new information, can signal an underlying trust concern (see [Composite Case Study: Healthcare](/docs/sales-coaching/composite-case-study-healthcare))
- [ ] I can hold the discount-guidelines discipline consistently across repeated, escalating pressure (see [Price Negotiation Case Study](/docs/sales-coaching/price-negotiation-case-study))
- [ ] I can respond to comparison invitations honestly, without disparaging a competitor (see [Consultancy Comparison Case Study](/docs/sales-coaching/consultancy-comparison-case-study))

Review and reflection tools
- [ ] I know how to give or receive specific, moment-based feedback rather than a vague overall impression (see [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework))
- [ ] I recognize the recurring mistake patterns that show up across multiple modules (see [Common Sales Mistakes](/docs/sales-coaching/common-sales-mistakes))
- [ ] I run a brief, honest self-review after calls, not just the difficult ones (see [Call Self-Review Checklist](/docs/sales-coaching/call-self-review-checklist))

# Call Self-Review Checklist

Run this immediately after any call, while the details are still fresh — not just after the difficult ones.

**Why this matters:** a skill gap noticed days later is much harder to name specifically than one caught while the call is still fresh.
**When to use this:** immediately after every call, not only the ones that felt difficult or went poorly.
**What skill this builds:** building the habit of brief, honest self-review, avoiding both excessive self-criticism and reflexive self-approval.
**What happens next:** this personal review habit feeds into the module's closing checklist, which reviews the coaching skills as a complete set.

## Lesson Objective

- Build the habit of a brief, honest self-review after every call
- Catch a skill gap while it's still fresh enough to name specifically
- Avoid both excessive self-criticism and reflexive self-approval

# Tone & Call Energy

The same words, said flat or said warm, land completely differently. Energy isn't about being loud — it's about being present.

**Why this matters:** flat energy can undercut an otherwise well-chosen response, and a candidate reacts to how something is said as much as what's said.
**When to use this:** throughout every call, and especially any time your own energy risks dragging a conversation down rather than matching it.
**What skill this builds:** matching a candidate's energy without imposing your own mood, and keeping tone steady through both good and difficult moments.
**What happens next:** steady tone becomes the foundation the next lesson's trust-building moments are built on.

## Lesson Objective

- Match your energy to the candidate's, rather than imposing your own mood on the call
- Recognize when low energy on your end is dragging the conversation down
- Keep tone steady through both good and difficult moments

**Better — energy is present but generic:**

  appearance="phone"

    { sender: "candidate", text: "Hi! I'm really excited to learn more about this, I've heard great things." },
    { sender: "rep", text: "That's great to hear! Let's dive in.", annotation: "Better: matches energy, but the enthusiasm is generic, not specific to her" },
  ]}
/>

**Excellent — energy is matched and made specific:**

<ConversationViewer
  repName="Aayush"
  candidateName="Emily"
  appearance="phone"
  candidateMood="positive"
  messages={[
    { sender: "candidate", text: "Hi! I'm really excited to learn more about this, I've heard great things." },
    { sender: "rep", text: "Love that energy — what specifically did you hear that got you excited? I want to make sure we actually cover that.", annotation: "Excellent: matches her energy and turns it into a specific, useful question" },
  ]}
/>

<DecisionPoint
  context="Same opening from Emily, three different responses. Energy alone isn't enough — it has to connect back to something specific."
  prompt="What made the excellent response better than the better one?"
  bestAnswer="It matched her energy and immediately turned it into a real, specific question, rather than just mirroring enthusiasm without doing anything useful with it."
/>

<ManagerTip from="Aayush" priority="important">
What should have happened, and how to improve: check your own energy before every call, not just your script. A flat tone communicates disinterest even when your words are fine.
</ManagerTip>

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Matching Emily's energy and immediately using it to ask something specific kept the momentum she brought to the call, instead of letting a flat response drain it right at the start.
</CoachingNotes>

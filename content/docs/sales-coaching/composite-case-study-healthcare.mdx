# Building Trust

Trust isn't built with one good answer. It's built in small, specific moments, most of which have nothing to do with reassurance.

## Lesson Objective

- Notice subtle tone shifts that signal a trust concern, even without new information
- Build trust through specific honesty, not general reassurance
- Recognize that trust accumulates across a call, not in a single moment

<DecisionPoint

<ManagerTip from="Aayush" priority="critical">
What should have happened, and how to improve: a tone shift without new information is still information. Say what you noticed, gently, rather than just responding more carefully underneath it.
</ManagerTip>

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Naming the shift directly gave Neha an opening to say what was actually underneath it, rather than leaving an unspoken guardedness to quietly shape the rest of the call.
</CoachingNotes>

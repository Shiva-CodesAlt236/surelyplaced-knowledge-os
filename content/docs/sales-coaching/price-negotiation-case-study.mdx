# Confidence & Emotional Control

Confidence isn't never feeling pressure. It's staying steady while you feel it, instead of caving or getting defensive.

**Why this matters:** caving under repeated pressure, or turning defensive against it, both cost more trust than simply staying steady would have.
**When to use this:** any time pressure repeats or escalates within a single call — a price pushed on twice, an objection raised a third time.
**What skill this builds:** holding a position consistently under escalating pressure while staying warm, and noticing your own emotional reaction before it leaks into tone.
**What happens next:** this steadiness under pressure is the same skill the module's quieter techniques — mirroring, pausing, silence — are built to support.

## Lesson Objective

- Hold a position consistently across repeated, escalating pressure
- Stay warm while declining to cave, without turning cold or rigid
- Notice your own emotional reaction before it leaks into your tone

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Naming the frustration directly — "I know it's frustrating to hear the same answer" — let Aayush hold the identical position without sounding cold, which is the actual skill here, not just repeating himself.
</CoachingNotes>

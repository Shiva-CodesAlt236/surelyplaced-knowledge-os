## Conversation Walkthrough

The following patterns recur across multiple modules in this repository:

**Responding to words instead of the signal underneath them.** Documented independently in the Objections, Pricing, and Sales Coaching modules — an advisor answers the literal question asked rather than the underlying concern actually driving it. See [Handling Price Objections](/docs/pricing/handling-price-objections) and [Reading a Sales Conversation](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation).

**Filling an honest gap with a guess.** Documented in Pricing and Sales Operations — improvising a discount, a policy detail, or a CRM specific rather than confirming it through the correct channel. See [Discount Guidelines](/docs/pricing/discount-guidelines) and [CRM Workflow](/docs/sales-operations/crm-workflow).

**Letting urgency override honest evaluation.** Documented in the Visa Playbooks and Closing modules — treating a visa timeline or a candidate's eagerness as a reason to rush past a real, unresolved concern. See [Visa Discovery Framework](/docs/visa-playbooks/visa-discovery-framework) and [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation).

**Treating a rehearsed response as a substitute for diagnosis.** Documented in Objections and Industry Playbooks — applying the same script to every version of an objection or concern rather than identifying which specific variant is actually present. See [Objection Handling Framework](/docs/objections/objection-handling-framework).

**Letting records lag behind reality.** Documented in Sales Operations — notes, stages, or follow-up commitments that fall out of date, making the next conversation harder than it needs to be. See [Candidate Notes Standards](/docs/sales-operations/candidate-notes-standards).

## Decision Analysis

Each of these patterns shares a common root: substituting something easier — a guess, a script, a literal answer — for the harder, more honest work of genuinely diagnosing what's actually happening in the moment. Recognizing the pattern across modules makes it easier to catch in the moment, regardless of which specific stage of the process it shows up in.

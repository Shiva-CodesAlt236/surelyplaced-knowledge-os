# Sales Workflow Overview

> This is a sales operations playbook, not an employee handbook or HR manual. It describes the shape of the sales process as reflected in this repository's existing modules. Where a specific operational step, tool, or system isn't yet documented here, this article marks it **Operational Policy Pending** rather than inventing one — advisors should follow the latest approved internal process for anything marked that way.

## Purpose

Discovery, Discussion, Closing, Objections, Visa Playbooks, Industry Playbooks, and Pricing each cover a piece of the advisor's job in depth. This article is the map that connects them — a single, ordered view of how a candidate moves through the sales process, and where each existing module fits along that path. It exists so a newer advisor can see the whole shape of the job before going deep into any one part of it.

## Learning Objectives

- Describe the sales process as a sequence of stages, from first contact to enrollment
- Locate each existing module (Discovery, Discussion, Closing, Objections, Pricing) within that sequence
- Understand what "Operational Policy Pending" means and when it applies
- Recognize this article as a map, not a replacement for the detailed modules it references

## When to Use

- When onboarding a new advisor, as the first article read before any individual module
- When an advisor wants to understand how a specific conversation skill fits into the larger process
- When training materials need a single reference point for "how the whole thing works"

## Operational Context

The repository's existing modules describe the sales process in this order: a discovery call establishes rapport and qualifies the candidate (see [Discovery](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call)), a discussion call presents Surely Placed and the investment involved (see [Discussion](/docs/discussion/structuring-the-discussion-call)), objections are addressed as they arise at any stage (see [Objections](/docs/objections/objection-handling-framework)), and a closing call moves a ready candidate to a decision and agreement (see [Closing](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment)). Visa Playbooks and Industry Playbooks provide candidate-specific context that applies throughout, and Pricing provides the psychology and delivery skill behind every cost-related moment in that sequence.

This module — Sales Operations — sits alongside all of them, covering the operational discipline (tracking, documentation, follow-up, escalation) that supports the conversations themselves, rather than the conversations.

## Recommended Process

Move through the sequence in the order candidates naturally experience it: discovery, discussion, objection-handling as needed, closing. At each stage, the operational habits in this module — accurate CRM records (see [CRM Workflow](/docs/sales-operations/crm-workflow)), clear stage tracking (see [Lead Stage Management](/docs/sales-operations/lead-stage-management)), and consistent follow-up (see [Follow-Up Framework](/docs/sales-operations/follow-up-framework)) — run underneath the conversation itself, not instead of it.

Where this repository does not yet define a specific system, tool, or approval step, treat it as **Operational Policy Pending** and confirm the current process internally rather than assuming or improvising one.

## Advisor Responsibilities

- Understand where a given conversation sits in the overall sequence before starting it
- Apply the correct module's guidance for that stage rather than mixing frameworks
- Keep operational records (notes, stage status, follow-up) current as the candidate moves through the process
- Flag any step in this article that seems out of date or inconsistent with actual current practice, rather than following it blindly

## Quality Standards

A well-run candidate journey should be traceable end to end — anyone reviewing the record should be able to see what stage the candidate is in, what's already been discussed, and what's next, without needing to ask the advisor directly. This overview exists to keep that consistency across every advisor's work, not just each individual advisor's own process.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating this overview as a substitute for the detailed modules it references, rather than a map pointing to them
- Skipping stages or blending discovery and closing behavior in ways that lose the intent of either
- Assuming an operational detail not covered here is safe to invent, rather than confirming it internally

## Escalation Guidance

If any part of the sequence described here conflicts with actual current practice, or if a step in the process isn't covered anywhere in this repository, treat it as **Operational Policy Pending** and raise it with a manager or the appropriate internal process owner rather than resolving it independently.

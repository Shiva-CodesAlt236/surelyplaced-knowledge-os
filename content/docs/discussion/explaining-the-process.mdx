# Explaining the Process

> **Note on specifics:** The stage names in this lesson's examples are illustrative, not the current official program steps. Always confirm exact current stages, sequencing, and requirements against the authoritative internal resource before stating them to a candidate as fact. Never state a specific step, timeline, or requirement from memory if you're not certain it's current.

"Okay, so what would actually happen" is the natural next question once a recommendation lands. Answer it clearly, honestly, and with what's expected of the candidate — not just what they receive.

## Lesson Objective

- Explain the process so a candidate could repeat it back accurately
- State plainly what's expected of the candidate, not just what's provided
- Recognize when a process question is really a disguised question about timeline or outcome

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't rush past "none of this matters anyway" — that's the real sentence in this whole conversation. Let it land before moving to solutions.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Refusing to give a made-up number, then asking what's underneath the question, surfaced the real constraint (the husband's H1B renewal) instead of Aayush guessing at anxiety he couldn't see.
</CoachingNotes>

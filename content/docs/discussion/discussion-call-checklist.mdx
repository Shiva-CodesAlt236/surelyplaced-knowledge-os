# Discussion Call Checklist

By the end of a discussion call, the candidate has heard a real recommendation, a real process, and possibly real money. This close carries more weight than discovery's did.

## Lesson Objective

- Confirm the four-stage structure genuinely held before the call ends
- Get the candidate to restate next steps in their own words
- Name any unresolved hesitation directly rather than letting the call end on an assumption

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Ask "what's your understanding of what happens next" and actually wait for the full answer — don't fill the pause by summarizing it yourself first.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Confirming the recap in Rahul's own words (not Aayush's own summary) is the more reliable check — candidates will tell you what's actually landed if you ask plainly, rather than nodding along to your own recap.
</CoachingNotes>

# Discussion Calls — Module Overview

## Purpose

Discovery found the pain. Discussion is where you diagnose it properly — going deeper into a candidate's actual situation, presenting Surely Placed as a specific answer to what you've learned, and having the investment conversation honestly, before anyone is asked to decide anything.

## When You'll Use This

You'll use this module on the call that follows a productive Discovery conversation — once a candidate has agreed to talk further and you have a real sense of what they need. Discussion is a longer, more detailed conversation than Discovery: it's where you explain the process in full, connect it to the candidate's specific situation, and introduce cost as one honest fact among several, not a number to be avoided or rushed past.

## Real-World Examples

- Rahul's Discussion call recaps specific details from Discovery — his STEM extension timeline — to prove the earlier conversation was actually heard, not just logged.
- Priya, who wanted the price early, gets a Discussion call structured around her analytical style: process first, investment discussed plainly once the value is clear.
- Neha, on H4 EAD, raises that her husband's H1B situation affects the decision — Discussion is where that's treated as a legitimate factor, not an objection to route around.
- Akash's Discussion call surfaces the real financial strain behind his job loss, setting up the honest follow-up that happens later in Closing.

## Skills You'll Build

- Structuring a longer call so it builds logically instead of feeling like a pitch
- Presenting Surely Placed as a specific answer to a candidate's stated situation
- Explaining the process clearly enough that a candidate could repeat it back accurately
- Discussing investment honestly, without avoiding the number or over-justifying it
- Closing a Discussion call with genuine next steps, not vague good intentions

## What Comes Next

Once a candidate understands the process, the recommendation, and the investment, and genuinely has no open questions, the conversation moves into **Closing** — where you confirm real readiness and ask for the commitment directly. Discussion diagnoses the situation; Closing is where the candidate actually decides.

# Structuring the Discussion Call

Discovery gathers the picture. Discussion hands it back as an actual recommendation. Get the order wrong and even a good recommendation falls flat.

## Lesson Objective

- Run a discussion call in four stages: recap, recommendation, process, investment — in that order
- Open by proving discovery was heard, not by starting fresh
- Hold a cost question at bay honestly when it comes too early

<ManagerTip from="Aayush" priority="important">
Don't answer "what do you guys do" with a brochure line. If your first sentence back could be said to literally any candidate on any call, you've already lost the thread you just built with the recap. Anchor the answer in the exact two words the candidate just gave you.
</ManagerTip>

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Keep the recap to 20–30 seconds with one or two precise details — not a long summary. Precision proves you listened; length just proves you took notes. Let Rahul correct you before you build anything on top of the recap.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
The recap named a fact only Rahul could have told him (STEM OPT status), which made "did I get that right" land as real, not procedural. Confirming before recommending meant the whole rest of the call is now built on agreed ground, not an assumption.
</CoachingNotes>

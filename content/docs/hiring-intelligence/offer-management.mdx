# Offer Management

> This article covers how an advisor helps a candidate think clearly about a received offer. It does not provide legal, tax, immigration, or compensation-negotiation advice — those questions route to an appropriate qualified professional or, for authorization-specific questions, to [Visa Discovery Framework](/docs/visa-playbooks/visa-discovery-framework).

## Purpose

Receiving an offer can bring its own kind of stress — excitement mixed with pressure to respond quickly and uncertainty about what's actually reasonable to ask for or clarify. This article covers how an advisor helps a candidate approach that moment clearly rather than reactively.

## Scope

This article covers general orientation and clarifying-question practice. It does not state what compensation is reasonable to expect or request, does not draft or review contract language, and does not tell a candidate whether to accept a specific offer — that decision belongs entirely to the candidate.

## Core Content

Encourage a candidate to read an offer's full written terms carefully rather than relying only on what was said verbally, and to identify anything unclear before responding — start date, reporting structure, and any condition the offer is contingent on are common places where a clarifying question is genuinely useful.

Most employers expect a candidate to take some reasonable time to review an offer rather than respond within minutes; a candidate feeling rushed should feel able to ask for a specific, reasonable amount of time to consider it. This article does not state what amount of time is typical, since that varies by employer and situation.

Where a candidate has specific questions about compensation structure, negotiation, tax treatment, or contract terms, the honest and appropriate response is to direct them to a qualified professional or the latest approved internal process rather than the advisor answering from general knowledge — these are exactly the areas where an inaccurate answer carries real consequences.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article picks up directly where [Offer Decision Process](/docs/hiring-intelligence/offer-decision-process) leaves off, and defers to [Visa Discovery Framework](/docs/visa-playbooks/visa-discovery-framework) for any authorization-specific term an offer might reference.

## Advisor Guidance

Help a candidate slow down, read carefully, and ask reasonable clarifying questions. Route any legal, tax, or compensation-negotiation question to an appropriate qualified professional rather than answering it directly, and never tell a candidate whether to accept a specific offer.

Help a candidate read a received offer carefully, ask reasonable clarifying questions, and take a reasonable amount of time to decide — routing any legal, tax, or negotiation-specific question to a qualified professional rather than answering it from general knowledge.

# Hiring Intelligence — Overview

> **Scope note:** "Hiring" throughout this module means the employer's own decision process for the job the candidate is pursuing — evaluation, internal feedback, and the offer that may follow. It is never a reference to Surely Placed's own enrollment process, which is covered separately in [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process).

## Purpose

By the time a candidate has been through screening and interviews, the process moves into territory the candidate can see even less of — internal evaluation, feedback synthesis, and a decision made by people the candidate may never speak with directly. This module gives advisors a realistic, general picture of that later stage so a candidate isn't left guessing during what's often the most anxious part of a job search.

## Scope

This module covers the general shape of an employer's evaluation and decision process after interviews, and how offers are typically communicated. It does not describe any specific employer's actual process, does not predict a specific decision, and does not provide legal or contract-specific guidance on any offer's terms.

## Core Content

[Hiring Lifecycle](/docs/hiring-intelligence/hiring-lifecycle) gives the organizational view a role moves through from opening to close. [Candidate Evaluation Framework](/docs/hiring-intelligence/candidate-evaluation-framework) and [Interview Feedback Process](/docs/hiring-intelligence/interview-feedback-process) cover how a candidate is actually assessed once interviews are underway. [Offer Decision Process](/docs/hiring-intelligence/offer-decision-process) and [Stakeholder Decision-Making](/docs/hiring-intelligence/stakeholder-decision-making) cover how a decision actually gets made internally. [Offer Management](/docs/hiring-intelligence/offer-management) covers what happens once an offer is extended. [Hiring Red Flags](/docs/hiring-intelligence/hiring-red-flags) covers what to notice and route, [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/hiring-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide) covers the coaching conversation, and [Hiring Intelligence Checklist](/docs/hiring-intelligence/hiring-intelligence-checklist) closes the module.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This module picks up directly where [Recruiter Intelligence](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/overview) leaves off — that module covers the recruiter's role through screening and early coordination; this one covers what happens from interviews through an actual offer, including the wider set of internal stakeholders a recruiter alone doesn't represent.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this module to help a candidate understand what's genuinely happening, invisibly, during the later stages of a process, without ever predicting a specific outcome or timeline. Keep this module's "hiring" entirely separate from any conversation about Surely Placed's own enrollment process.

# Hiring Intelligence Checklist

## Purpose

The nine articles ahead of this one cover the organizational lifecycle, how candidates get evaluated and feedback gets synthesized, how a decision and offer actually come together, what to watch for, and how to coach a candidate through all of it. This checklist draws them into one working reference.

## Scope

Use this list to confirm consistent, honest coverage during a decision-or-offer-stage conversation — it isn't a new source of guidance, and every item traces to a specific article. It stops short of describing any specific employer's actual process, timeline, or offer terms, none of which this repository defines.

## Core Content

**Organizational context**
- [ ] Can I explain, using [Hiring Lifecycle](/docs/hiring-intelligence/hiring-lifecycle), why a role's pace and visibility often trace back to internal factors rather than the candidate?
- [ ] Have I distinguished this module's "hiring" from Surely Placed's own enrollment process wherever the distinction matters?

**Evaluation and feedback**
- [ ] Have I covered the full range of evaluation categories in [Candidate Evaluation Framework](/docs/hiring-intelligence/candidate-evaluation-framework), not just technical depth?
- [ ] Have I reframed post-interview silence using [Interview Feedback Process](/docs/hiring-intelligence/interview-feedback-process) rather than letting it read as a bad sign by default?

**Decision and offer**
- [ ] Have I been honest, per [Offer Decision Process](/docs/hiring-intelligence/offer-decision-process), that positive feedback doesn't guarantee an offer?
- [ ] Have I accounted for the wider stakeholder group in [Stakeholder Decision-Making](/docs/hiring-intelligence/stakeholder-decision-making) rather than assuming one person decides?
- [ ] Have I helped the candidate read any received offer carefully per [Offer Management](/docs/hiring-intelligence/offer-management), without directing their decision?

**Red flags and coaching conversation**
- [ ] Have I distinguished a genuine concern in [Hiring Red Flags](/docs/hiring-intelligence/hiring-red-flags) from ordinary decision-stage delay?
- [ ] Did the coaching conversation itself follow [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/hiring-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide) — accurate context over empty reassurance, and genuine care for the candidate as a person?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist closes Hiring Intelligence the same way the closing checklists in Interview, Resume, LinkedIn, and Recruiter Intelligence close their own modules — confirming consistent application, not introducing anything beyond what the nine preceding articles already cover.

## Advisor Guidance

Run this as a quick check before or during a decision-or-offer conversation, and return to the article behind any item that feels uncertain.

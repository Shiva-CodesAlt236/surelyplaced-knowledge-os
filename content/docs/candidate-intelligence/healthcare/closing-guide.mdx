# Closing Guide

> Nothing in this guide states a figure, discount, promised outcome, or regulatory fact — it covers closing technique only.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to `ROLE-HEALTH`, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for Healthcare IT candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before asking for a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), confirm any licensure or regulatory question raised during the call has actually been routed and resolved rather than assumed settled — per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/objection-guide), an unresolved question here is a reliable way to stall a decision that would otherwise move forward.

Ground the close in the candidate's actual described track and motivation, including the mission-driven element when it's genuinely present, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment) — a close that only addresses logistics can feel incomplete to this population.

Apply [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) for this step, and remain attentive to financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) as this framework expects for every role family.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's mission-and-caution findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm any regulatory or licensure question was properly resolved before closing, ground the close in the candidate's actual motivation, and stay alert to financial vulnerability as with any role family.

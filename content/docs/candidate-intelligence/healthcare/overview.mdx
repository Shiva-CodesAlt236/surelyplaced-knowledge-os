# Healthcare Role Collection — Overview

> This is the ninth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, following Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, QA Testing, Business Analysis, Project Management, and Supply Chain.

## Purpose

This article opens the Healthcare Role Collection, mapped to `ROLE-HEALTH` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and to the [Healthcare Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/healthcare-playbook). It walks through the four working patterns that follow and shows where this collection fits among those already published.

## Scope

Surely Placed places IT candidates, so this collection covers the technology-focused slice of healthcare careers specifically — health information systems, informatics, and IT support and coordination within healthcare organizations. It does not cover clinical practice, and nothing in this collection states a licensure, regulatory, or clinical fact; any such question is routed to an appropriate internal or external expert, consistent with the [Healthcare Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/healthcare-playbook).

## Core Content

Four working patterns make up this collection: [Health Informatics Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/health-informatics-analyst), [EHR / Clinical Systems Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/ehr-clinical-systems-analyst), [Healthcare IT Support Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/healthcare-it-support-specialist), and [Healthcare IT Project Coordinator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/healthcare-it-project-coordinator). From there, the collection covers representative tools at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/technology-ecosystem), sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), the [Healthcare Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/healthcare-playbook), and the fourteen-article pattern already established across the eight collections before it.

## Advisor Guidance

Work through this overview first, before any single working-pattern article in the collection. Never state a licensure, regulatory, or clinical fact directly in any conversation this collection informs — route that question to the appropriate expert every time, exactly as the underlying playbook requires.

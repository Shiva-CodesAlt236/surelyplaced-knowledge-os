# Advisor Coaching

> The coaching example that follows is a constructed illustration only, clearly marked as such, and describes no real advisor, candidate, or conversation.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for `ROLE-HEALTH`, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with Healthcare IT candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor ever guessed at a licensure, regulatory, or clinical detail instead of routing it to an appropriate expert — a serious lapse under `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) given this field's stakes; whether the candidate's mission-driven motivation was genuinely engaged with rather than treated as a logistics afterthought; and whether the candidate's specific track — informatics, clinical systems, frontline support, or coordination — was correctly identified rather than treated as one undifferentiated "healthcare" category.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — said directly that a regulatory question needed expert review rather than offering a guess, which the candidate later cited as building real trust"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches should treat any guessed regulatory or licensure answer as a serious, non-negotiable coaching flag, alongside genuine engagement with mission-driven motivation and accurate track identification, per the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

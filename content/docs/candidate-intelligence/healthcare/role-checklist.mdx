# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Healthcare Role Collection, the ninth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Healthcare Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the eight preceding collections established.

## Scope

This checklist verifies all fourteen articles in this collection, following the same organization used throughout: working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Health Informatics Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/health-informatics-analyst), [EHR / Clinical Systems Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/ehr-clinical-systems-analyst), [Healthcare IT Support Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/healthcare-it-support-specialist), and [Healthcare IT Project Coordinator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/healthcare-it-project-coordinator) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is each named tool presented strictly as an illustrative example, free from comparison or ranking?
- [ ] Is there any instance where a named product is favored over a competing one?
- [ ] Does the entire collection avoid stating any licensure, regulatory, or clinical fact, routing such questions to an appropriate expert instead?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular mission-and-caution mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook?
- [ ] Does each stage guide draw directly on its corresponding core sales-stage module rather than introducing an original technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Is every article free of hiring figures, salary numbers, promised results, and employer-specific claims?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the eight preceding collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its regulatory-caution requirement specific to healthcare.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the eight preceding collections' checklists to confirm all nine Role Collections remain structurally consistent.

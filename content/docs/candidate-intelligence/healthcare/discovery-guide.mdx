# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to `ROLE-HEALTH`, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook. This guide states no clinical or regulatory fact at any point.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Healthcare IT candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Ask about the candidate's specific track within healthcare IT early, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/candidate-behaviour) — confirming whether they sit closer to informatics, clinical systems, frontline support, or coordination signals genuine understanding of a field this population knows isn't one undifferentiated category.

Consistent with [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), ask directly what draws the candidate to their target role beyond the practical logistics — this population's motivation is often genuinely mission-connected, and surfacing that explicitly tends to build trust rather than feeling like an unnecessary detour.

If a licensure, regulatory, or clinical question comes up, say plainly that it will be routed to the appropriate expert rather than answering directly — per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/healthcare/buying-patterns), leaving that kind of question open is one of the more reliable ways to lose this population's trust.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's mission-and-caution findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm the candidate's specific track early, ask directly about mission-connected motivation, and route any licensure or regulatory question to the right expert immediately rather than leaving it open.

# Technology Ecosystem

> Every product named in this article — including SIEM platforms generally, or specific examples such as Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk, CrowdStrike, Microsoft Defender, Kali Linux, Burp Suite, or Wireshark — is mentioned strictly as an illustrative example of a technology category. None is evaluated, ranked, compared against another, or recommended.

## Purpose

[Security Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/security-analyst) through [Cloud Security Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/cloud-security-engineer) each touch on pattern-specific technology categories. This article consolidates the categories that cut across all four patterns.

## Scope

This article names general technology categories relevant to cybersecurity broadly — monitoring and alerting, endpoint protection, offensive-testing utilities, and network analysis — without evaluating or comparing specific products within each category.

## Core Content

**Security information and event management (SIEM)** is a category central to monitoring-focused work, particularly [SOC Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/soc-analyst) — illustrative examples of products in this category include Microsoft Sentinel and Splunk, among many others, named here only to indicate the category, not to compare or endorse either.

**Endpoint detection and response** is a category relevant across several patterns, with illustrative examples including CrowdStrike and Microsoft Defender — again named only as category examples.

**Offensive-testing and network-analysis tooling** is most directly relevant to [Penetration Tester](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/penetration-tester), with illustrative examples including Kali Linux as an operating-system distribution built for testing, Burp Suite as a web-application testing utility category, and Wireshark as a network-traffic analysis category.

**Cloud posture and identity management** tooling is most directly relevant to [Cloud Security Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/cloud-security-engineer), varying by which cloud platform a candidate has worked within.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article is the cross-cutting companion to the four pattern-specific articles in this collection, paralleling [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/technology-ecosystem) in the Data & AI collection structurally while covering entirely distinct, cybersecurity-specific content.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this article only for general orientation, never to compare a candidate's stated tools against one another or to suggest one platform is preferable. Route any genuine technical evaluation to internal SMEs, and record what a candidate states in `technology_summary` factually and without ranking.

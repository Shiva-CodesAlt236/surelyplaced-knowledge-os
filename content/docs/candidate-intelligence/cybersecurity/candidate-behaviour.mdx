# Candidate Behaviour

> The tendencies described here are general patterns across a broad role family, not fixed traits of any individual. Treat every candidate's actual words as the real source of information, not this article.

## Purpose

This article describes behavioral tendencies across the four `ROLE-SEC` patterns — security analysts, SOC analysts, penetration testers, and cloud security engineers — so an advisor recognizes common threads without flattening a genuinely diverse role family into one profile.

## Scope

This article covers general tendencies across cybersecurity candidates. It does not describe a real or specific candidate, and none of these tendencies should be treated as universal.

## Core Content

A risk-aware, evidence-seeking evaluation style runs across all four patterns, consistent with `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` in [Buyer Persona Schema](/docs/candidate-intelligence/BUYER_PERSONA_SCHEMA), but its expression differs by pattern. Security analysts often frame it through process and documentation questions; SOC analysts through structured, procedural questions reflecting their day-to-day work; penetration testers through direct, evidence-and-credential-based skepticism; and cloud security engineers through a blend of security and infrastructure concerns.

Trust and credibility carry particular weight in this role family generally — cybersecurity professionals evaluate services partly through the lens of their own professional training in spotting inconsistency or overstatement, so accuracy and consistency across a conversation matter more here than in most other role families, echoing [Trust and Credibility](/docs/objections/trust-and-credibility).

Career stage and visa category operate largely independently of working pattern, the same as in the other collections — an F-1 OPT security analyst and an H-1B penetration tester face different timeline pressures despite both falling within `ROLE-SEC`. Consult the specific Visa Playbook rather than inferring urgency from role.

## Advisor Guidance

Expect risk-aware, evidence-seeking behavior broadly, calibrated to the specific working pattern a candidate matches. Hold consistency and accuracy as a particularly high priority with this role family given their professional training in spotting overstatement.

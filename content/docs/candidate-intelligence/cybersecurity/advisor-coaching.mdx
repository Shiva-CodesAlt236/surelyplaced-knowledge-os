# Advisor Coaching

> Every example below is a hypothetical, clearly labeled illustration — not a record of an actual call.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for `ROLE-SEC`, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with cybersecurity candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor maintained the precision and accuracy this trust-sensitive role family expects, consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) when that standard slips; whether a trust or credibility objection, when raised, was genuinely resolved rather than glossed over, per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/objection-guide); and whether the advisor correctly calibrated to the candidate's specific working pattern rather than applying a generic cybersecurity approach.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — corrected a minor inaccuracy proactively before the candidate could notice it, reinforcing trust with a candidate from a highly scrutiny-trained role family"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

Coaches should prioritize accuracy discipline, genuine resolution of trust concerns, and working-pattern calibration when reviewing calls with this role family, alongside the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

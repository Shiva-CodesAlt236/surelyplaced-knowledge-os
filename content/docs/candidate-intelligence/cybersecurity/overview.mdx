# Cybersecurity Role Collection: Overview

> This is the third Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework. Tools named anywhere in this collection — SIEM platforms, endpoint detection products, offensive-security utilities, and similar — appear strictly as illustrative ecosystem examples. This collection never compares those tools against one another and never recommends one vendor over another.

## Purpose

Cybersecurity candidates make up a meaningful share of `ROLE-SEC` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION). This collection gives advisors the same structured depth for this role family that the Software Engineering and Data & AI collections already provide for theirs.

## Scope

This collection treats cybersecurity as one role family with four working patterns commonly seen within it. No article here is a deep dive into a specific tool, platform, or vendor product — every technology mention stays at the illustrative, category level.

## Core Content

Thirteen articles follow this overview: four working-pattern articles ([Security Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/security-analyst), [SOC Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/soc-analyst), [Penetration Tester](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/penetration-tester), [Cloud Security Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/cloud-security-engineer)), a cross-cutting [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/technology-ecosystem) article, two Sales Intelligence articles, four advisor guides ordered by funnel stage, and closing articles on coaching and a role checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection rests on [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the Sprint 12 [Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), [Cybersecurity Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/cybersecurity-playbook), and the structural precedent set by the [Software Engineering](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview) and [Data & AI](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/overview) collections, alongside Discovery through Sales Constitution. No new sales technique originates here.

## Advisor Guidance

Reach for the Reference Profile when a candidate matches its narrow early-career, F-1 OPT, software engineering archetype closely. Reach for this collection whenever a candidate's role sits within `ROLE-SEC` — security analysis, SOC work, penetration testing, or cloud security — regardless of visa category or seniority.

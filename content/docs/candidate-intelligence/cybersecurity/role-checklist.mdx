# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Cybersecurity Role Collection, the third complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Cybersecurity Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the Software Engineering and Data & AI collections established.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized the same way as the two preceding collections: working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Security Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/security-analyst), [SOC Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/soc-analyst), [Penetration Tester](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/penetration-tester), and [Cloud Security Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/cloud-security-engineer) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every named tool throughout the collection — including SIEM, Sentinel, Splunk, CrowdStrike, Defender, Kali Linux, Burp Suite, and Wireshark — treated purely as an illustrative example, never compared or ranked against another?
- [ ] Does the collection avoid recommending any vendor over another anywhere?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular trust-sensitivity rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook?
- [ ] Does each guide build on its corresponding stage-specific module rather than inventing new technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Is the collection free of any stated hiring statistic, salary figure, guarantee, or employer-specific expectation?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the Software Engineering and Data & AI collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its added never-compare, never-recommend constraint.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the Software Engineering and Data & AI collections' checklists to confirm all three Role Collections remain structurally consistent.

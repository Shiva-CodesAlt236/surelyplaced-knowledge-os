# Presentation Guide

> No specific price, statistic, or guarantee is stated in this guide.

## Purpose

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation) to the `ROLE-SEC` family specifically.

## Scope

This guide covers presenting Surely Placed's value to cybersecurity candidates broadly, building on the existing Discussion and Sales Constitution modules.

## Core Content

Present Surely Placed's documented scope with particular precision, consistent with [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation) — this role family's professional training in spotting inconsistency, noted in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/candidate-behaviour), means any imprecision is more likely to be noticed and to cost credibility than with other role families.

Tie the presentation to the candidate's specific working pattern and stated goals rather than a generic cybersecurity pitch, since this role family's genuinely diverse patterns respond poorly to a one-size-fits-all framing.

When ROI comes up, which [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/buying-patterns) treats as an especially trust-sensitive moment for this role family, apply [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) with extra care — an overstated or approximated figure risks more damage here than elsewhere in this Knowledge OS.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed), [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation), and [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) directly, shaped by this collection's behavior and buying-pattern findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Prioritize precision over polish with this role family — a candidate in this family is more likely to notice and react to a small overstatement than an average candidate. Ground every claim in what's actually documented rather than general enthusiasm.

Presenting to this role family requires particular precision, since candidates here are professionally trained to notice inconsistency or overstatement — ground every claim in documented fact, tie it to the candidate's specific working pattern, and apply the ROI Conversations discipline with extra care.

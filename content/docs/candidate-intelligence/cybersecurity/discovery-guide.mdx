# Discovery Guide

> This guide is directional, not a script — every candidate's actual situation should shape the conversation more than any guidance written here.

## Purpose

This guide adapts [Discovery](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) to the `ROLE-SEC` family's four working patterns.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for cybersecurity candidates broadly, deferring to the relevant Visa Playbook for visa-specific questions and to [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification) for the underlying qualification framework.

## Core Content

Open consistent with [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), holding a slightly higher bar for accuracy and consistency from the first exchange, given the trust-sensitivity noted in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/candidate-behaviour).

Identify which of the four working patterns the candidate matches early — [Security Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/security-analyst), [SOC Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/soc-analyst), [Penetration Tester](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/penetration-tester), or [Cloud Security Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/cloud-security-engineer) — since these patterns diverge meaningfully in daily work and evaluation style.

For penetration tester candidates specifically, ask openly about the full range of their experience, including self-directed or certification-based background, rather than assuming a conventional employment-only history.

Confirm visa category directly and route to the matching Visa Playbook, since timeline pressure varies independently of which cybersecurity pattern a candidate works in.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals) directly, generalized through this collection's working-pattern articles.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm working pattern, visa category, and career stage independently, and ask penetration tester candidates specifically about the full breadth of their experience rather than assuming a standard employment path.

## Key Takeaways

Discovery across the cybersecurity role family should confirm working pattern, visa category, and career stage independently, hold a high accuracy bar from the outset given this role family's trust-sensitivity, and ask penetration tester candidates openly about non-traditional experience.

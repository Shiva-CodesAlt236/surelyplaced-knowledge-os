# Backend Specializations

> This article discusses backend engineering only at the ecosystem level. Any technology named below is an illustrative example of a category, not an endorsement, ranking, or exhaustive list — actual technologies and interview expectations vary by employer and role.

## Purpose

Backend-focused candidates are a large share of the software engineering candidates Surely Placed works with. This article describes what generally distinguishes a backend specialization at the ecosystem level, so an advisor can recognize the pattern without needing deep technical expertise.

## Scope

This article covers backend engineering as a category of specialization within `ROLE-SWE` — general characteristics, ecosystem-level technology categories, and how this specialization tends to differ from frontend, full-stack, and mobile work. It does not evaluate any specific language, framework, or database technically, and it does not rank technologies against each other.

## Core Content

Backend engineering generally centers on server-side logic, data storage, and the systems that power an application behind the scenes rather than what a user directly sees. Candidates in this specialization often describe their work in terms of services, APIs, databases, and system reliability rather than visual interfaces.

At the ecosystem level, backend work commonly involves a server-side programming language (illustrative examples include languages such as Java, Python, or Node.js, among many others), a database or data-storage layer, and increasingly some exposure to cloud infrastructure — but which specific combination a given candidate has used varies enormously by employer, team, and project, and no single combination should be assumed.

Backend-focused candidates often bring a systems-level, reliability-oriented mindset to how they evaluate a service like Surely Placed as well — favoring specific, verifiable claims over general reassurance, consistent with the `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` pattern described in [Buyer Persona Schema](/docs/candidate-intelligence/BUYER_PERSONA_SCHEMA).

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article extends [Software Engineering Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/software-engineering-playbook) and [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING) specifically to the backend specialization, without introducing any new technical claim beyond what those articles already establish at a general level.

## Advisor Guidance

Record a candidate's stated backend focus in `technology_summary` per [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING) using their own words, not an inferred technology stack. Route any genuine technical evaluation question to internal technical SMEs rather than assessing backend competence directly, and avoid assuming a specific tool or platform based on the "backend" label alone.

# Objection Guide

> This guide anticipates likely objection patterns across a broad role family. It does not describe a real conversation and states no specific price, discount, or guarantee.

## Purpose

[Reference Profile: Objection Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/objection-strategy) covers likely objections for one specific archetype. This guide generalizes that anticipation across the full `ROLE-SWE` role family.

## Scope

This guide covers likely objection patterns for software engineering candidates broadly, routing each to existing Objections and Pricing guidance rather than introducing new objection-handling technique.

## Core Content

**Comparison objections** are common across this role family regardless of specialization, consistent with the evidence-seeking pattern in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour) — apply [Handling Price Objections](/docs/pricing/handling-price-objections) and, where a specific alternative consultancy is named, [Already Working With a Consultancy](/docs/objections/already-working-with-a-consultancy).

**Value-uncertainty objections**, often expressed as a direct request for an ROI figure, are common across career stages within this role family, consistent with [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/buying-patterns) — apply [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) regardless of the candidate's apparent seniority.

**Visa-timeline-driven urgency or hesitation** varies significantly by visa category and should be handled using the matching Visa Playbook rather than a generic software-engineering response — an F-1 OPT candidate's timeline pressure differs meaningfully from an H-1B candidate's, for instance.

**Affordability concerns tied to first-time major financial decisions** are more likely for earlier-career candidates, consistent with [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/buying-patterns) — apply [Financial Decision-Makers](/docs/pricing/financial-decision-makers) where a co-decision-maker may be relevant, and [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) if genuine financial vulnerability appears.

## Cross-Module Alignment

Every objection type and response in this guide routes directly to existing guidance in Objections, Pricing, and the relevant Visa Playbook — no new objection-handling technique is introduced.

## Advisor Guidance

Diagnose which objection type is actually present using [Handling Price Objections](/docs/pricing/handling-price-objections)' diagnostic approach, and route visa-specific concerns to the candidate's actual, confirmed visa category rather than a generic response.

This role family is most likely to raise comparison objections, ROI-figure requests, visa-timeline urgency that varies by specific category, and first-time-financial-decision affordability concerns — each routed to existing Objections and Pricing guidance rather than a new, role-family-specific technique.

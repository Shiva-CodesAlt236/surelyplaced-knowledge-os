# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Software Engineering Role Collection, the first complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework. Use it both to verify this collection and as a template for any future role collection.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Software Engineering Role Collection meets the framework's standards, and doubles as a working template for whichever role family is chosen for a future Role Collection.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized by the same groupings used in [Overview](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview): specializations, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Specializations and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Backend](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/backend-specializations), [Frontend](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/frontend-specializations), [Full-Stack](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/fullstack-specializations), and [Mobile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/mobile-specializations) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone article for any specific language, framework, or platform?
- [ ] Does every named technology throughout the collection carry an explicit "illustrative example, varies by employer" disclaimer?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/buying-patterns) generalize beyond the Sprint 12 reference archetype rather than assuming every candidate is early-career and on F-1 OPT?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook rather than restating or assuming a single visa category?
- [ ] Does every guide apply existing stage-specific modules directly, without introducing new sales technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical and illustrative only?
- [ ] Does every article in this collection avoid stating any hiring statistic, salary figure, guarantee, or employer-specific expectation?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the discipline established in [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES) and [Reference Profile: Checklist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/reference-profile-checklist), applied specifically to a Role Collection rather than a single reference profile.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection for accuracy, and adapt its structure directly when a future Role Collection is created for a different role family — the section groupings here (specializations/ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, coaching) generalize to any role family, not just software engineering.

# Presentation Guide

> This guide covers presentation-stage advisor approach across the software engineering role family. It states no specific price, statistic, or guarantee.

## Purpose

[Reference Profile: Presentation Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/presentation-strategy) covers presentation for one specific archetype. This guide generalizes that approach across the full `ROLE-SWE` role family.

## Scope

This guide covers how to present Surely Placed's value to software engineering candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation) rather than restating them.

## Core Content

Present Surely Placed's documented scope honestly and specifically, consistent with [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation), regardless of the candidate's specific specialization — the evidence-seeking evaluation style described in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour) applies broadly across this role family, not only to the Sprint 12 reference archetype.

Connect the presentation to what the candidate specifically described in discovery — their specialization, their stated goals, their visa timeline if relevant — rather than a generic pitch, since this role family generally responds better to specificity than to broad enthusiasm.

When a candidate asks about outcomes or return, which [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/buying-patterns) identifies as common across this role family regardless of career stage, apply [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) precisely — redirect to qualitative, structural value without citing a figure, holding that line as firmly for an experienced candidate as for an early-career one.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed), [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation), and [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) directly, generalized using this collection's behavior and buying-pattern articles.

## Advisor Guidance

Ground every presentation in what the specific candidate has actually said, not a generic software-engineering pitch, and hold the ROI Conversations discipline consistently regardless of how confident or experienced the candidate appears.

Presenting to this role family works best through specific, honest description connected to what the candidate has actually said, with the ROI Conversations discipline held consistently regardless of the candidate's apparent experience level — generalized here beyond the single Sprint 12 reference archetype.

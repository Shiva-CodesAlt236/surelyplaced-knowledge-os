# Discovery Guide

> This guide covers discovery-stage advisor approach across the software engineering role family. It is guidance, not a script, and assumes no specific candidate situation beyond what's stated directly by the candidate.

## Purpose

[Reference Profile: Discovery Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/discovery-strategy) covers discovery for one specific archetype. This guide generalizes that approach across the full `ROLE-SWE` role family, so it applies regardless of a candidate's specific specialization, visa category, or career stage.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for software engineering candidates broadly. It defers to the specific Visa Playbook for visa-specific discovery questions and to [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification) for the general qualification framework this guide builds on.

## Core Content

Open the call consistent with [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), and expect the evidence-seeking, specific-question pattern described in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour) to appear regardless of specialization — this is a role-family-wide tendency, not specific to any one specialization.

Confirm the candidate's specific specialization early, using [Backend](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/backend-specializations), [Frontend](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/frontend-specializations), [Full-Stack](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/fullstack-specializations), or [Mobile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/mobile-specializations) as reference for what to expect, rather than assuming from job title alone.

Confirm the candidate's visa category directly and route to the corresponding Visa Playbook for any timeline-specific discovery questions — this guide doesn't restate visa-specific urgency, since it varies meaningfully by category as covered in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour).

Use [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals) to calibrate for career stage — an early-career candidate's goals and an experienced candidate's goals often differ substantially even within the same specialization, and this guide should not be applied as if every candidate matches the Sprint 12 reference profile's early-career framing.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals) directly, generalized using this collection's specialization and behavior articles rather than the single Sprint 12 reference profile.

## Advisor Guidance

Treat specialization, visa category, and career stage as three independent dimensions to confirm directly with each candidate — never inferred from one another. Route visa-specific questions to the matching Visa Playbook rather than this guide.

# Mobile Specializations

> This article discusses mobile engineering only at the ecosystem level. Any platform or tool named below is an illustrative example of a category, not a ranking or exhaustive list — actual platform focus and interview expectations vary by employer.

## Purpose

This article completes the four-specialization view of `ROLE-SWE` in this collection, describing what generally distinguishes mobile-focused candidates from the backend, frontend, and full-stack specializations already covered.

## Scope

This article covers mobile engineering as a category of specialization within `ROLE-SWE`. It does not evaluate any specific mobile platform or development approach technically, and takes no position on which platform focus is more valuable or in demand.

## Core Content

Mobile engineering generally centers on building applications specifically for phones and tablets, which introduces platform-specific considerations — illustrative examples include native development approaches tied to a specific operating system, or cross-platform approaches intended to work across systems — that don't apply in the same way to web-focused backend, frontend, or full-stack work.

Which specific platform approach a given candidate has experience with, and how that experience was gained, varies significantly and should never be assumed from the "mobile" label alone. A mobile-focused candidate may have specialized in one platform exclusively or worked across multiple, and this distinction matters more to genuine technical evaluation than this repository is positioned to assess.

Mobile candidates sometimes describe their interest in terms of the tangible, consumer-facing nature of the work — building something a large number of people interact with directly — which can be a distinct discovery thread from the more infrastructure-oriented framing common among backend-focused candidates.

## Cross-Module Alignment

## Advisor Guidance

Ask directly which platform approach a candidate has worked in, rather than assuming from the "mobile" label, and record their answer in `technology_summary` using their own words per [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING). Route any genuine platform-specific technical evaluation to internal SMEs.

## Key Takeaways

Mobile engineering candidates work within platform-specific considerations distinct from web-focused specializations, and may be drawn to the tangible, consumer-facing nature of the work — but which specific platform approach applies to a given candidate should always be asked directly, never assumed from the specialization label.

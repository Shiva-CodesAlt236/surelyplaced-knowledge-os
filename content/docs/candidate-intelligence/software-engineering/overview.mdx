# Software Engineering Role Collection: Overview

> This is the first Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework — broader than the single [Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), which describes one illustrative early-career, F-1 OPT archetype. This collection covers the `ROLE-SWE` role family across specializations, visa categories, and career stages. Technologies are discussed only at the ecosystem level; no article in this collection covers a specific language, framework, or cloud platform in isolation.

## Purpose

The Reference Profile from Sprint 12 demonstrated the framework using one narrow, illustrative archetype. This collection generalizes that pattern to the full `ROLE-SWE` role family as defined in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) — covering the range of specializations and candidate situations an advisor will actually encounter, not just the single reference case.

## Scope

This collection covers software engineering as a broad discipline: backend, frontend, full-stack, and mobile specializations at the ecosystem level, general candidate behavior and buying patterns across the role family, and stage-by-stage advisor guidance. It does not create separate articles for individual languages, frameworks, or platforms (Java, Python, React, AWS, Node, .NET, or similar) — those remain ecosystem-level mentions within the broader specialization articles, never standalone deep dives.

## Core Content

This collection contains thirteen articles beyond this overview: four specialization articles ([Backend](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/backend-specializations), [Frontend](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/frontend-specializations), [Full-Stack](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/fullstack-specializations), [Mobile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/mobile-specializations)), one cross-cutting [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/technology-ecosystem) article, two role-family-level Sales Intelligence articles ([Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/buying-patterns)), four stage-by-stage advisor guides ([Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/closing-guide)), and closing articles on [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/advisor-coaching) and a [Role Checklist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/role-checklist).

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds directly on [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the Sprint 12 [Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), [Software Engineering Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/software-engineering-playbook), the Visa Playbooks module, and Discovery, Discussion, Closing, Objections, Pricing, Sales Operations, Sales Coaching, and Sales Constitution. No new sales technique or candidate fact is introduced anywhere in this collection beyond what those modules already establish.

## Advisor Guidance

Use the Reference Profile ([Overview](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview)) when working with a candidate who closely matches its specific archetype. Use this collection when a candidate's specialization, visa category, or career stage differs from that archetype but still falls within `ROLE-SWE` — this collection is written to generalize across that broader range rather than assuming any single candidate situation.

# Advisor Coaching

> This article covers coaching review priorities for this role family generally, not a real conversation record. Any example is hypothetical and clearly marked.

## Purpose

[Reference Profile: Advisor Coaching Notes](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/advisor-coaching-notes) demonstrates coaching signal usage for one specific archetype. This article generalizes those review priorities across the full `ROLE-SWE` role family.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach or reviewer should prioritize when reviewing calls with software engineering candidates broadly, using the codes defined in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS).

## Core Content

Across this role family, the recurring risk areas worth prioritizing in review are: whether the advisor correctly distinguished the evidence-seeking `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` pattern described in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour) from genuine disengagement, consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS); whether the ROI Conversations discipline held under a direct request for a figure, which [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/buying-patterns) identifies as common across this role family; and whether visa timeline urgency, where relevant, was ever used as closing pressure rather than treated as a factor the candidate is honestly weighing.

Because this role family spans a wide range of career stages, coaching review should also check whether the advisor correctly calibrated their approach to the specific candidate's actual experience level rather than applying early-career assumptions from the Sprint 12 reference profile universally.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note demonstrating format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — correctly distinguished detailed technical questioning from disengagement, per Candidate Behaviour"]`. No advisor or candidate matching this note exists; it exists purely to show the format.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article applies [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) and [Reading a Sales Conversation](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation) to this role family's specific risk patterns, established earlier in this collection.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches reviewing calls with software engineering candidates should prioritize signal-versus-disengagement calibration, ROI Conversations discipline, and visa-timeline pressure discipline as the three most role-family-specific review areas, in addition to the general review framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

# Closing Guide

> This guide covers closing-stage advisor approach across the software engineering role family. It states no specific price, guarantee, or placement outcome.

## Purpose

[Reference Profile: Closing Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/closing-strategy) covers closing for one specific archetype. This guide generalizes that approach across the full `ROLE-SWE` role family, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for software engineering candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation) rather than restating them.

## Core Content

Confirm genuine readiness before moving to a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) — for this role family broadly, verify that the evidence-seeking questions typical of [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/candidate-behaviour) were actually resolved earlier in the conversation, not just that the call progressed.

Ask for the commitment directly, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), without leaning on visa timeline urgency as a pressure tactic — where relevant, the specific visa category's timeline (confirmed via the matching Visa Playbook) is a real factor the candidate is weighing, not a lever to close faster.

If hesitation appears, apply [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation), watching for genuine financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) — more likely, per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/buying-patterns), for earlier-career candidates navigating a first major financial decision, though this should always be confirmed rather than assumed.

Handle payment and agreement consistent with [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), watching for a financial co-decision-maker per [Financial Decision-Makers](/docs/pricing/financial-decision-makers) where the candidate's career stage suggests it may be relevant.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation), and [Financial Decision-Makers](/docs/pricing/financial-decision-makers) directly, generalized using this collection's behavior and buying-pattern articles.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm that this role family's characteristic evidence-seeking questions were genuinely resolved before closing, never use visa timeline as a pressure tactic, and calibrate attention to financial vulnerability and co-decision-makers to the specific candidate's actual career stage and circumstances.

# Role Checklist

> This article closes the SAP Role Collection, the eleventh complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework and the second of this batch's four platform-focused collections.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the SAP Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the preceding collections established.

## Scope

This checklist steps through every one of the collection's fourteen articles, in the same order used throughout the framework: working patterns, ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [SAP Functional Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-functional-analyst), [SAP Technical Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-technical-developer), [SAP Business Process Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-business-process-consultant), and [SAP Solution Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-solution-architect) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every named tool treated only as a category example, never compared or ranked against another?
- [ ] Does the collection stay free of any comparison to a competing platform, any claim about market position, and any push toward a certification?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular process-rigor evaluation mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/closing-guide) stay platform-neutral throughout?
- [ ] Does each stage guide build on its matching core sales-stage module rather than introducing a technique of its own?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Is the collection free throughout of hiring-demand claims, salary figures, promised results, and employer-specific statements?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist follows the same shape as every checklist before it and as [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), adapted here for this collection's four working patterns and this batch's platform-neutrality requirement.

## Advisor Guidance

Run through this checklist during any review of this collection, cross-checking it against earlier collections' checklists so the framework holds together structurally.

# SAP Role Collection — Overview

> This is the eleventh complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, and the second of four platform-focused collections generated together, following Salesforce.

## Purpose

This article opens the SAP Role Collection. No platform-specific Industry Playbook exists yet for SAP, so this collection classifies under `ROLE-PENDING` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and defers broader industry-level guidance to [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework) until a dedicated playbook is scoped. What follows introduces the collection's four working patterns and situates this collection relative to what came before it.

## Scope

This collection treats SAP as a single enterprise-software ecosystem a candidate might work within, not a product to be sold, evaluated, or ranked against anything else. The four working patterns ahead capture how a candidate whose background centers on this platform typically frames their own experience. Nothing here claims a market position, cites hiring demand, or points toward a specific certification.

## Core Content

Four working patterns make up this collection: [SAP Functional Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-functional-analyst), [SAP Technical Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-technical-developer), [SAP Business Process Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-business-process-consultant), and [SAP Solution Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-solution-architect). From there, the collection covers representative ecosystem tooling at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/technology-ecosystem), sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection rests on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), and [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework), following the fourteen-article pattern every collection before it has used.

## Advisor Guidance

Read this overview before moving into any single working-pattern article. Keep SAP framed as the candidate's working ecosystem only — no weighing it against a competing platform, no implication of market standing, and no certification suggestion the candidate hasn't already brought up themselves.

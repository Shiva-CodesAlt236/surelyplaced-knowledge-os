# Presentation Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discussion module to the SAP role family, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers presentation-call approach for SAP candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process).

## Core Content

Walk through [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process) in the same sequence introduced during discovery, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/candidate-behaviour) — restating the sequence consistently reinforces that the process is genuinely structured, not improvised.

When presenting, per [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed), frame the opportunity in terms relevant to the candidate's specific working pattern from this collection's range — module-specific, technical, cross-functional, or program-level — rather than a generic description of the platform.

Keep the language free of anything that could be read as weighing this platform against another, per this collection's platform-neutrality requirement — stay within the candidate's own working pattern and the role itself.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), shaped by this collection's process-rigor findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Restate the process sequence consistently with what was described during discovery, frame the opportunity in terms specific to the candidate's working pattern, and keep the conversation strictly platform-neutral throughout.

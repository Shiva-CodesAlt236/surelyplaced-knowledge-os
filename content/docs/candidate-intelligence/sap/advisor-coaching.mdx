# Advisor Coaching

> The coaching example that follows is a constructed illustration only, clearly marked as such, and describes no real advisor, candidate, or conversation.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for the SAP role family, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with SAP candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor described the process sequence clearly and consistently across the conversation — a lapse worth flagging under `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) given how heavily this population weighs sequencing consistency; whether the advisor stayed strictly platform-neutral throughout, never implying this platform's standing relative to any other; and whether a request for time to think was respected as structured evaluation rather than treated as a soft decline requiring pressure.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — noticed the candidate had flagged an earlier inconsistency in how a step was described and corrected it directly before moving forward"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

Coaches should treat consistent process sequencing and strict platform-neutrality as non-negotiable coaching priorities, alongside respecting a candidate's request for time to think as structured evaluation, per the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

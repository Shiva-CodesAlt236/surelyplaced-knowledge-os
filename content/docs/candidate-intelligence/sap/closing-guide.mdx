# Closing Guide

> No figure, discount, guaranteed outcome, or platform comparison appears anywhere in this guide — closing technique is its only subject.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to the SAP role family, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for SAP candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before asking for a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), walk the candidate through exactly what happens at each remaining step — per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/objection-guide), an unclear next step is a reliable way to stall a decision that would otherwise move forward with this population.

Ground the close in the candidate's specific working pattern and the scope they've described, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment) — a close that treats every candidate identically, regardless of whether they work at module, technical, process, or program scope, tends to feel less credible to this population.

Follow [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) at this step, and stay alert to signs of financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags), consistent with what this framework requires of every role family.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's process-rigor findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Walk through each remaining step clearly before asking for a decision, ground the close in the candidate's specific working pattern and scope, and stay alert to financial vulnerability as with any role family.

# SAP Business Process Consultant

## Purpose

This article describes the SAP Business Process Consultant working pattern — candidates who design and lead structured process change across multiple business areas, typically during a defined implementation or transformation phase.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a cross-functional, process-design-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe running structured workshops with stakeholders across several business areas, documenting current-state and future-state process design, and managing the change-management side of an implementation rather than a single module's configuration. Many carry a business-process or management-consulting background as much as a platform-specific one, and often describe their work in terms of implementation phases — blueprint, realization, testing, go-live — reflecting a structured lifecycle mindset common to this population.

This pattern differs from [SAP Functional Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-functional-analyst) in scope: where a Functional Analyst typically owns one module's configuration, a Business Process Consultant typically owns how several modules and business areas connect procedurally, and candidates in this pattern usually describe governance and structured rollout discipline as central to their value.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the cross-functional process-design point of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the single-module focus of [SAP Functional Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-functional-analyst) and the technical-build focus of [SAP Technical Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/sap/sap-technical-developer).

## Advisor Guidance

Ask which implementation phases the candidate has led or supported, since this population frequently organizes their own experience around that structured lifecycle. This pattern has genuine overlap with [Process & Operations Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/overview), which is worth pointing out directly when a candidate appears to sit close to that line.

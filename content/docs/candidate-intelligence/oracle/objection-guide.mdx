# Objection Guide

> No real conversation is described anywhere in this guide, and it names no price, discount, guaranteed result, or platform comparison.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Objection Handling module to the Oracle role family, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers objection-handling approach for Oracle candidates broadly, building on [Trust and Credibility](/docs/objections/trust-and-credibility) and [Already Working with a Consultancy](/docs/objections/already-working-with-a-consultancy).

## Core Content

Per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/candidate-behaviour), an objection that sounds like [Trust and Credibility](/docs/objections/trust-and-credibility) sometimes actually traces back to lingering scope ambiguity from earlier in the conversation — check whether the specific technical slice was ever fully confirmed before responding as though the concern is about the advisor's general credibility.

Given how broad this ecosystem is, [Already Working with a Consultancy](/docs/objections/already-working-with-a-consultancy) may come up more than once across a candidate's various specializations — acknowledge each one directly rather than treating it as a single, resolved concern.

Watch for hesitation that traces back to a role feeling too generically described relative to the candidate's specific specialization, per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/buying-patterns) — naming the specific slice directly usually resolves it faster than restating the opportunity's other merits.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Trust and Credibility](/docs/objections/trust-and-credibility) and [Already Working with a Consultancy](/docs/objections/already-working-with-a-consultancy), shaped by this collection's breadth-navigation findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Check whether the technical slice was ever fully confirmed before treating an objection as a general trust concern, address each instance of outside consultancy involvement individually, and name the specific slice directly when scope-related hesitation appears.

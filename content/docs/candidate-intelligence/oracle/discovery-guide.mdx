# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to the Oracle role family, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Oracle candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Ask the candidate to specify exactly which slice of this ecosystem their background centers on early, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/candidate-behaviour) — database, applications, integration, or some defined combination — since this population has often been burned by a conversation that stayed generic on this point.

Consistent with [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), confirm which of this collection's four working patterns most closely matches the candidate's actual experience, rather than assuming a single label covers their background.

Once the specific slice is confirmed, expect the conversation to open up, per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/buying-patterns) — this population's early caution reflects learned habit, not reluctance about the opportunity itself.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's breadth-navigation findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Ask early and specifically which slice of the ecosystem the candidate's background centers on, confirm the matching working pattern, and expect genuine engagement to open up once that precision is established.

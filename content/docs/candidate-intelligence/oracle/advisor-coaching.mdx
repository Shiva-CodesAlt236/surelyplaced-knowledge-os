# Advisor Coaching

> The coaching example that follows is a constructed illustration only, clearly marked as such, and describes no real advisor, candidate, or conversation.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for the Oracle role family, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with Oracle candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor confirmed the candidate's specific technical slice early and precisely, rather than relying on the ecosystem's general name — a miss worth flagging under `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) given how much this population's engagement depends on that precision; whether the advisor stayed strictly platform-neutral throughout, never implying this platform's standing relative to any other; and whether scope stayed consistent across discovery, presentation, and closing rather than drifting.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — caught that the role description had drifted from the candidate's confirmed database specialization toward general applications language, and corrected it before the candidate noticed"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

# Oracle Role Collection — Overview

> This is the thirteenth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, and the fourth and final collection of this batch's platform-focused set, following Salesforce, SAP, and ServiceNow.

## Purpose

This article opens the Oracle Role Collection. No platform-specific Industry Playbook exists yet for Oracle, so this collection classifies under `ROLE-PENDING` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and defers broader industry-level guidance to [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework) until a dedicated playbook is scoped. From here, the article moves into the four working patterns that define this collection and how it fits alongside the collections that precede it.

## Scope

This collection treats Oracle as a single, broad enterprise-technology ecosystem a candidate might work within, spanning database, enterprise applications, and integration technology, not a product to be sold, evaluated, or ranked against anything else. It covers the working patterns a candidate typically describes when their professional background centers on this ecosystem. It states no market-position claim, no hiring-demand figure, and recommends no certification path.

## Core Content

Four working patterns make up this collection: [Oracle Database Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-database-administrator), [Oracle ERP Cloud Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-erp-cloud-analyst), [Oracle Developer & Integration Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-developer-integration-specialist), and [Oracle Solution Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-solution-architect). From there, the collection covers representative ecosystem tooling at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/technology-ecosystem), sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection draws on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), and [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework), and holds to the fourteen-article pattern every collection before it has followed.

## Advisor Guidance

Read through this overview ahead of any single working-pattern article that follows. Oracle should stay framed strictly as the candidate's working ecosystem throughout — no weighing it against a different platform, no hint that it holds a leading position, and no certification suggested unless the candidate brought it up first.

# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Oracle Role Collection, the thirteenth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework and the fourth and final collection in this batch's platform-focused set.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Oracle Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the preceding collections established.

## Scope

This checklist reviews the collection's fourteen articles one by one, in the sequence used throughout: working patterns, ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Oracle Database Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-database-administrator), [Oracle ERP Cloud Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-erp-cloud-analyst), [Oracle Developer & Integration Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-developer-integration-specialist), and [Oracle Solution Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-solution-architect) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every named tool limited to an illustrative, category-level mention with no ranking or comparison?
- [ ] Does the collection steer clear of comparing this ecosystem to a competitor, asserting any market position, or nudging toward a certification?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular breadth-navigation caution mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/closing-guide) stay platform-neutral throughout?
- [ ] Does every stage guide extend its matching core sales-stage module instead of introducing a new technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Does the collection stay clear of hiring-demand claims, salary figures, promised results, and employer-specific statements everywhere?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist carries forward the structure every earlier collection's checklist and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES) already established, fitted to this collection's four working patterns and this batch's platform-neutrality requirement.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the preceding collections' checklists to confirm structural consistency across the whole framework, including this batch's four platform-focused collections together.

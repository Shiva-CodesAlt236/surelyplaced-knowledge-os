# Closing Guide

> Closing technique is the only subject of this guide — it states no figure, no discount, no promised outcome, and draws no platform comparison.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to the Oracle role family, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for Oracle candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before asking for a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), reconfirm the specific technical slice one final time — per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/objection-guide), any lingering ambiguity about scope is a reliable way to stall a decision that would otherwise move forward with this population.

Ground the close in the candidate's specific working pattern rather than the ecosystem's general name, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment) — this population responds to precision at every stage, and closing is no exception.

Apply [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) here, and keep watching for financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags), as this framework requires regardless of role family.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's breadth-navigation findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Reconfirm the specific technical slice one final time before closing, ground the close in the candidate's specific working pattern, and stay alert to financial vulnerability as with any role family.

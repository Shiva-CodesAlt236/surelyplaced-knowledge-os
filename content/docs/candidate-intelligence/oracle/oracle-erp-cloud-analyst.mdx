# Oracle ERP Cloud Analyst

## Purpose

This article describes the Oracle ERP Cloud Analyst working pattern — candidates who configure and support cloud-based enterprise applications covering business functions like finance or supply chain operations.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes an applications-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe configuring modules within a cloud ERP application, supporting end users, and translating business-process needs into how the application is set up — work that sits at the applications layer of this ecosystem rather than the underlying database or infrastructure. Many carry a finance, supply chain, or operations background specific to the business function they support, meaning their functional business knowledge is often as central to their value as their applications knowledge.

Given how broad this ecosystem is, candidates in this pattern are often just as careful as the [Oracle Database Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-database-administrator) pattern about specifying their scope early — clarifying that their expertise is in cloud applications specifically, distinct from the database or on-premises infrastructure layers, helps avoid a mismatched role description.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the enterprise-applications point of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the core-database focus of [Oracle Database Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-database-administrator) and the custom-build focus of [Oracle Developer & Integration Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/oracle-developer-integration-specialist).

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm which specific business function the candidate's applications work centers on, and clarify early that a role centers on cloud applications specifically rather than database or infrastructure work — this population values that precision given the breadth of the ecosystem they work within.

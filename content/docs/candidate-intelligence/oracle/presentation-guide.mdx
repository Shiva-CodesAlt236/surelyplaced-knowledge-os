# Presentation Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discussion module to the Oracle role family, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers presentation-call approach for Oracle candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process).

## Core Content

Restate the specific technical slice confirmed during discovery at the start of the presentation, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/oracle/candidate-behaviour) — reinforcing that precision here signals the earlier conversation was genuinely heard, not just logged.

When explaining the process per [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), keep every reference to the opportunity scoped to that confirmed slice rather than drifting into the ecosystem's general name, since this population notices that drift quickly.

Rule out any phrasing that could read as a comparison to a different platform, per this collection's platform-neutrality requirement, and keep the opportunity described only in terms of the candidate's own working pattern and the role itself.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), shaped by this collection's breadth-navigation findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Restate the confirmed technical slice at the start of the presentation, keep every reference scoped to that slice rather than drifting into generic ecosystem language, and keep the conversation strictly platform-neutral throughout.

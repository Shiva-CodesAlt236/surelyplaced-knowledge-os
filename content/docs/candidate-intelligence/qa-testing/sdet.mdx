# SDET

## Purpose

This article describes the SDET (Software Development Engineer in Test) working pattern within `ROLE-QA` — candidates who build the testing infrastructure and tooling other testers and developers rely on, rather than primarily executing tests themselves.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes an SDET-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms closer to software development than testing execution — building internal frameworks, custom tooling, and testing infrastructure that other QA and engineering team members use. This pattern represents the most code-heavy end of the QA Testing spectrum, and candidates here often have a background that overlaps meaningfully with general software engineering, sometimes having worked in both disciplines over the course of a career.

Because this pattern sits so close to development work, candidates here may be evaluating opportunities across both the QA Testing and Software Engineering fields simultaneously, and an advisor should be prepared to discuss both framings honestly rather than assuming the candidate has already settled on "QA" as their identity.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern is the collection's clearest bridge to the [Software Engineering Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview), particularly [Backend Specializations](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/backend-specializations), and an advisor should treat that overlap as a real discovery consideration rather than a documentation inconsistency.

## Advisor Guidance

Ask directly whether the candidate identifies primarily with testing or with general development, since this pattern sits at the boundary between two collections. Don't assume the candidate has already settled on one identity over the other.

# Role Checklist

> This article closes the QA Testing Role Collection, the fifth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the QA Testing Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, and Cloud & DevOps collections established.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized the same way as the four preceding collections: working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Manual QA Tester](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/manual-qa-tester), [Automation Test Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/automation-test-engineer), [QA Lead / Test Manager](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/qa-lead-test-manager), and [SDET](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/sdet) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every named tool throughout the collection treated purely as an illustrative example, with no comparison or ranking against another?
- [ ] Does any part of the collection favor or recommend one vendor's product over a competitor's?
- [ ] Does this collection stay clearly distinct from the Software Engineering collection rather than duplicating it, particularly at the SDET boundary?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular credibility-sensitivity rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook?
- [ ] Does each of the four stage guides build directly on its corresponding core sales-stage module rather than introducing a standalone technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Is the collection free of any stated hiring number, salary figure, promised outcome, or employer-specific claim?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, and Cloud & DevOps collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its overlap-avoidance requirement with the Software Engineering collection.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the four preceding collections' checklists to confirm all five Role Collections remain structurally consistent.

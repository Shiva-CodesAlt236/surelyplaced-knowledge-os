# QA Testing Role Collection — Overview

> This is the fifth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, following Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, and Cloud & DevOps.

## Purpose

This article introduces the QA Testing Role Collection, mapped to `ROLE-QA` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and to the [QA & Testing Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/qa-testing-playbook). It orients an advisor to this collection's four working patterns and shows how this collection fits alongside the collections already published.

## Scope

This collection covers candidates whose work centers on verifying software quality — from manual, exploratory testing through fully automated, code-driven test engineering. It does not cover general software development; where a candidate's background sits closer to building features than testing them, [Software Engineering Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview) is the better fit.

## Core Content

This collection documents four working patterns within `ROLE-QA`: [Manual QA Tester](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/manual-qa-tester), [Automation Test Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/automation-test-engineer), [QA Lead / Test Manager](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/qa-lead-test-manager), and [SDET](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/sdet). It then covers the ecosystem of representative tools at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/technology-ecosystem), this role family's sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds directly on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), the [QA & Testing Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/qa-testing-playbook), and the structural pattern established by the [Software Engineering](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview), [Data & AI](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/overview), [Cybersecurity](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/overview), and [Cloud & DevOps](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/overview) collections.

## Advisor Guidance

Start here before opening any single working-pattern article in this collection. Where a candidate's background blends testing and development work closely, cross-check this collection against [SDET](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/sdet) and the Software Engineering collection rather than assuming one framing covers everything.

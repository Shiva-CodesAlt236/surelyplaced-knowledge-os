# Advisor Coaching

> The example note further below is invented for illustration and clearly labeled as such — no real advisor, candidate, or call is being described.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for `ROLE-QA`, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with QA Testing candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor's language at any point implied testing is a lesser or transitional path, consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) when such language went unnoticed or uncorrected; whether the advisor accurately distinguished the candidate's specific working pattern rather than defaulting to a generic "QA" framing, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/candidate-behaviour); and whether an open skill-direction question, when raised, was actually addressed rather than left unresolved through to closing.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — named the candidate's automation specialization precisely rather than defaulting to generic QA language, visibly building trust early in the call"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches should prioritize careful language review for anything implying testing is a lesser path, accurate working-pattern distinction, and full resolution of skill-direction questions when reviewing calls with this role family, alongside the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

# QA Lead / Test Manager

## Purpose

This article describes the QA Lead / Test Manager working pattern within `ROLE-QA` — candidates whose focus has shifted from individual test execution toward quality strategy and team coordination.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a leadership-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms of test strategy, process ownership, and coordinating a team's quality efforts across a project rather than executing individual test cases themselves day to day. This pattern most often represents a later career stage, typically reached by candidates who started as a manual tester or automation engineer and grew into a role responsible for how a whole team approaches quality, not just their own individual output.

Because this pattern sits closer to organizational and process decisions, candidates here often evaluate a career move partly on scope of responsibility and influence over how quality is approached, not on hands-on technical fit alone — a consideration that echoes what senior candidates in other collections have described, but applied specifically to quality process rather than infrastructure or system design.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern represents the most senior end of the spectrum the [QA & Testing Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/qa-testing-playbook) describes only in general terms, and its scope-and-influence consideration parallels [Cloud Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/cloud-architect) in the Cloud & DevOps collection, though grounded in quality strategy rather than infrastructure design.

## Advisor Guidance

Ask about scope of responsibility and process ownership, not just individual technical skill, when discovery touches this pattern. Recognize that a candidate here may be weighing a move on influence and team-level impact as much as on the specific role's day-to-day tasks.

## Key Takeaways

QA Leads and Test Managers operate at a strategy-and-coordination level above the other three working patterns in this collection, typically reach this pattern from within the same role family, and may weigh a career move partly on scope and process influence rather than hands-on technical fit alone.

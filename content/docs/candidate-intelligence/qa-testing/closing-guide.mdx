# Closing Guide

> This guide includes no figure, discount, or promised outcome anywhere — closing technique only.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to `ROLE-QA`, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for QA Testing candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before moving to a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), confirm explicitly that any credibility concern raised earlier — the most common underlying hesitation for this population, per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/objection-guide) — has been genuinely resolved, and that the candidate feels their specific specialization has been understood accurately throughout the conversation.

Ask for the commitment directly, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and if a skill-direction question was raised earlier and left partly open, revisit it briefly before closing so the candidate isn't deciding with that question still unresolved in the background.

Follow [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) for the payment and agreement step itself. Given this pattern's wide range of entry backgrounds, per [Manual QA Tester](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/manual-qa-tester), stay alert to financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) the same way any other role family would warrant.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's credibility-sensitivity findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm the candidate feels genuinely understood before closing, revisit any unresolved skill-direction question briefly rather than leaving it hanging, and stay alert to financial vulnerability given this population's wide range of entry backgrounds.

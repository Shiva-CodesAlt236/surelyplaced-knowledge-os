# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to `ROLE-QA`, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for QA Testing candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Open by asking the candidate to describe their specific mix of manual and automation work in their own words, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/candidate-behaviour) — this signals from the first exchange that the advisor understands QA isn't one undifferentiated category, which matters more to this population than to most.

Consistent with [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), be conscious of language throughout the call that could imply testing is a lesser or transitional path — even an offhand phrase can undercut trust with a candidate who has encountered that framing before.

When exploring goals per [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), ask directly whether the candidate is weighing a skill-development question — such as a manual tester considering automation — since per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/buying-patterns), this is a genuine open direction question for many candidates, not a settled preference. For candidates whose background leans toward [SDET](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/sdet), ask explicitly whether they identify more with testing or general development, since that identity question shapes the rest of the conversation.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's credibility-sensitivity findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Signal understanding of the field's internal range early, watch language carefully for anything that could imply testing is a lesser path, and surface open skill-direction questions directly rather than assuming a candidate has already decided.

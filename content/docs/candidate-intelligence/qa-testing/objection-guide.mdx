# Objection Guide

> This guide names no real conversation, and no price, discount, or guaranteed result appears anywhere in it.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Objection Handling module to `ROLE-QA`, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers objection-handling approach for QA Testing candidates broadly, building on [Trust and Credibility](/docs/objections/trust-and-credibility) and [Already Applying Myself](/docs/objections/already-applying-myself).

## Core Content

Per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/candidate-behaviour), a candidate who has previously felt underestimated may raise a sharper-than-usual version of [Trust and Credibility](/docs/objections/trust-and-credibility) — sometimes prompted by a single word choice earlier in the conversation rather than a fundamental doubt about the service itself. Recognize this pattern for what it is and address the specific concern directly rather than treating it as a generic trust objection.

A version of [Already Applying Myself](/docs/objections/already-applying-myself) is also common, often connected to the open skill-direction question described in [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/buying-patterns) — a candidate weighing whether to build new skills independently before deciding a program is worth it. Respond by helping clarify that direction question rather than only defending the value of the program.

Watch for a quieter signal specific to this population: a candidate who feels their specific working pattern was mischaracterized earlier in the call may raise an objection that sounds unrelated to that mischaracterization on the surface. Checking in directly — confirming the recommendation reflects their actual specialization — can surface and resolve this before it hardens into a harder objection.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Trust and Credibility](/docs/objections/trust-and-credibility) and [Already Applying Myself](/docs/objections/already-applying-myself), shaped by this collection's credibility-sensitivity findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Recognize a sharper trust objection as a possible response to an earlier mischaracterization, help clarify open skill-direction questions rather than only defending program value, and confirm the recommendation reflects the candidate's actual specialization when an objection seems disconnected from its likely root cause.

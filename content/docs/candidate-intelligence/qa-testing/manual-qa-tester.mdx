# Manual QA Tester

## Purpose

This article describes the Manual QA Tester working pattern within `ROLE-QA` — candidates whose testing work is exploratory and functional rather than driven by a code-based automation framework.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a manual-testing-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms of exploring an application directly — designing test cases, running through user flows, documenting defects, and thinking critically about how a system might break in ways an automated script wouldn't catch on its own. Many entered QA as their first technical role, and this pattern often represents the widest range of prior backgrounds of any pattern in this collection, including candidates who transitioned from a non-technical field entirely.

What distinguishes this pattern most clearly from [Automation Test Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/automation-test-engineer) is that the work itself doesn't center on writing code, even though a manual tester may understand testing concepts just as deeply. This distinction matters a great deal to candidates in this pattern, many of whom have encountered a perception that manual testing is a lesser or purely entry-level skill — a perception this collection's sales intelligence addresses directly.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern maps to the general QA population described in the [QA & Testing Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/qa-testing-playbook), giving specific shape to its "manual testing" reference and grounding the playbook's underestimation theme in a concrete working pattern.

## Advisor Guidance

Treat this pattern's work as a genuine technical specialization from the first sentence of a conversation — never imply, even subtly, that manual testing is a stepping stone to "real" engineering work unless the candidate has stated that as their own goal.

## Key Takeaways

Manual QA Testers focus on exploratory and functional testing without a code-based framework, often arrive from a wide range of prior backgrounds, and are best engaged by treating their work as a genuine technical specialization rather than a stepping stone to something else.

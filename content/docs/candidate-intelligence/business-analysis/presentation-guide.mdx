# Presentation Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discussion module to `ROLE-BA`, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers presentation-call approach for Business Analysis candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process).

## Core Content

Structure the presentation itself clearly — an organized, logically sequenced walkthrough tends to land better with this population than a looser conversational style, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/candidate-behaviour). Name the candidate's specific working pattern explicitly rather than defaulting to a generic "business analyst" framing.

When explaining the process per [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), be prepared for detailed follow-up questions — this population's professional habit of understanding fully before deciding often surfaces as thorough, specific questions rather than passive listening, and that should be read as genuine engagement rather than resistance.

For candidates whose pattern leans toward [Product & Stakeholder Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/product-stakeholder-business-analyst), address how the recommendation supports movement toward product-focused work if that direction came up during discovery.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), shaped by this collection's structured-evaluation findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Present in a clearly organized sequence, expect and welcome detailed follow-up questions as genuine engagement, and address longer-term product direction where it was raised during discovery.

# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to `ROLE-BA`, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Business Analysis candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Open with a structured, organized approach to the call itself, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/candidate-behaviour) — this population notices and responds to a well-organized conversation more than most, and a disorganized opening can cost credibility early.

Consistent with [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), ask directly where the candidate sits on the technical-to-stakeholder-facing spectrum this collection is organized around, rather than assuming a fixed meaning for "business analyst." For candidates whose background leans toward [Data & Reporting Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/data-and-reporting-business-analyst), clarify whether they define reporting requirements or build the underlying data work, since the two are easy to conflate.

Give this population time to think through their answers rather than pushing for quick responses — consistent with [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), rushing a population whose professional habit is thorough evaluation tends to work against building trust rather than for it.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's structured-evaluation findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Structure the call itself deliberately, confirm where the candidate sits on the technical-to-stakeholder-facing spectrum early, and give this population room to think rather than rushing toward quick answers.

## Key Takeaways

Discovery with this role family works best when the call itself is deliberately organized, the candidate's specific position on the technical-to-stakeholder-facing spectrum is confirmed early, and the candidate is given room to evaluate thoroughly rather than rushed toward a quick answer.

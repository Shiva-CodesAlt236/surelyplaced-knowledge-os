# Business Analysis Role Collection — Overview

> This is the sixth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, following Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, and QA Testing.

## Purpose

This article introduces the Business Analysis Role Collection, mapped to `ROLE-BA` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and to the [Business Analyst Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/business-analyst-playbook). It walks an advisor through this collection's four working patterns and situates the collection among the ones already delivered.

## Scope

This collection covers candidates whose work sits at the intersection of business needs and technical or operational systems, spanning a spectrum from technically-oriented requirements work through purely stakeholder-facing process design. It does not cover general software development, data engineering, or project delivery management — where a candidate's background sits closer to one of those, the [Software Engineering](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview) or [Data & AI](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/overview) collections, or a future Project Management collection, may be the better fit.

## Core Content

This collection documents four working patterns within `ROLE-BA`, arranged along the field's technical-to-stakeholder-facing spectrum: [Technical/Systems Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/technical-systems-business-analyst), [Process & Operations Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/process-operations-business-analyst), [Data & Reporting Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/data-and-reporting-business-analyst), and [Product & Stakeholder Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/product-stakeholder-business-analyst). It then covers the ecosystem of representative tools at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/technology-ecosystem), this role family's sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds directly on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), the [Business Analyst Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/business-analyst-playbook), and the structural pattern established by the [Software Engineering](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview), [Data & AI](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/overview), [Cybersecurity](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/overview), [Cloud & DevOps](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/overview), and [QA Testing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/overview) collections.

## Advisor Guidance

Read this overview before any single working-pattern article in the collection. Because "business analyst" varies more by employer than most role families, confirming where a candidate actually sits on the technical-to-stakeholder-facing spectrum matters more here than in most other collections.

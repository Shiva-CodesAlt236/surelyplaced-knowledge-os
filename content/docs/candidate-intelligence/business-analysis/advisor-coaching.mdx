# Advisor Coaching

> The illustration in this article is invented for training purposes and clearly marked as such — it does not describe any actual advisor, candidate, or conversation.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for `ROLE-BA`, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with Business Analysis candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor kept the call structured and organized throughout, consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) when disorganization went unnoticed or uncorrected; whether the advisor correctly identified and named the candidate's specific working pattern rather than defaulting to a generic "business analyst" framing, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/candidate-behaviour); and whether a genuine [Need Time to Think](/docs/objections/need-time-to-think) response was met with specific, concrete follow-up rather than pressure or generic reassurance.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — restructured a rambling explanation mid-call into a clear, organized summary after noticing the candidate's attention shift"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article maps [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) and [Reading a Sales Conversation](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation) onto the specific risk patterns this collection identified for Business Analysis.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches should prioritize structure and organization throughout the call, accurate working-pattern identification, and genuine follow-through on requests for time to think, alongside the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

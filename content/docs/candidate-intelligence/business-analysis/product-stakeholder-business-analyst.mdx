# Product & Stakeholder Business Analyst

## Purpose

This article describes the Product & Stakeholder Business Analyst working pattern within `ROLE-BA` — candidates whose primary skill is eliciting requirements from stakeholders and translating them into specifications a product or delivery team can act on.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a stakeholder-facing candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms of stakeholder interviews, requirements workshops, and managing the inevitable tension between what different stakeholders say they want. Much of the skill in this pattern is interpersonal and communication-based rather than technical — spotting when two stakeholders are describing the same need differently, or the reverse, and reconciling that before it becomes a costly misunderstanding downstream.

This pattern often sits closest to product management work of any pattern in this collection, and candidates here may be evaluating a career move partly on whether it leads toward a product-management-style role over time — a consideration worth surfacing directly rather than assuming this pattern's ambitions stop at business analysis.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern represents the collection's clearest bridge toward product and delivery-focused roles, a boundary the [Business Analyst Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/business-analyst-playbook) references only in general terms.

## Advisor Guidance

Ask whether a candidate sees business analysis as their long-term direction or as a step toward a more product-focused role, since that ambition can meaningfully shape what they're looking for in a next opportunity.

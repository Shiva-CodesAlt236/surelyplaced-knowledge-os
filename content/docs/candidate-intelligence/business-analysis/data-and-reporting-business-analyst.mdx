# Data & Reporting Business Analyst

## Purpose

This article describes the Data & Reporting Business Analyst working pattern within `ROLE-BA` — candidates who define what a business needs to measure and report on, rather than building the underlying data infrastructure themselves.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a data-informed candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms of defining metrics, specifying reporting requirements, and translating a business question into something a dashboard or report can actually answer. This is a meaningfully different discipline from building the data pipelines or models that power those reports — the distinction matters enough that this article draws it explicitly, since it's easy to conflate this pattern with a Data & AI working pattern on first impression.

Where a [Data Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-analyst) in the Data & AI collection typically builds and runs the analysis itself, this pattern more often defines what needs to be measured and hands that specification to a data or engineering team to implement — a distinction worth confirming directly in discovery rather than assuming from a job title alone.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits adjacent to, but distinct from, the [Data & AI Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/overview), and an advisor should treat that adjacency as a discovery question to clarify rather than a documentation inconsistency.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm directly whether a candidate defines reporting requirements or builds the underlying data work themselves — the two are easy to conflate and the distinction meaningfully affects which role framing fits.

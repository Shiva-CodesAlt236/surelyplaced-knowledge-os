# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Business Analysis Role Collection, the sixth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Business Analysis Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, and QA Testing collections established.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized the same way as the five preceding collections: working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Technical/Systems Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/technical-systems-business-analyst), [Process & Operations Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/process-operations-business-analyst), [Data & Reporting Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/data-and-reporting-business-analyst), and [Product & Stakeholder Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/product-stakeholder-business-analyst) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every tool named across the collection presented purely as an illustrative example, free of comparison or ranking against another?
- [ ] Does any article slip into favoring one named product over a competing one?
- [ ] Does this collection stay clearly distinct from the Data & AI collection rather than duplicating it, particularly at the Data & Reporting Business Analyst boundary?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular structured-evaluation mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook?
- [ ] Does each of the four stage guides extend its matching core sales-stage module rather than inventing its own technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Does the collection avoid stating any hiring number, salary figure, promised outcome, or employer-specific claim?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, and QA Testing collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its overlap-avoidance requirement with the Data & AI collection.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the five preceding collections' checklists to confirm all six Role Collections remain structurally consistent.

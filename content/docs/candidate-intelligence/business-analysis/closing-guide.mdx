# Closing Guide

> Nothing in this guide states a figure, discount, or promised outcome — it covers closing technique only.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to `ROLE-BA`, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for Business Analysis candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before asking for a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), summarize the conversation in the same structured way this population would summarize their own analysis — a clear recap of what was discussed and why the recommendation follows from it tends to land better here than an emotional or urgency-driven close.

If [Need Time to Think](/docs/objections/need-time-to-think) surfaced earlier, confirm what specific information, if any, is still needed before revisiting [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment) — per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/objection-guide), this population's hesitation is usually structural rather than a signal the opportunity is wrong.

Handle payment and agreement per [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), and stay alert to financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) the same way any other role family would warrant.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's structured-evaluation findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Close with a clear, structured summary rather than urgency, confirm what specific information a candidate still needs before asking again for commitment, and stay alert to financial vulnerability as with any role family.

## Key Takeaways

Closing across this role family means summarizing clearly and structurally rather than leaning on urgency, confirming what specific information a hesitant candidate still needs, and treating hesitation as this population's structural evaluation process rather than a sign the opportunity is wrong.

# Scrum Master / Agile Delivery Lead

## Purpose

This article describes the Scrum Master / Agile Delivery Lead working pattern within `ROLE-PM` — candidates focused on facilitating a team's agile process and removing whatever is blocking its progress, rather than owning a project plan directly.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a facilitation-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms of team dynamics, process facilitation, and clearing obstacles rather than authoring a traditional project plan — the distinction from [IT Project Manager](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/it-project-manager) is real, even though the two patterns are sometimes conflated by employers unfamiliar with the difference. This pattern's success is measured by how well a team functions and delivers, not by a single document or timeline.

Many candidates in this pattern came from a different technical role originally — sometimes engineering, sometimes QA — and moved into this facilitation-focused path over time, which can make their technical vocabulary more current than a traditional project manager's, even though their day-to-day work now centers on people and process.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern is a distinct facilitation-focused variant the [Project Manager Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/project-manager-playbook) doesn't separate out explicitly, and it shares some entry-path characteristics with [Automation Test Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/qa-testing/automation-test-engineer) in the QA Testing collection, where candidates similarly transition in from an adjacent technical role.

## Advisor Guidance

Don't conflate this pattern with a traditional project manager — ask specifically whether the candidate's role centers on team facilitation or plan ownership, since employers frequently blur the two and a mismatched framing can undercut a candidate's actual strengths.

## Key Takeaways

Scrum Masters and Agile Delivery Leads facilitate a team's process and remove blockers rather than owning a project plan directly, are frequently and inaccurately conflated with traditional project managers, and often bring current technical vocabulary from an earlier role in engineering or QA.

# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Project Management Role Collection, the seventh complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Project Management Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, QA Testing, and Business Analysis collections established.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized the same way as the six preceding collections: working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Technical Project Coordinator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/technical-project-coordinator), [IT Project Manager](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/it-project-manager), [Scrum Master / Agile Delivery Lead](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/scrum-master-agile-delivery-lead), and [Program Manager](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/program-manager) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product or methodology?
- [ ] Is every named tool in the collection treated strictly as an illustrative example, without comparison or ranking?
- [ ] Is any named product favored or promoted over a competing one anywhere in the collection?
- [ ] Does this collection stay clearly distinct from the Business Analysis collection rather than duplicating it?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular plan-and-risk-transparency mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook?
- [ ] Does each stage guide build on its corresponding core sales-stage module rather than introducing new technique of its own?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Is the collection free of any hiring figure, salary number, promised result, or employer-specific claim?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, QA Testing, and Business Analysis collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its overlap-avoidance requirement with the Business Analysis collection.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the six preceding collections' checklists to confirm all seven Role Collections remain structurally consistent.

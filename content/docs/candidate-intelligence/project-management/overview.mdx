# Project Management Role Collection — Overview

> This is the seventh complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, following Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, QA Testing, and Business Analysis.

## Purpose

This article opens the Project Management Role Collection, mapped to `ROLE-PM` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and to the [Project Manager Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/project-manager-playbook). It sets up the four working patterns that follow and shows how this collection sits alongside what's already published.

## Scope

This collection covers candidates responsible for planning and delivering work through other people — coordinating scope, timeline, risk, and stakeholders rather than producing the underlying technical output themselves. It runs from entry-level coordination through senior, portfolio-level program leadership. It does not cover the Business Analysis field's requirements-and-translation focus, even though the two frequently work side by side on the same initiative.

## Core Content

Four working patterns make up this collection, running roughly from earlier-career coordination through senior program leadership: [Technical Project Coordinator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/technical-project-coordinator), [IT Project Manager](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/it-project-manager), [Scrum Master / Agile Delivery Lead](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/scrum-master-agile-delivery-lead), and [Program Manager](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/program-manager). From there, the collection covers representative tools at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/technology-ecosystem), sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), the [Project Manager Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/project-manager-playbook), and the fourteen-article pattern already established across the six collections before it.

## Advisor Guidance

Open with this article before moving into any single working pattern. Where a candidate's work overlaps meaningfully with the [Business Analysis Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/overview) — common on cross-functional initiatives — treat that overlap as a real discovery question rather than a documentation gap.

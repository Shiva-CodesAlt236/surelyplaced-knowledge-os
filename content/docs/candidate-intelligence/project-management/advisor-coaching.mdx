# Advisor Coaching

> The example below is a fabricated illustration, explicitly labeled — it does not reflect any real advisor, candidate, or call.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for `ROLE-PM`, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with Project Management candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor communicated risk and dependencies honestly rather than only the happy path, consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) when a vague or overly optimistic statement went uncorrected; whether the advisor avoided manufactured urgency entirely, given how quickly this population notices and penalizes it; and whether the candidate's specific working pattern — coordinator, project manager, Scrum Master, or program manager — was correctly identified rather than assumed from title alone.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — named an open risk directly rather than smoothing it over, which visibly increased the candidate's engagement in the rest of the call"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

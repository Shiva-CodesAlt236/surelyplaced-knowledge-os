# Closing Guide

> This guide addresses closing technique only — no figure, discount, or promised outcome appears anywhere in it.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to `ROLE-PM`, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for Project Management candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Close the way this population would close out a project milestone, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/candidate-behaviour) — a clear recap of what's been agreed, what happens next, and by when, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment).

Before asking for commitment, confirm any risk-management question raised earlier — the most common underlying concern for this population, per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/objection-guide) — was genuinely addressed, not just acknowledged. Never manufacture urgency at this stage; consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), a specific, honest next step works better with this population than pressure.

Follow [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) for the payment and agreement step, watching for financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) as with any role family.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's plan-and-risk-transparency findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Close with a clear recap of what's agreed and what happens next, confirm any risk-management concern was genuinely resolved, and never lean on manufactured urgency with this population.

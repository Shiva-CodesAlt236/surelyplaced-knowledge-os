# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to `ROLE-PM`, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Project Management candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Set expectations early and honestly about how the call will run, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/candidate-behaviour) — telegraphing the shape of the conversation mirrors how this population would open a project kickoff, and skipping that step can read as disorganized to a population that notices exactly that.

Consistent with [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), ask directly where a candidate sits across the collection's coordination-to-program spectrum rather than assuming from the title "project manager" alone. For candidates whose pattern leans toward [Scrum Master / Agile Delivery Lead](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/scrum-master-agile-delivery-lead), confirm whether their role centers on facilitation or plan ownership, since the two get conflated often.

Ask for a specific project example, including one where something didn't go as planned — per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/project-management/buying-patterns), this population trusts a conversation more once it's clear risk and setbacks can be discussed honestly rather than only success stories.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's plan-and-risk-transparency findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Telegraph the shape of the call early, confirm the candidate's specific position on the coordination-to-program spectrum, and ask for an example that includes a real setback, not just a success story.

## Key Takeaways

Discovery with this role family works best when the call's shape is set early, the candidate's specific position on the field's range is confirmed directly, and discussion of a real project setback is invited rather than avoided.

# Coaching Signals

> This article defines a taxonomy of coaching-relevant observation codes. It is not a performance evaluation or disciplinary record — it flags specific moments worth reviewing, consistent with the constructive, improvement-oriented framing in Sales Coaching. It contains no real or illustrative-but-realistic candidate data.

## Purpose

[Buyer Persona Schema](/docs/candidate-intelligence/BUYER_PERSONA_SCHEMA) records candidate-side buying patterns. This article covers the complementary advisor-side observations: specific moments in a candidate's journey worth flagging for coaching review, using the four-layer reading framework and mistake catalog already established in Sales Coaching.

## Scope

This taxonomy records specific, reviewable moments — not a general performance rating. It exists to make [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework) and [Call Self-Review Checklist](/docs/sales-coaching/call-self-review-checklist) easier to apply by flagging exactly where in a candidate's journey a moment worth reviewing occurred.

## Standard Structure

`coaching_notes` accepts one or more coded flags, each optionally paired with a brief, factual note about what happened — never a general judgment about the advisor's overall skill.

## Required Fields

The defined coaching signal codes:

| Code | Signal | Grounded In |
|---|---|---|
| `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` | A candidate signal was likely missed or misread | [Reading a Sales Conversation](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation) |
| `COACH-GAP-FILLED` | An honest gap may have been filled with a guess rather than escalated | [Common Sales Mistakes](/docs/sales-coaching/common-sales-mistakes) |
| `COACH-SCRIPT-OVER-DIAGNOSIS` | A rehearsed response may have substituted for genuine diagnosis | [Common Sales Mistakes](/docs/sales-coaching/common-sales-mistakes) |
| `COACH-STRONG-MOMENT` | A moment worth highlighting as a positive example | [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework) |
| `COACH-REVIEW-REQUESTED` | The advisor has proactively flagged this conversation for review | [Call Self-Review Checklist](/docs/sales-coaching/call-self-review-checklist) |

## Relationships with Other Modules

Every code above points directly to a concept already defined in Sales Coaching — this schema doesn't introduce new coaching theory, only a way to flag where in a specific candidate's journey one of those existing concepts applies. A profile with coaching signals attached should be read alongside the relevant Sales Coaching article, not instead of it.

## Author Guidelines

Use these codes to flag specific, reviewable moments, not to render a general judgment about an advisor's overall competence. Pair each code with a brief, factual note describing what happened, written the way [Candidate Notes Standards](/docs/sales-operations/candidate-notes-standards) already requires — specific and honest, not vague. Include positive moments (`COACH-STRONG-MOMENT`) as readily as areas for growth, consistent with the constructive framing throughout Sales Coaching.

## Reviewer Checklist

- [ ] Is each coaching signal tied to a specific, describable moment rather than a general impression?
- [ ] Does each note stay factual and specific, avoiding vague language?
- [ ] Are positive moments recorded as readily as growth areas, keeping the tone constructive rather than only corrective?
- [ ] Does this field avoid functioning as an informal disciplinary record outside the coaching framework it's meant to support?

# Candidate Behaviour

> This article describes general behavioral tendencies across a broad role family, not a fixed profile. Individual candidates may differ, and this article should never substitute for genuine discovery conversation.

## Purpose

This article describes general behavioral tendencies across the `ROLE-DATA` family — data analysts, data engineers, machine learning engineers, and AI engineers — so an advisor can recognize common threads across this diverse role family without treating any one pattern as representative of all four.

## Scope

This article covers general behavioral tendencies across Data & AI candidates as a role family. It does not describe any real or specific candidate, and these are tendencies worth watching for, not fixed rules that apply universally.

## Core Content

Across all four patterns, an evidence-seeking, comparison-driven evaluation style is common, consistent with the `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` pattern in [Buyer Persona Schema](/docs/candidate-intelligence/BUYER_PERSONA_SCHEMA) — but the specific flavor of that skepticism differs by pattern. Data analysts often express it through a demand for clear, well-substantiated explanation; data engineers and machine learning engineers often express it through specific, systems-level verification questions closer to the pattern seen in the Software Engineering collection; AI engineers, given the field's newness, sometimes express it through genuine uncertainty about their own readiness rather than confident skepticism.

Career stage shapes this pattern the same way it does across other role families — a candidate earlier in their career, consistent with the Sprint 12 reference profile's general framing, may combine evidence-seeking tendencies with more visible hesitation navigating an unfamiliar major decision, while a more experienced candidate may apply the same tendencies with more confidence.

Visa category introduces urgency largely independent of which Data & AI pattern a candidate works in — an F-1 OPT data analyst and an H-1B machine learning engineer face meaningfully different timeline pressures despite both falling within `ROLE-DATA`. Consult the candidate's specific Visa Playbook rather than assuming urgency from role classification.

## Advisor Guidance

Expect evidence-seeking behavior broadly, but calibrate for which of the four patterns a candidate matches — an analyst's demand for clear explanation differs meaningfully from an engineer's demand for systems-level verification. Always confirm visa category directly rather than inferring urgency from role.

## Key Takeaways

Data & AI candidates broadly tend toward evidence-seeking evaluation, but the specific flavor varies by pattern — analysts seek clear explanation, engineers seek systems-level verification, and AI engineers may show more genuine uncertainty given the field's newness — while career stage and visa category shape the presentation independently of role.

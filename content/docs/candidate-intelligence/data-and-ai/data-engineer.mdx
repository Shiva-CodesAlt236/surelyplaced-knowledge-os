# Data Engineer

> This article discusses data engineering only at the ecosystem level. Any platform named below is an illustrative example, not a ranking or exhaustive list — actual infrastructure choices vary considerably by employer.

## Purpose

This article describes what generally distinguishes the data engineer working pattern within `ROLE-DATA`, complementing [Data Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-analyst) with the infrastructure side of data work.

## Scope

This article covers data engineering as a category of working pattern within `ROLE-DATA`. It does not evaluate any specific pipeline, warehousing, or orchestration platform technically, and makes no claim about which infrastructure choice is more valuable to a given employer.

## Core Content

Data engineering generally centers on building and maintaining the infrastructure that moves and stores data reliably — pipelines, warehouses, and the systems that make data usable for analysts and other downstream consumers, rather than the interpretation work covered in [Data Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-analyst).

At the ecosystem level, this pattern commonly involves a data warehousing or storage platform, some form of pipeline or workflow orchestration, and often cloud infrastructure exposure — but which specific combination a candidate has used varies enormously by employer and should never be assumed from the title alone.

Data engineers often bring a systems-reliability mindset closer to the backend engineering pattern described in the Software Engineering collection than to the analyst pattern — favoring specific, verifiable claims about how something actually works over general reassurance, consistent with the evidence-seeking tendency described in [Data and AI Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/data-and-ai-playbook).

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article extends [Data and AI Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/data-and-ai-playbook) and [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING) to the data engineer pattern, and draws a deliberate parallel to [Backend Specializations](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/backend-specializations) in the Software Engineering collection without repeating its content.

## Advisor Guidance

Record a candidate's stated infrastructure focus in `technology_summary` using their own words, and route genuine technical evaluation to internal SMEs. Recognize this pattern's closer kinship to backend engineering's reliability-oriented mindset than to the analyst pattern's communication-oriented one.

Data engineer candidates generally focus on building reliable data infrastructure rather than interpreting data directly, bringing a systems-reliability mindset closer to backend engineering than to data analysis — but specific platforms vary by employer and should never be assumed from the title alone.

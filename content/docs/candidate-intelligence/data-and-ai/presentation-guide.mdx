# Presentation Guide

> This guide covers presentation-stage advisor approach across the Data & AI role family. It states no specific price, statistic, or guarantee.

## Purpose

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation) specifically to the `ROLE-DATA` family.

## Scope

This guide covers how to present Surely Placed's value to Data & AI candidates broadly, building on the existing Discussion and Sales Constitution modules rather than restating them.

## Core Content

Present Surely Placed's documented scope honestly and specifically, consistent with [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation), regardless of which of the four working patterns the candidate matches — the evidence-seeking tendency described in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/candidate-behaviour) applies broadly.

Connect the presentation to the specific working pattern and goals the candidate described in discovery, since a generic "data and AI" pitch is less effective across this role family's genuinely diverse patterns than specificity is.

When a candidate asks about return on investment, which [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/buying-patterns) identifies as especially common for this role family, apply [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) with the added insight that transparent acknowledgment of uncertainty often lands particularly well with candidates who work professionally with quantified uncertainty themselves.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide draws directly on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed), [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation), and [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations), interpreted through this collection's own behavior and buying-pattern findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Ground every presentation in the candidate's specific working pattern and stated goals rather than a generic pitch, and use transparent uncertainty acknowledgment as a genuine credibility-building tool with this role family rather than something to avoid.

## Key Takeaways

Presenting to this role family works best through specificity tied to the candidate's actual working pattern, with the ROI Conversations discipline applied through transparent, honest acknowledgment of uncertainty — a style this analytically-minded role family tends to respond well to.

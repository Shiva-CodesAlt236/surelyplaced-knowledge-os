# Advisor Coaching

> This article sets coaching priorities for the Data & AI role family as a whole. Nothing here describes an actual call; every example is hypothetical and labeled as such.

## Purpose

This article generalizes coaching signal usage, established in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS), across the `ROLE-DATA` family's four working patterns.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach or reviewer should prioritize when reviewing calls with Data & AI candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Across this role family, review should prioritize: whether the advisor correctly distinguished the evidence-seeking pattern from genuine disengagement, calibrated to the candidate's specific working pattern per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/candidate-behaviour), consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS); whether the ROI Conversations discipline was applied with genuine transparency about uncertainty rather than avoidance, which [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/buying-patterns) identifies as this role family's particular strength when done well; and, for AI engineer candidates specifically, whether genuine readiness self-doubt was distinguished from an ordinary objection and addressed with direct honesty rather than a standard rebuttal.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note demonstrating format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — addressed AI engineer candidate's readiness uncertainty directly and honestly rather than treating it as a standard objection"]`. This note describes no actual advisor or candidate — it is a formatting example only.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article maps [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) and [Reading a Sales Conversation](/docs/sales-coaching/reading-a-sales-conversation) onto the risk patterns this collection identified for the Data & AI role family specifically.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches reviewing calls with Data & AI candidates should prioritize pattern-calibrated signal reading, transparent ROI-uncertainty discipline, and — for AI engineer candidates specifically — correctly distinguishing readiness self-doubt from a standard objection, in addition to the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

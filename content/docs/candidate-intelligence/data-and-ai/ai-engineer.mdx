# AI Engineer

> This article discusses AI engineering only at the ecosystem level. Any platform named below is an illustrative example, not a ranking or exhaustive list — actual tools and interview expectations vary by employer, and this field changes quickly.

## Purpose

This article completes the four-pattern view of `ROLE-DATA` in this collection, describing what generally distinguishes AI engineer candidates from the machine learning engineering pattern already covered.

## Scope

This article covers AI engineering as a category of working pattern within `ROLE-DATA`. It does not evaluate any specific AI platform, API, or integration approach technically, and takes no position on which approach is more advanced or in demand.

## Core Content

AI engineering generally centers on applying and integrating existing AI capabilities — often pre-trained models or third-party AI platforms — into products and systems, rather than building and training models from the ground up, which is closer to the focus described in [Machine Learning Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/machine-learning-engineer).

At the ecosystem level, this pattern commonly involves integrating with AI platforms or APIs, some familiarity with how large models are prompted or configured for a specific application, and product-oriented engineering skills for building around those capabilities — but the specific tools and depth of experience vary considerably, and this is a fast-changing area where assumptions age quickly.

AI engineers, given how recently this specific working pattern has become common, sometimes have less standardized formal experience to point to than candidates in more established patterns, which can shape how they evaluate their own readiness and how they respond to career guidance — worth keeping in mind without assuming it applies to every individual candidate.

Ask directly about a candidate's specific AI engineering experience rather than assuming standardized background, given how new and fast-moving this pattern is. Record their answer in `technology_summary` using their own words, and route genuine technical evaluation to internal SMEs.

## Key Takeaways

AI engineer candidates generally focus on integrating existing AI capabilities into products rather than building models from scratch, and this fast-moving, relatively new working pattern means formal experience is less standardized than in more established patterns — worth confirming directly rather than assuming from the title alone.

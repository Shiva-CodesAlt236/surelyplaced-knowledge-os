# Discovery Guide

> This guide covers discovery-stage advisor approach across the Data & AI role family. It is guidance, not a script, and assumes no specific candidate situation beyond what's stated directly.

## Purpose

This guide applies the discovery framework already established in [Discovery](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) to the `ROLE-DATA` family specifically, calibrated to its four distinct working patterns.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Data & AI candidates broadly, deferring to the relevant Visa Playbook for visa-specific discovery questions and to [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification) for the general qualification framework this guide builds on.

## Core Content

Open the call consistent with [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), and expect the evidence-seeking tendency described in [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/candidate-behaviour) to appear across all four patterns, though its specific flavor differs.

Confirm which of the four working patterns a candidate matches early — [Data Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-analyst), [Data Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-engineer), [Machine Learning Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/machine-learning-engineer), or [AI Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/ai-engineer) — since these patterns differ meaningfully in mindset and technical focus despite all falling within `ROLE-DATA`.

For AI engineer candidates specifically, be prepared for potentially less standardized formal experience given the field's newness, and ask open, direct questions about their actual background rather than assuming a conventional career trajectory.

Confirm the candidate's visa category directly and route to the corresponding Visa Playbook, since urgency varies independently of which Data & AI pattern the candidate works in, consistent with [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/candidate-behaviour).

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals) directly, generalized using this collection's four working-pattern articles.

## Advisor Guidance

Treat working pattern, visa category, and career stage as three independent dimensions to confirm directly — never inferred from one another or from a job title alone. Be especially open-ended with AI engineer candidates given the pattern's newness.

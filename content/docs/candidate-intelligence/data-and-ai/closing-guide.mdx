# Closing Guide

> This guide covers closing-stage advisor approach across the Data & AI role family. It states no specific price, guarantee, or placement outcome.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to the `ROLE-DATA` family, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for Data & AI candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation).

## Core Content

Confirm genuine readiness before moving to a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) — verify that the evidence-seeking questions typical of this role family, whatever specific form they took for the candidate's working pattern, were actually resolved earlier in the conversation.

Ask for the commitment directly, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), without leaning on visa timeline urgency as a pressure tactic — the specific visa category's timeline is a real factor the candidate is weighing, not a lever to close faster.

If hesitation appears, apply [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation). For AI engineer candidates specifically, distinguish genuine readiness self-doubt, noted in [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/objection-guide), from ordinary closing-stage hesitation — the two call for somewhat different responses, with the former deserving direct, honest reassurance about the legitimacy of their path rather than a standard objection-handling response.

Watch for genuine financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags), more likely for earlier-career candidates per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/buying-patterns), and for a financial co-decision-maker per [Financial Decision-Makers](/docs/pricing/financial-decision-makers).

## Cross-Module Alignment

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm this role family's evidence-seeking questions were genuinely resolved before closing, distinguish AI engineer readiness self-doubt from ordinary hesitation, and never use visa timeline as pressure.

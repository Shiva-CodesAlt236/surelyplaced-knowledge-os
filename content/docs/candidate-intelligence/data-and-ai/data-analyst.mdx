# Data Analyst

> This article discusses data analysis only at the ecosystem level. Any tool named below is an illustrative example of a category, not an endorsement or exhaustive list — actual tools and interview expectations differ by employer.

## Purpose

Data analyst candidates are one of the four common working patterns within the `ROLE-DATA` family. This article describes what generally distinguishes this pattern at the ecosystem level, giving an advisor enough recognition to have an informed conversation.

## Scope

This article covers data analysis as a category of working pattern within `ROLE-DATA`. It does not evaluate any specific reporting, query, or spreadsheet tool technically, and takes no position on which tool is more valuable to a given employer.

## Core Content

Data analysis generally centers on interpreting existing data to answer specific business questions and communicate findings clearly — building reports, dashboards, and summaries that others in an organization act on, rather than building the underlying data infrastructure itself.

At the ecosystem level, this pattern commonly involves querying data from a structured source, some familiarity with spreadsheet or statistical tooling, and increasingly a business-intelligence or visualization layer for presenting findings — but which specific combination a candidate has used, and how deep their experience runs, varies by employer and should never be assumed.

Data analysts often bring a communication-oriented, business-context-aware mindset to how they evaluate a service like Surely Placed, sometimes paying close attention to how clearly a claim is explained and substantiated rather than the claim's surface confidence alone — a pattern consistent with the evidence-seeking tendency described generally in [Data and AI Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/data-and-ai-playbook).

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article extends [Data and AI Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/data-and-ai-playbook) and [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING) specifically to the data analyst pattern, introducing no new technical claim beyond what those articles already establish generally.

## Advisor Guidance

Record a candidate's stated analytical focus in `technology_summary` using their own words per [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING), rather than assuming a specific tool stack from the "data analyst" title alone. Route genuine technical evaluation to internal SMEs, and expect this candidate to value clear, well-substantiated explanation as much as the underlying claim itself.

Data analyst candidates generally focus on interpreting existing data and communicating findings clearly, and tend to value well-substantiated explanation during a sales conversation as much as the claim itself — but the exact tools involved vary by employer and should never be assumed from the title alone.

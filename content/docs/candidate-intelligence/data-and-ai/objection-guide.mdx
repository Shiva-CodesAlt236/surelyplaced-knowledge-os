# Objection Guide

> This guide anticipates the objection patterns most likely across the Data & AI role family specifically. No real conversation is described, and no price, discount, or guarantee is stated anywhere.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Objections and Pricing modules to the likely objection patterns across the `ROLE-DATA` family's four working patterns.

## Scope

This guide covers likely objection patterns for Data & AI candidates broadly, routing each to existing guidance rather than introducing new objection-handling technique.

## Core Content

**Value-uncertainty objections**, frequently framed as a direct request for an ROI figure, are especially common across this role family per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/buying-patterns) — apply [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations), leaning into transparent uncertainty acknowledgment.

**Comparison objections** are common, particularly for AI engineer candidates given how crowded and fast-moving that specific space is — apply [Handling Price Objections](/docs/pricing/handling-price-objections) and, where a named alternative comes up, [Already Working With a Consultancy](/docs/objections/already-working-with-a-consultancy).

**Readiness or self-doubt objections** are worth watching for specifically with AI engineer candidates, given the pattern's newness and less standardized formal experience noted in [AI Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/ai-engineer) — this may present less as a classic sales objection and more as genuine uncertainty about their own qualifications, worth addressing with the same honesty and care as any other underlying concern.

**Visa-timeline-driven urgency** varies by specific visa category rather than by Data & AI pattern — route to the matching Visa Playbook rather than a generic response.

## Cross-Module Alignment

Each pattern named here points back to existing Objections, Pricing, or Visa Playbook guidance rather than a new technique invented for this role family.

## Advisor Guidance

Diagnose objection type using [Handling Price Objections](/docs/pricing/handling-price-objections)' framework, and stay alert to readiness-related self-doubt specifically among AI engineer candidates as a distinct pattern from a classic price or comparison objection.

This role family is most likely to raise ROI-figure requests, comparison objections (especially among AI engineers), and occasionally genuine readiness self-doubt rather than a classic sales objection — each routed to existing Objections and Pricing guidance, with visa-timeline urgency handled through the specific candidate's Visa Playbook.

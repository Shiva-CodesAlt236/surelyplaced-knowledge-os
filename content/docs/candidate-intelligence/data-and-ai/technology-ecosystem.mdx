# Technology Ecosystem

> This article stays at the category level — server-side languages, warehousing platforms, and the like — rather than any specific product. Tools sometimes associated with this field, such as TensorFlow, PyTorch, Snowflake, Power BI, Tableau, or Databricks, are not evaluated, ranked, or given standalone treatment anywhere here.

## Purpose

[Data Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-analyst) through [AI Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/ai-engineer) each touch on pattern-specific technology categories. This article covers the categories that cut across all four, consolidating what would otherwise be repeated in each.

## Scope

This article names general technology categories relevant to Data & AI broadly — query and storage systems, statistical and modeling approaches, and cloud infrastructure — without naming or evaluating specific products within each category beyond illustrative mention.

## Core Content

**Query and storage systems** are foundational across nearly all four patterns — some ability to retrieve and work with structured data appears in data analysis, data engineering, and often machine learning work, though the specific systems vary widely.

**Statistical and modeling approaches** range from descriptive analysis common in the data analyst pattern to predictive modeling common in machine learning engineering — the depth and formality of statistical background varies considerably by individual and specific role.

**Cloud infrastructure** exposure is increasingly common across all four patterns, particularly for data engineering and machine learning work, though the specific platform and depth of experience should never be assumed.

**Model evaluation and interpretability practices** — understanding not just how to build something but how to assess whether it's working correctly — are a genuine point of technical evaluation across machine learning and AI engineering specifically, and sit outside what an advisor should assess directly.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article is the cross-cutting companion to the four pattern-specific articles in this collection, paralleling the structural role [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/technology-ecosystem) plays in the Software Engineering collection, while covering genuinely distinct content specific to Data & AI.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this article as general orientation, not an interview-readiness checklist. Route genuine technical assessment, particularly around model evaluation and interpretability, to internal technical SMEs rather than attempting it directly.

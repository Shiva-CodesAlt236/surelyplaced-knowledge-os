# Data & AI Role Collection: Overview

> This is the second Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, following the pattern the Software Engineering collection established. Technologies are named only as ecosystem-level examples; no article in this collection is a dedicated deep dive into a specific tool, framework, or platform.

## Purpose

This collection applies the Role Collection pattern to the `ROLE-DATA` family defined in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION), giving advisors the same depth of guidance for data and AI candidates that the [Software Engineering Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview) already provides for that role family.

## Scope

This collection treats Data & AI as one role family with four common working patterns within it — data analysis, data engineering, machine learning engineering, and AI engineering. It does not create standalone articles for specific tools or platforms; those appear only as illustrative ecosystem-level examples within the broader articles.

## Core Content

This collection contains thirteen articles beyond this overview: four role-pattern articles ([Data Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-analyst), [Data Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-engineer), [Machine Learning Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/machine-learning-engineer), [AI Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/ai-engineer)), one cross-cutting [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/technology-ecosystem) article, two Sales Intelligence articles ([Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/buying-patterns)), four stage-by-stage advisor guides, and closing articles on advisor coaching and a role checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds on [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the Sprint 12 [Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), [Data and AI Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/data-and-ai-playbook), the [Software Engineering Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/overview) as a structural model, and Discovery through Sales Constitution. No new sales technique is introduced anywhere in this collection.

## Advisor Guidance

Use the Reference Profile when a candidate closely matches its specific early-career, F-1 OPT, software engineering archetype. Use this collection when working with a candidate whose role falls within the broader `ROLE-DATA` family — data analysis, data engineering, machine learning, or AI engineering — regardless of visa category or career stage.

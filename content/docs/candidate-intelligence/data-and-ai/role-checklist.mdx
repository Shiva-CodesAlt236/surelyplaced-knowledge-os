# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Data & AI Role Collection, the second complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework. Use it both to verify this collection and to confirm consistency with the Software Engineering collection's template.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Data & AI Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the structural template the Software Engineering collection established.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized the same way as [Role Checklist (Software Engineering)](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/role-checklist): working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Data Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-analyst), [Data Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-engineer), [Machine Learning Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/machine-learning-engineer), and [AI Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/ai-engineer) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone article for TensorFlow, PyTorch, Snowflake, Power BI, Tableau, Databricks, SQL, Python, or any other specific tool?
- [ ] Is each named tool clearly framed as one illustrative example among many, with a note that actual employer expectations differ?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/buying-patterns) differentiate across the four working patterns rather than treating `ROLE-DATA` as uniform?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook rather than assuming a single visa category?
- [ ] Does each guide build on the corresponding stage-specific module rather than inventing its own technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical and illustrative only?
- [ ] Is the collection free of any stated hiring statistic, salary number, guarantee, or claim about what a specific employer expects?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors [Role Checklist (Software Engineering)](/docs/candidate-intelligence/software-engineering/role-checklist) and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied specifically to this collection's four working patterns.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection for accuracy, and compare directly against the Software Engineering collection's equivalent checklist to confirm the two Role Collections remain structurally consistent with each other.

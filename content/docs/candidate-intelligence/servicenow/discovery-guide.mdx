# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to the ServiceNow role family, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for ServiceNow candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Ask the candidate to describe the outcome their work produces, not just their day-to-day tasks, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/candidate-behaviour) — this population reflexively thinks in terms of downstream service impact, and asking about it directly signals genuine understanding of how they see their own work.

Consistent with [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), confirm which of this collection's four working patterns the candidate identifies with — administration, custom build, process design, or architecture — since their goals often track directly with where they sit on that spectrum.

Avoid any narrow technical label, per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/buying-patterns) — this population responds better to being described in terms of the service outcome they enable than a single tool or function.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's service-outcome findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Ask directly about the outcome the candidate's work produces, confirm which working pattern they identify with, and avoid any narrow technical label throughout the conversation.

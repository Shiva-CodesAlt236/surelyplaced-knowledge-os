# ServiceNow Process Consultant

## Purpose

This article describes the ServiceNow Process Consultant working pattern — candidates who design how a service workflow, such as incident or change management, should actually run across an organization before it's configured or built.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a service-process-design-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe running workshops with service-desk and departmental stakeholders, mapping how a request should flow from submission to resolution, and defining service-level expectations before an administrator or developer implements the workflow. Many carry an IT service management or process-improvement background as much as a platform-specific one, and consistently frame their work around the end-to-end service experience a requester or affected user actually has — this outcome-first framing is often the clearest way this pattern distinguishes itself in conversation.

This pattern differs from [ServiceNow Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-administrator) in scope: where an Administrator typically maintains a workflow that's already defined, a Process Consultant typically defines what that workflow should be in the first place, working across departmental boundaries to do so.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the service-process-design point of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the platform-maintenance focus of [ServiceNow Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-administrator) and the custom-build focus of [ServiceNow Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-developer).

## Advisor Guidance

Ask which service workflows the candidate has designed or redesigned, and listen for how they describe the end-to-end experience of the person the workflow serves — that framing is usually central to how this population describes their own value. Avoid reducing this pattern's work to "the ticketing person" in any framing.

## Key Takeaways

ServiceNow Process Consultants design how a service workflow should run across an organization before it's built, carry an IT-service-management or process background, and consistently frame their work around the end-to-end service experience of the person the workflow ultimately serves.

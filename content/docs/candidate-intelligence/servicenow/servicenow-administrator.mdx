# ServiceNow Administrator

## Purpose

This article describes the ServiceNow Administrator working pattern — candidates who keep an organization's platform instance configured and running, managing users, roles, and the core workflow modules day to day.

## Scope

This article is meant to help an advisor recognize a configuration-focused candidate against this collection's other three working patterns, purely for sales-conversation purposes. This article doesn't name a certification path and makes no claim about the prevalence of this pattern.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe managing user access, maintaining catalog items, tuning notification rules, and keeping core workflow modules like incident or change management configured to match how an organization's IT and service teams actually operate. Many entered this pattern from an IT support or systems-administration background, and often describe their value in terms of how smoothly the day-to-day service experience runs for the people relying on it.

This population tends to think in terms of the downstream service outcome their configuration work enables — a well-tuned notification rule or catalog item isn't just a settings change to them, it's a direct improvement to how quickly a request or incident actually gets resolved for someone else in the organization.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the platform-configuration end of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the custom-build focus of [ServiceNow Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-developer) and the process-design focus of [ServiceNow Process Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-process-consultant).

## Advisor Guidance

Ask which core modules the candidate configures most — incident, change, catalog, or another — since this pattern spans a real range. Reflect back the service-outcome framing they use rather than describing their work only in terms of settings and configuration.

# ServiceNow Role Collection — Overview

> This is the twelfth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, and the third of four platform-focused collections generated together, following Salesforce and SAP.

## Purpose

This article opens the ServiceNow Role Collection. No platform-specific Industry Playbook exists yet for ServiceNow, so this collection classifies under `ROLE-PENDING` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and defers broader industry-level guidance to [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework) until a dedicated playbook is scoped. The sections ahead introduce this collection's four working patterns and place it relative to the collections published so far.

## Scope

This collection treats ServiceNow as a single workflow-and-service-management ecosystem a candidate might work within, not a product to be sold, evaluated, or ranked against anything else. The four working patterns that follow reflect how a candidate whose background centers on this platform tends to describe their own work. No market-position claim, hiring-demand figure, or certification recommendation appears anywhere in this collection.

## Core Content

Four working patterns make up this collection: [ServiceNow Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-administrator), [ServiceNow Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-developer), [ServiceNow Process Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-process-consultant), and [ServiceNow Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-architect). From there, the collection covers representative ecosystem tooling at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/technology-ecosystem), sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection extends the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), and [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework), consistent with the fourteen-article pattern used across every collection so far.

## Advisor Guidance

Go through this overview first, ahead of any individual working-pattern article. ServiceNow should stay framed strictly as the candidate's working ecosystem — no comparison to another platform, no hint of market standing, and no certification pushed that the candidate hasn't already mentioned.

# Advisor Coaching

> The coaching example that follows is a constructed illustration only, clearly marked as such, and describes no real advisor, candidate, or conversation.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for the ServiceNow role family, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with ServiceNow candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor framed the opportunity in outcome terms rather than staying at the level of features or logistics — a miss worth flagging under `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) given how central outcome-framing is to this population; whether the advisor stayed strictly platform-neutral throughout, never implying this platform's standing relative to any other; and whether any narrow or reductive labeling of the candidate's work was caught and corrected during the call.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — reframed the role description around the service outcome the candidate's work produces after noticing it had been described too narrowly, which the candidate responded to positively"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches should treat outcome-first framing and strict platform-neutrality as non-negotiable coaching priorities, alongside catching and correcting any reductive labeling of a candidate's work, per the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

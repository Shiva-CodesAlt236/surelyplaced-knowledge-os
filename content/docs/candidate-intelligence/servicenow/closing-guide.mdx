# Closing Guide

> This guide stays limited to closing technique — no figure, discount, promised outcome, or platform comparison appears in it.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to the ServiceNow role family, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for ServiceNow candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before asking for a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), restate the concrete outcome this decision moves the candidate toward — per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/objection-guide), a close that stays at the level of logistics without naming that outcome can feel incomplete to this population.

Ground the close in the candidate's specific working pattern and their described service-outcome framing, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), rather than a generic close that could apply to any candidate.

Use [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) to guide this step, staying watchful for financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) exactly as this framework expects across every role family.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's service-outcome findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Restate the concrete outcome the decision moves the candidate toward, ground the close in their specific working pattern, and stay alert to financial vulnerability as with any role family.

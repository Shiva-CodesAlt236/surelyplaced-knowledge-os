# Presentation Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discussion module to the ServiceNow role family, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers presentation-call approach for ServiceNow candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process).

## Core Content

Frame the opportunity around the concrete outcome it offers the candidate, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/candidate-behaviour) — this population evaluates opportunities the same way they evaluate their own work, by what actually changes for the person on the receiving end.

When explaining the process per [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), connect each step to the outcome it moves the candidate toward, rather than describing the process purely as a sequence of logistics.

Watch for any phrasing that might imply a comparison to another platform, per this collection's platform-neutrality requirement — frame the opportunity entirely around the candidate's own working pattern and the role itself.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), shaped by this collection's service-outcome findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Frame the opportunity around a concrete outcome, connect each process step to that outcome, and keep the conversation strictly platform-neutral throughout.

# ServiceNow Architect

## Purpose

This article describes the ServiceNow Architect working pattern — candidates responsible for the overall governance and structure of a platform implementation, spanning multiple workflow modules and business units.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a senior, governance-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only. It names no specific certification level.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe setting technical direction and governance standards across a platform implementation rather than owning a single module or workflow — decisions about instance architecture, module integration, and how new workflow requests are prioritized and approved organization-wide. Most carry substantial prior experience in one or more of this collection's other three patterns before moving into architecture, and often describe the shift as one from delivering a specific workflow outcome to being accountable for the consistency of service outcomes across the whole platform.

This population tends to speak in terms of platform-wide service consistency and long-term governance rather than a single project's deliverables, and often has direct experience reviewing the work of administrators, developers, and process consultants on the same implementation.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the senior end of the spectrum this collection documents, drawing on the platform-configuration, custom-build, and service-process-design focuses of the other three working patterns without being defined by any single one of them.

## Advisor Guidance

Ask which of the other three patterns came before the candidate's move into architecture, since this population almost always brings direct experience in at least one. Center discovery on platform-wide governance and service consistency rather than a single technical task, since that's how this population tends to describe its own value.

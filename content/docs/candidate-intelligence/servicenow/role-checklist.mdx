# Role Checklist

> This article closes the ServiceNow Role Collection, the twelfth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework and the third of this batch's four platform-focused collections.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the ServiceNow Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the preceding collections established.

## Scope

This checklist walks through the collection's full set of fourteen articles, following the standard organization: working patterns, ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [ServiceNow Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-administrator), [ServiceNow Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-developer), [ServiceNow Process Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-process-consultant), and [ServiceNow Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-architect) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every named tool kept to an illustrative, category-only role, with no ranking or comparison anywhere?
- [ ] Does the collection consistently avoid comparing this platform to a competitor, claiming a market position, or recommending a certification path?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular service-outcome orientation mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/closing-guide) stay platform-neutral throughout?
- [ ] Is every stage guide grounded in its matching core sales-stage module rather than a newly invented technique?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Does the collection avoid hiring-demand claims, salary figures, promised results, and employer-specific statements throughout?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist tracks the pattern set by every prior collection's checklist and by [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), tailored to this collection's four working patterns and this batch's platform-neutrality requirement.

## Advisor Guidance

Apply this checklist whenever this collection comes up for review, checking it side by side with earlier checklists to keep the whole framework structurally aligned.

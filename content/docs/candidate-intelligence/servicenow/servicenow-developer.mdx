# ServiceNow Developer

## Purpose

This article describes the ServiceNow Developer working pattern — candidates who build custom applications and workflows on the platform when an out-of-the-box module doesn't fully cover what a business process needs.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a custom-application-build-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only. It stays at a general, ecosystem level rather than a specific technical deep dive.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe designing and building custom workflow applications, scripting business logic, and extending the platform's core capability to automate a process that doesn't map cleanly onto a standard module. Many carry a general software-development background applied to this specific workflow ecosystem, and often describe close collaboration with process-focused colleagues who define what a workflow actually needs to accomplish before the build begins.

This population tends to describe their work in terms of the service or workflow outcome the finished application delivers — not just the build itself — reflecting the same outcome-oriented framing common across this collection's other working patterns.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the custom-build end of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the platform-configuration focus of [ServiceNow Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-administrator) and the senior, governance-focused design of [ServiceNow Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/servicenow/servicenow-architect).

## Advisor Guidance

Ask whether the candidate's build work is mostly custom applications, scripted business logic, or both, and how closely they work with process-focused colleagues to define requirements. Reflect back the outcome their build work is meant to deliver, not just the technical build itself.

## Key Takeaways

ServiceNow Developers build custom applications and workflows where out-of-the-box modules aren't sufficient, apply a general development background to this specific workflow ecosystem, and typically frame their build work in terms of the service outcome it ultimately delivers.

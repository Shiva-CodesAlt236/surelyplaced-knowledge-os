# Reference Profile: Career Background

> This article is part of the Gold Standard reference profile — an illustrative archetype, never a real person. No named employer, university, salary, or dated event is stated anywhere below; the background is deliberately generic.

## Purpose

Every completed profile needs a Layer 1 background section giving an advisor quick orientation before a call. This article demonstrates how to write that section: specific enough to be useful, generic enough to avoid inventing details a real candidate would need to confirm themselves.

## Layer 1 — Candidate Identity

The reference candidate completed a degree in a computer-science-adjacent field within the United States and is currently authorized to work under F-1 Optional Practical Training. Their professional experience is limited — consistent with the "early career" designation — typically a first role or an early role since completing their studies, rather than an extended track record. They are actively evaluating their next career step within the window their OPT authorization allows.

This level of detail is intentionally the ceiling for this section — no specific graduation date, institution, employer, or prior salary is invented, because a real advisor would learn those specifics directly from a real candidate rather than from a template.

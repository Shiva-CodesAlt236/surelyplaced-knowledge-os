# Reference Profile: Discovery Strategy

> This article is part of the Gold Standard reference profile — an illustrative archetype, never a real person. This is guidance for approaching a discovery call with this archetype, not a transcript or a real conversation.

## Purpose

This article shows how a completed profile translates Layer 1 identity and Layer 2 mindset into Layer 3 stage-specific advisor guidance, using discovery as the first example.

## Layer 1 — Candidate Identity

Applies as established in [Career Background](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/career-background) and [Technology Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/technology-profile): an early-career software engineer on F-1 OPT.

## Layer 2 — Sales Intelligence

Applies as established in [Candidate Mindset](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/candidate-mindset): skepticism-first evaluation combined with bounded OPT timeline urgency.

## Layer 3 — Advisor Playbook

Open the call consistent with [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), but expect the candidate to move toward specific, practical questions faster than a less technically-minded candidate might — this isn't impatience, it's how this archetype evaluates.

Qualify using [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification), giving explicit space early on for the OPT timeline to surface, consistent with the F1/OPT Playbook's emphasis on acknowledging that timeline honestly rather than waiting for the candidate to bring it up under pressure later.

Use [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals) to distinguish between the candidate's immediate goal (securing a role before their OPT window narrows) and their longer-term career direction — both matter, and conflating them risks either over-indexing on urgency or missing it.

Build rapport consistent with [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), recognizing that for a skepticism-first evaluator, rapport is often built through demonstrated competence and specific, accurate answers more than through purely social warmth.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This discovery strategy is a direct application of the existing Discovery module, shaped by this profile's Layer 1 and Layer 2 content — it introduces no new discovery technique beyond what [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), [Candidate Qualification](/docs/discovery/candidate-qualification), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals) already establish.

## Key Takeaways

Discovery with this archetype should give explicit early space to the OPT timeline, expect specific and practical questions as a sign of genuine engagement rather than impatience, and build rapport partly through demonstrated competence — all direct applications of the existing Discovery module rather than new technique.

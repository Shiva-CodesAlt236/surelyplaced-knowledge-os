# Reference Profile: Interview Support

> **Operational Policy Pending.** This repository does not document a specific interview-support curriculum, service structure, or delivery process. This article covers only what an advisor should generally understand about this archetype's likely interview-readiness needs — never a description of a specific program, session structure, or guaranteed outcome. Advisors should follow the latest approved internal process for any actual interview-support offering.

## Purpose

Once a candidate enrolls, their needs shift from evaluating whether to work with Surely Placed to preparing for what comes next. This article covers what an advisor should generally understand about this archetype's likely interview-readiness needs post-enrollment, without describing or inventing a specific interview-support program this repository doesn't document.

## Layer 1 — Candidate Identity

Applies as established throughout this reference profile: an early-career software engineer on F-1 OPT, whose limited professional experience (see [Career Background](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/career-background)) likely means limited formal interview experience as well.

## Layer 2 — Sales Intelligence

An early-career candidate with the `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` evaluation style from [Candidate Mindset](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/candidate-mindset) may bring the same evidence-seeking approach to interview preparation that they brought to evaluating Surely Placed itself — likely to ask specific, practical questions about what to expect rather than accepting general reassurance.

## Layer 3 — Advisor Playbook

Recognize that this archetype's technical background, described generally in [Technology Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/technology-profile), means genuine interview-readiness questions likely require input from internal technical SMEs rather than the advisor's own assessment, consistent with the non-invention standard in [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING).

Any specific interview-support offering, session structure, or timeline should be described to the candidate using only the current, internally approved description of that program — never approximated or promised based on general expectation.

Where the OPT timeline is a factor, be honest about the fact that interview readiness and timeline pressure can compound each other, without promising a specific outcome or pace, consistent with [Things We Never Say](/docs/sales-constitution/things-we-never-say).

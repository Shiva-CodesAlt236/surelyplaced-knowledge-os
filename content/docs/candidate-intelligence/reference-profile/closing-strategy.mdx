# Reference Profile: Closing Strategy

> This article is part of the Gold Standard reference profile — an illustrative archetype, never a real person. This is guidance for approaching a closing call with this archetype, not a transcript or a real conversation.

## Purpose

This article completes the core funnel-stage sequence of this profile's Layer 3 advisor playbook, following [Discovery Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/discovery-strategy), [Discussion Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/discussion-strategy), and [Objection Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/objection-strategy).

## Layer 1 — Candidate Identity

The same identity holds from earlier in this profile: an early-career software engineer on F-1 OPT.

## Layer 2 — Sales Intelligence

By the closing stage, most `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` signals from [Candidate Mindset](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/candidate-mindset) should already be resolved through the discussion and objection stages — a candidate reaching closing while still raising unresolved technical or comparison questions may indicate those earlier stages need revisiting rather than proceeding to close, consistent with [Risk Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/risk-signals).

## Layer 3 — Advisor Playbook

Confirm genuine readiness before moving to a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) — for this archetype specifically, verify that the earlier technical and comparison questions were actually resolved, not just that the conversation moved forward.

Ask for the commitment directly and honestly, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), without leaning on OPT timeline urgency as a pressure tactic — the timeline is a real factor the candidate is already weighing, and using it as a closing lever would cross into the pressure this Knowledge OS consistently warns against.

If hesitation appears at this stage, apply [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation), watching specifically for whether it reflects genuine financial vulnerability consistent with [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags), given that this may be the candidate's first major independent financial decision.

Handle payment and agreement consistent with [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) and [Payment Conversations](/docs/pricing/payment-conversations), watching for whether a financial co-decision-maker — a parent, in many early-career cases — needs to be included, consistent with [Financial Decision-Makers](/docs/pricing/financial-decision-makers).

## Cross-Module Alignment

This closing strategy applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), [Handling Final Hesitation](/docs/closing/handling-final-hesitation), and [Financial Decision-Makers](/docs/pricing/financial-decision-makers) directly — no new closing technique is introduced here.

## Key Takeaways

Closing with this archetype means confirming that earlier technical and comparison questions were genuinely resolved, never leaning on OPT timeline urgency as pressure, and staying alert to the possibility of a financial co-decision-maker or genuine financial vulnerability given this may be a first major independent financial decision — all direct applications of the existing Closing and Pricing modules.

# Reference Profile: Presentation Strategy

> This article is part of the Gold Standard reference profile — an illustrative archetype, never a real person. This article demonstrates presentation *approach*, not a script, and states no specific price, statistic, or guarantee.

## Purpose

This article expands on the presentation portion of [Discussion Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/discussion-strategy), showing in more depth how to present Surely Placed's value specifically to this archetype.

## Layer 1 — Candidate Identity

Carries forward unchanged from the earlier articles in this profile: an early-career software engineer on F-1 OPT.

## Layer 2 — Sales Intelligence

The `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` pattern from [Candidate Mindset](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/candidate-mindset) is most directly relevant here — this archetype tends to evaluate a presentation the way they'd evaluate a technical claim: looking for specificity and evidence rather than being persuaded by confident general statements alone.

## Layer 3 — Advisor Playbook

Present Surely Placed's documented scope specifically and honestly, consistent with [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation) — work with candidates who already hold valid work authorization, in IT-related roles, across the US, Canada, and UK — rather than a general claim about quality or success.

Connect the presentation to the candidate's own stated goals from discovery, consistent with [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed), since a specific connection to what the candidate has already said matters more to this archetype than a broad, generic pitch.

Let the consultative quality of the conversation itself demonstrate value, consistent with [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation) — for a skepticism-first evaluator, the way questions are actually answered during the call often carries more weight than any single statement about the company.

If the candidate asks about outcomes or return, apply [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) precisely — this archetype is statistically likely to ask directly for a number, and the discipline of redirecting to qualitative, structural value without citing a figure matters as much here as anywhere else in this Knowledge OS.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This presentation strategy applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed), [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation), and [ROI Conversations](/docs/pricing/roi-conversations) directly — no new presentation technique or claim is introduced here.

## Key Takeaways

Presenting to this archetype works best through specific, honest, verifiable description connected to the candidate's own stated goals, rather than general enthusiasm — and should be prepared for a direct request for a return figure, which is answered qualitatively per ROI Conversations, never with an invented number.

# Reference Profile: Discussion Strategy

> This article is part of the Gold Standard reference profile — an illustrative archetype, never a real person. This is guidance for approaching a discussion call with this archetype, not a transcript or a real conversation.

## Purpose

This article continues the Layer 3 advisor playbook from [Discovery Strategy](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/discovery-strategy) into the discussion stage.

## Layer 1 — Candidate Identity

Applies as established in [Career Background](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/career-background): an early-career software engineer on F-1 OPT.

## Layer 2 — Sales Intelligence

Applies as established in [Candidate Mindset](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/candidate-mindset), particularly relevant here: the `PERSONA-SKEPTIC` pattern and the investment-psychology dynamic of a candidate spending on their own career for what may be the first time.

## Layer 3 — Advisor Playbook

Structure the call consistent with [Structuring the Discussion Call](/docs/discussion/structuring-the-discussion-call), giving slightly more room than average for specific, detail-seeking questions before moving to the investment conversation, since rushing a skepticism-first evaluator toward cost tends to produce more resistance, not less.

When discussing investment, follow [Discussing Investment](/docs/discussion/discussing-investment) for structure and [Presenting Investment Confidently](/docs/pricing/presenting-investment-confidently) for delivery — state the cost steadily and without rushing, since a candidate evaluating their first major independent financial decision is likely to notice hesitation in the advisor's tone as much as the number itself.

Present Surely Placed's value consistent with [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation), favoring specific, verifiable description over general enthusiasm, consistent with this archetype's evidence-seeking evaluation style.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This discussion strategy applies [Structuring the Discussion Call](/docs/discussion/structuring-the-discussion-call), [Discussing Investment](/docs/discussion/discussing-investment), [Presenting Investment Confidently](/docs/pricing/presenting-investment-confidently), and [Surely Placed Differentiation](/docs/sales-constitution/surely-placed-differentiation) directly — no new discussion-stage technique is introduced here.

## Key Takeaways

Discussion with this archetype benefits from unhurried, specific engagement with the candidate's detail-seeking questions before moving to cost, steady and confident delivery of the investment conversation, and value presentation grounded in verifiable specifics rather than general enthusiasm — all direct applications of the existing Discussion and Pricing modules.

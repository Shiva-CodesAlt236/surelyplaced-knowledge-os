# Closing Guide

> This guide names no figure, discount, or promised outcome, and draws no comparison between platforms — it covers closing technique only.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to the Salesforce role family, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for Salesforce candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before asking for a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), confirm the working-pattern label discussed throughout the conversation still feels accurate to the candidate — per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/objection-guide), an unresolved labeling mismatch is a reliable way to stall a decision that would otherwise move forward.

Ground the close in the trajectory the candidate described during discovery, consistent with [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment) — connecting the decision back to where they said they wanted to grow within the ecosystem tends to land better than a purely logistical close.

Apply [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process) for this step, watching for signs of financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags) the same way this framework expects for every role family it covers.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's platform-fluency findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm the working-pattern label still feels accurate before closing, ground the close in the candidate's stated trajectory, and stay alert to financial vulnerability as with any role family.

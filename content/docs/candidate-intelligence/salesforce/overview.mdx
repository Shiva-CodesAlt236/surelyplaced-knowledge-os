# Salesforce Role Collection — Overview

> This is the tenth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, and the first of four platform-focused collections generated together — Salesforce, SAP, ServiceNow, and Oracle — following Software Engineering through Healthcare.

## Purpose

This article opens the Salesforce Role Collection. No platform-specific Industry Playbook exists yet for Salesforce, so this collection classifies under `ROLE-PENDING` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and defers broader industry-level guidance to [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework) until a dedicated playbook is scoped. The four working patterns below make up the rest of the collection, positioned against what's already been published.

## Scope

This collection treats Salesforce as a single ecosystem a candidate might work within, not a product to be sold, evaluated, or ranked against anything else. The working patterns that follow describe how a candidate whose background centers on this platform tends to talk about their own experience. No claim is made anywhere about market position, hiring demand, or which certification a candidate should pursue.

## Core Content

Four working patterns make up this collection: [Salesforce Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-administrator), [Salesforce Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-developer), [Salesforce Functional Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-functional-consultant), and [Salesforce Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-architect). From there, the collection covers representative ecosystem tooling at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/technology-ecosystem), sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), [Industry Discovery Framework](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework), and the fourteen-article pattern already established across the nine collections before it.

## Advisor Guidance

Read this overview before opening any single working-pattern article that follows it. Keep Salesforce framed strictly as the candidate's working ecosystem: no comparison to a competing platform, no suggestion of market standing, and no certification recommendation the candidate didn't raise first.

# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to the Salesforce role family, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Salesforce candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Ask the candidate to describe their working pattern in their own words early, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/candidate-behaviour) — inviting them to self-identify as closer to Administrator, Developer, Consultant, or Architect work, rather than assigning a label based on a title alone, signals genuine understanding of how this population thinks about their own experience.

Consistent with [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), ask directly where the candidate sees themselves moving next along this collection's spectrum — toward custom build, toward stakeholder and process work, or toward broader architectural scope — since that trajectory often matters as much to them as their current position.

Confirm the working-pattern label you've landed on together before moving forward, per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/buying-patterns) — a mismatch here is one of the more reliable ways to stall this population's engagement.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's platform-fluency findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Invite the candidate to self-identify their working pattern rather than assigning one, ask directly about their intended trajectory within the ecosystem, and confirm the working-pattern label together before moving forward.

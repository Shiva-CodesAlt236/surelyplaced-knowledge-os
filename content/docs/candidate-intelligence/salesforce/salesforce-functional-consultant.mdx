# Salesforce Functional Consultant

## Purpose

This article describes the Salesforce Functional Consultant working pattern — candidates who sit between a business's stated needs and the platform's actual capability, shaping requirements into something the ecosystem can deliver.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a process-translation-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe running discovery sessions with business stakeholders, documenting requirements, and designing how a business process should actually work inside the platform before an administrator or developer implements it. Many carry a business-analysis or business-operations background as much as a technical one, and often describe their strongest skill as translation — hearing what a department actually needs and turning it into something buildable, rather than the build itself.

This pattern is frequently client-facing or cross-departmental, and candidates in it often describe stakeholder management and requirements documentation as central to the role, distinct from [Salesforce Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-administrator) candidates who describe their work more in terms of direct platform maintenance.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the process-translation point of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the configuration focus of [Salesforce Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-administrator) and the custom-build focus of [Salesforce Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-developer).

## Advisor Guidance

Ask how much of the candidate's work is stakeholder-facing versus hands-on configuration, since this pattern spans both to varying degrees. This pattern overlaps meaningfully with [Process & Operations Business Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/business-analysis/overview), and it's worth calling that out directly if a candidate seems to sit near that boundary.

## Key Takeaways

Salesforce Functional Consultants translate business requirements into platform-deliverable designs, typically carry a business-process background alongside platform knowledge, and often work in stakeholder-facing or cross-departmental capacities distinct from direct platform maintenance.

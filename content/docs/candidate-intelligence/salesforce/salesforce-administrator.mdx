# Salesforce Administrator

## Purpose

This article describes the Salesforce Administrator working pattern — candidates who keep an organization's Salesforce environment running and adapted to changing business needs, primarily through the platform's built-in configuration tools rather than custom code.

## Scope

This article exists to help an advisor tell a configuration-focused candidate apart from this collection's other three working patterns in conversation. No certification path is named, and no claim is made about how common or in-demand this pattern is.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their day-to-day work in terms of user setup, permissions, workflow automation, page layouts, and reporting — adjusting the platform to fit how a business actually operates, usually without writing custom code. Many entered this pattern from an operations, sales support, or general business-systems background rather than a formal software background, and often describe their value in terms of how directly their changes are felt by the people who use the system daily.

This pattern is frequently an entry point into the broader ecosystem: administrators who take on more complex automation or process-design work sometimes grow toward the [Salesforce Functional Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-functional-consultant) pattern, while those drawn toward writing custom logic sometimes grow toward [Salesforce Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-developer). A candidate describing this trajectory is naming a real and common path within the ecosystem, not overstating their experience.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the configuration end of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the custom-build focus of [Salesforce Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-developer) and the process-translation focus of [Salesforce Functional Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-functional-consultant).

## Advisor Guidance

Ask what kind of configuration work the candidate handles most — user administration, automation, or reporting — since this pattern spans a real range. Confirm whether they see themselves growing toward custom development or toward process and stakeholder work, since that distinction shapes which of this collection's other working patterns is most relevant to their goals.

Salesforce Administrators keep an organization's environment adapted to its operations primarily through configuration rather than custom code, often serve as an entry point into the broader ecosystem, and frequently grow toward either the developer or functional-consultant pattern depending on their own interests.

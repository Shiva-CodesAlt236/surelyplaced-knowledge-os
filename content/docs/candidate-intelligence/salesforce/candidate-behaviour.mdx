# Candidate Behaviour

## Purpose

This article names the mindset that most reliably shows up across the four Salesforce working patterns, giving this collection its own distinct anchor apart from the nine collections before it: platform-fluency self-positioning.

## Scope

These patterns hold across the role family in general. A specific working pattern's own article notes it directly wherever its behaviour meaningfully departs from this baseline.

## Core Content

Because this ecosystem uses its own well-known labels — Administrator, Developer, Consultant, Architect — as a recognized maturity ladder, candidates in this population tend to be unusually sensitive to being labeled precisely and correctly. Being described in a way that undersells their actual scope of work, even unintentionally, tends to read as a real slight rather than a minor wording issue, because the label itself carries meaning within the community this candidate identifies with.

A second, related trait: this population often self-positions along the configuration-to-custom-build spectrum this collection documents, actively distinguishing their own work from an adjacent pattern even when a conversation doesn't ask them to. An advisor who reflects that self-positioning back accurately, rather than flattening it into a generic platform-skills description, tends to build credibility quickly with this population.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article establishes this collection's own behavioural anchor, consistent with how each preceding collection identifies a theme genuinely distinct from every other collection in the framework.

## Advisor Guidance

Use the candidate's own working-pattern label rather than a generic substitute, and reflect their self-described position on the configuration-to-custom-build spectrum back accurately rather than flattening it.

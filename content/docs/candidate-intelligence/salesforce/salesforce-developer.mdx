# Salesforce Developer

## Purpose

This article describes the Salesforce Developer working pattern — candidates who build custom functionality on the platform when its declarative configuration tools aren't sufficient for what an organization needs.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a custom-build-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only. It stays at a general, ecosystem level rather than a specific programming-language deep dive.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe writing custom logic, building integrations with outside systems, and extending the platform's standard capabilities to meet requirements that configuration alone can't satisfy. Many arrive with a general software-development background and picked up platform-specific development conventions on the job, while others grew into this pattern from [Salesforce Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-administrator) work as their organization's needs became more technically demanding.

This pattern often works closely alongside administrators and functional consultants on the same project, translating a business requirement those roles have already shaped into working custom functionality — candidates frequently describe this collaborative handoff explicitly when talking about how their work fits into a larger team.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the custom-build end of the spectrum this collection documents, distinct from the configuration focus of [Salesforce Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-administrator) and the senior, cross-cloud design focus of [Salesforce Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-architect).

## Advisor Guidance

Ask whether the candidate's development work is mostly custom logic, integrations, or both, and whether they came to it from a general software background or grew into it from platform administration. Both paths are common and neither should be treated as more legitimate than the other.

## Key Takeaways

Salesforce Developers extend the platform through custom code and integrations where declarative configuration isn't enough, arrive from either a general software background or a growth path out of administration, and typically collaborate closely with administrators and functional consultants on shared projects.

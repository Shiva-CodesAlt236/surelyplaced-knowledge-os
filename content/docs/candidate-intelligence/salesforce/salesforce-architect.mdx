# Salesforce Architect

## Purpose

This article describes the Salesforce Architect working pattern — candidates responsible for the overall structure and long-term technical direction of a platform implementation, typically spanning multiple clouds or business units.

## Scope

This article helps an advisor recognize a senior, design-focused candidate against this collection's other three working patterns in conversation. No specific certification level is named.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe setting technical direction across a platform implementation rather than executing a single piece of it — decisions about data architecture, integration strategy, and how multiple clouds or business units fit together over time. Most carry substantial prior experience in one or more of the other three patterns in this collection before moving into architecture, and often describe the shift as one from building or configuring individual pieces to being accountable for how everything fits together.

This population tends to speak in terms of long-term maintainability and organizational impact rather than a single project's deliverables, and often has direct experience mentoring or reviewing the work of administrators, developers, and consultants on the same implementation.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern sits at the senior end of the spectrum this collection documents, drawing on the configuration, custom-build, and process-translation focuses of the other three working patterns without being defined by any single one of them.

## Advisor Guidance

Ask which of the other three patterns preceded the candidate's move into architecture, since this population almost always has direct experience in at least one. Keep discovery centered on scope and structural responsibility, rather than a specific technical task, since that's how this population tends to describe their own value.

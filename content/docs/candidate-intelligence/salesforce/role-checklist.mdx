# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Salesforce Role Collection, the tenth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework and the first of this batch's four platform-focused collections.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Salesforce Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the nine preceding collections established.

## Scope

This checklist works through all fourteen articles that make up this collection, in the order established throughout: working patterns, ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Salesforce Administrator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-administrator), [Salesforce Developer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-developer), [Salesforce Functional Consultant](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-functional-consultant), and [Salesforce Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/salesforce-architect) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every named tool treated purely as an illustration of its category, with no ranking or comparison anywhere?
- [ ] Does the collection avoid weighing this platform against any competing platform, claiming any kind of market position, or steering a candidate toward a certification?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular platform-fluency self-positioning mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/closing-guide) stay platform-neutral throughout?
- [ ] Is each stage guide built directly on top of its matching core sales-stage module, without inventing a new technique of its own?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Does every article avoid any hiring-demand claim, salary figure, promised result, or employer-specific statement?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the nine preceding collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its platform-neutrality requirement specific to this batch.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the nine preceding collections' checklists to confirm structural consistency across the whole framework.

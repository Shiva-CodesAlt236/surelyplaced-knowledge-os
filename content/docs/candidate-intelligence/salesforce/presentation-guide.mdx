# Presentation Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discussion module to the Salesforce role family, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers presentation-call approach for Salesforce candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process).

## Core Content

Present opportunities using the working-pattern label confirmed during discovery, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/salesforce/candidate-behaviour) — restating it accurately here reinforces that the earlier conversation was genuinely heard, not just logged.

When explaining the process per [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), connect it to the trajectory the candidate named during discovery if one came up — showing how the opportunity fits their stated direction within the ecosystem, whether toward custom build, stakeholder work, or broader architecture, tends to land better than a generic walkthrough.

Steer clear of any wording that could sound like a comparison to another platform, per this collection's platform-neutrality requirement — describe the opportunity purely through the candidate's own working pattern and the role itself.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), shaped by this collection's platform-fluency findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Restate the confirmed working-pattern label accurately, connect the process to the candidate's stated trajectory, and keep the conversation strictly platform-neutral throughout.

# Advisor Coaching

> The coaching example that follows is a constructed illustration only, clearly marked as such, and describes no real advisor, candidate, or conversation.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for the Salesforce role family, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with Salesforce candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor correctly identified and used the candidate's actual working-pattern label rather than a generic substitute — a miss worth flagging under `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) given how much this population reads into precise labeling; whether the advisor stayed strictly platform-neutral throughout, never implying this platform's standing relative to any other; and whether a labeling or scope mismatch, once identified, was actually corrected rather than left unresolved.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-STRONG-MOMENT — caught that the role description undersold the candidate's actual scope of work and corrected it directly, which the candidate later cited as a reason they trusted the process"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches should treat correct working-pattern identification and strict platform-neutrality as non-negotiable coaching priorities, alongside timely correction of any identified labeling mismatch, per the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

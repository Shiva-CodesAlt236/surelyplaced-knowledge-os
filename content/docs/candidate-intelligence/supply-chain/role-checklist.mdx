# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Supply Chain Role Collection, the eighth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Supply Chain Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the seven preceding collections established.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized the same way as the collections before it: working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Procurement & Sourcing Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/procurement-sourcing-analyst), [Logistics & Operations Coordinator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/logistics-operations-coordinator), [Supply Chain Planner](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-planner), and [Supply Chain Analytics & Systems Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-analytics-systems-specialist) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Does every named tool appear strictly as an illustrative example, with no comparison or ranking implied?
- [ ] Does any article show a preference for one named product over a competitor's?
- [ ] Does this collection stay clearly distinct from the Data & AI collection rather than duplicating it, particularly at the Analytics & Systems Specialist boundary?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular reliability-focused mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook?
- [ ] Do all four stage guides apply their matching core sales-stage module instead of inventing a separate approach?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Does the collection state no hiring figure, salary number, promised result, or employer-specific claim anywhere?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the seven preceding collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its overlap-avoidance requirement with the Data & AI collection.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the seven preceding collections' checklists to confirm all eight Role Collections remain structurally consistent.

# Supply Chain Role Collection — Overview

> This is the eighth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework, following Software Engineering, Data & AI, Cybersecurity, Cloud & DevOps, QA Testing, Business Analysis, and Project Management.

## Purpose

This article opens the Supply Chain Role Collection, mapped to `ROLE-SUPPLY` in [Role Classification](/docs/candidate-intelligence/ROLE_CLASSIFICATION) and to the [Supply Chain Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/supply-chain-playbook). It introduces the four working patterns that follow and places this collection among the ones already published.

## Scope

This collection covers candidates working across procurement, logistics, planning, and supply chain systems and analytics — a field that has grown increasingly technology-driven, spanning purely operational roles through heavily data- and systems-focused ones. It does not cover general data engineering or analytics work outside a supply chain context; where a candidate's background sits closer to that, the [Data & AI Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/overview) may be the better fit.

## Core Content

Four working patterns make up this collection: [Procurement & Sourcing Analyst](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/procurement-sourcing-analyst), [Logistics & Operations Coordinator](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/logistics-operations-coordinator), [Supply Chain Planner](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-planner), and [Supply Chain Analytics & Systems Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-analytics-systems-specialist). From there, the collection covers representative tools at [Technology Ecosystem](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/technology-ecosystem), sales intelligence at [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/buying-patterns), a four-stage advisor playbook, coaching priorities, and a closing checklist.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This collection builds on the [Candidate Intelligence Framework](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), the [Gold Standard Reference Profile](/docs/candidate-intelligence/reference-profile/overview), the [Supply Chain Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/supply-chain-playbook), and the fourteen-article pattern already established across the seven collections before it.

## Advisor Guidance

Start with this overview before opening any individual working-pattern article in the collection. Because supply chain work spans a genuine operational-to-analytical range, confirming where a candidate sits on that range matters as much here as the technical-to-stakeholder-facing question does in the Business Analysis collection.

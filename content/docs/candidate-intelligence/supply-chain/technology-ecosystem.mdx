# Technology Ecosystem

> Products named below illustrate a category only — none is being compared, ranked, or endorsed over another.

## Purpose

This article gives an advisor a working vocabulary of the systems a Supply Chain candidate might reference, in line with [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING). It is not logistics or systems training, and it evaluates no specific product.

## Scope

Four categories of tooling common across this role family are covered below, spanning procurement through analytics. No single tool receives its own dedicated treatment.

## Core Content

**Enterprise resource planning and procurement systems** — platforms for managing purchasing, vendor records, and organization-wide resource planning. SAP and Oracle are two illustrative examples of what sits in this category, mentioned only to show its shape, not to compare or endorse either.

**Warehouse and transportation management** — systems coordinating physical movement and storage of goods. Manhattan Associates and Blue Yonder are two illustrative examples of what sits in this category, mentioned only to show its shape, not to compare or endorse either.

**Demand planning and forecasting** — tools supporting inventory and demand projections over time. Kinaxis and o9 Solutions are two illustrative examples of what sits in this category, mentioned only to show its shape, not to compare or endorse either.

**Supply chain analytics and reporting** — dashboarding and data tools used to analyze supply chain performance. Power BI and Tableau are two illustrative examples of what sits in this category, mentioned only to show its shape, not to compare or endorse either.

Many candidates reference a system outside this list entirely, or a direct competitor to one named here — this is a starting vocabulary, not a full catalog of the field.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article applies the non-evaluative, category-first approach [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING) sets out generally, matching the equivalent article's role across the seven collections before this one.

## Advisor Guidance

Use these categories only to follow a candidate's vocabulary, not to judge whether their specific system choices were correct. No named tool should ever be compared, ranked, or presented as preferable to a competitor.

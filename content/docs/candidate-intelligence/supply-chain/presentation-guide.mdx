# Presentation Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discussion module to `ROLE-SUPPLY`, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers presentation-call approach for Supply Chain candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process).

## Core Content

Present the recommendation concretely and specifically, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/candidate-behaviour) — this population responds to demonstrable substance rather than broad enthusiasm, and a pitch that stays general risks losing credibility with a population trained to demand evidence.

When explaining the process per [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), ground each step in what it concretely delivers rather than describing it abstractly. For candidates whose pattern leans toward [Supply Chain Analytics & Systems Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-analytics-systems-specialist), address directly whether the recommendation reflects a supply chain framing or a broader data and systems framing, based on what discovery surfaced.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), shaped by this collection's reliability-focused findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Present concretely and specifically rather than with broad enthusiasm, ground each process step in what it actually delivers, and address framing directly for candidates near the Data & AI boundary.

# Closing Guide

> No specific figure, discount, or promised result is stated in this guide; it covers closing technique only.

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Closing module to `ROLE-SUPPLY`, completing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers closing-call approach for Supply Chain candidates broadly, building on [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment) and [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

## Core Content

Before asking for a decision, consistent with [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), summarize concretely what's been discussed and demonstrated rather than closing on enthusiasm — this population responds to substance, and a close that leans on excitement rather than evidence is likely to feel unconvincing.

Confirm any ROI-framed concern raised earlier — the most common underlying objection for this population, per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/objection-guide) — was genuinely addressed with specifics before revisiting [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment).

Payment and agreement should follow [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process); watch for signs of financial vulnerability per [Pricing Red Flags](/docs/pricing/pricing-red-flags), consistent with every other role family in this framework.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Decision to Enrollment](/docs/closing/decision-to-enrollment), [Asking for the Commitment](/docs/closing/asking-for-the-commitment), and [Payment and Agreement Process](/docs/closing/payment-and-agreement-process), shaped by this collection's reliability-focused findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Close with a concrete summary of demonstrated substance rather than enthusiasm, confirm any ROI-framed concern was genuinely resolved with specifics, and stay alert to financial vulnerability as with any role family.

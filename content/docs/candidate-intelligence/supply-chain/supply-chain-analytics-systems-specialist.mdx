# Supply Chain Analytics & Systems Specialist

## Purpose

This article describes the Supply Chain Analytics & Systems Specialist working pattern within `ROLE-SUPPLY` — candidates who build and maintain the systems and analytical models that power planning and operations decisions elsewhere in the field.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a systems- and analytics-heavy candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms of data models, systems configuration, and analytical tools that other supply chain professionals rely on to plan and operate. This pattern represents the field's most technical end and its background often overlaps meaningfully with data-focused work outside supply chain specifically — some candidates could plausibly describe their experience through a Data & AI lens instead, and it's worth confirming directly which framing fits better.

Unlike [Supply Chain Planner](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-planner), whose primary output is a forecast or inventory decision, this pattern's primary output is the tool or system that makes those decisions possible in the first place — a distinction that matters when discussing career direction and fit.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern is the collection's clearest bridge to the [Data & AI Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/overview), particularly [Data Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/data-and-ai/data-engineer), and an advisor should treat that overlap as a real discovery question rather than a documentation inconsistency.

## Advisor Guidance

Confirm directly whether a candidate identifies more with supply chain domain expertise or with the underlying data and systems work itself — the two framings can lead to meaningfully different conversations about fit.

# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to `ROLE-SUPPLY`, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Supply Chain candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Ask concrete, specific questions from the outset rather than opening broadly, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/candidate-behaviour) — this population responds to specificity and tends to disengage from a conversation that stays abstract too long.

Consistent with [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), confirm directly where a candidate sits on the operational-to-analytical spectrum this collection is organized around. For candidates whose background leans toward [Supply Chain Analytics & Systems Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-analytics-systems-specialist), clarify whether they identify more with supply chain domain expertise or with the underlying data and systems work.

Ask for a specific example involving a real disruption or setback, not just a smooth outcome — per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/buying-patterns), this population tends to describe that kind of story candidly, and it reveals more than a success story would.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call) and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's reliability-focused findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Open with specifics rather than broad framing, confirm the candidate's actual position on the operational-to-analytical spectrum, and invite an example that includes a real disruption rather than only a success story.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with Supply Chain candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor grounded claims in something concrete rather than general enthusiasm, consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) when a vague or unsubstantiated statement went uncorrected; whether the advisor correctly identified the candidate's specific position on the operational-to-analytical spectrum rather than assuming; and whether framing confusion at the Data & AI boundary, when present, was addressed directly for [Supply Chain Analytics & Systems Specialist](/docs/candidate-intelligence/supply-chain/supply-chain-analytics-systems-specialist) candidates.

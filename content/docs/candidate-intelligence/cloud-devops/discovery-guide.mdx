# Discovery Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discovery module to `ROLE-CLOUD`, opening this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers discovery-call approach for Cloud & DevOps candidates broadly, building on [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals).

## Core Content

Open by letting the candidate describe their actual responsibilities in their own words before offering any category label — per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/buying-patterns), this population disengages quietly when it senses a generic pitch, and starting with the candidate's own framing is the most reliable way to avoid that from the first few minutes.

Consistent with [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), match this population's preference for directness — a shorter, more efficient discovery conversation tends to build more trust here than a long, exploratory one. Use correct technical vocabulary or ask rather than guess, since imprecise language is noticed quickly by this candidate population, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/candidate-behaviour).

When exploring goals per [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), ask specifically which of the four working patterns the candidate identifies with most, since [Site Reliability Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/site-reliability-engineer) candidates in particular may raise career sustainability as a real goal worth taking seriously rather than treating it as secondary to advancement.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Opening the Discovery Call](/docs/discovery/opening-the-discovery-call), [Building Rapport](/docs/discovery/building-rapport), and [Understanding Candidate Goals](/docs/discovery/understanding-candidate-goals), shaped by this collection's ownership-and-efficiency findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Lead with the candidate's own description of their work, keep the conversation efficient, use precise technical vocabulary, and treat career-sustainability goals as legitimate rather than secondary when they come up.

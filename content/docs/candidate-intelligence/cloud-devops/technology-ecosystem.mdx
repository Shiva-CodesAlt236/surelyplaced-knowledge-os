# Technology Ecosystem

> Every tool and platform named in this article is a category-illustrative example only. None are compared, ranked, or recommended over another.

## Purpose

This article names representative technologies across the Cloud & DevOps ecosystem so an advisor recognizes vocabulary a candidate might use, consistent with [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING). It is not technical training and does not evaluate any product.

## Scope

This article covers four broad technology categories relevant to this role family. It does not provide deep-dive documentation on any single tool.

## Core Content

**Cloud platforms** — the underlying infrastructure providers this role family builds on. Illustrative examples of products in this category include Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform, named here only to indicate the category, not to compare or endorse any of them.

**Containerization and orchestration** — technologies for packaging and running applications consistently across environments. Illustrative examples of products in this category include Docker and Kubernetes, named here only to indicate the category, not to compare or endorse either.

**Infrastructure as Code** — tooling for defining infrastructure in version-controlled configuration rather than manual setup. Illustrative examples of products in this category include Terraform and Ansible, named here only to indicate the category, not to compare or endorse either.

**CI/CD and automation tooling** — platforms for automating build, test, and deployment pipelines. Illustrative examples of products in this category include Jenkins and GitHub Actions, named here only to indicate the category, not to compare or endorse either.

A candidate may mention any of these, a competing product in the same category, or something not listed here at all — this list exists to orient an advisor to category vocabulary, not to define the boundaries of the field.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article applies [Technology Mapping](/docs/candidate-intelligence/TECHNOLOGY_MAPPING)'s non-evaluative, category-based approach to `ROLE-CLOUD`, following the same pattern used in the Software Engineering, Data & AI, and Cybersecurity collections' equivalent articles.

## Advisor Guidance

Use these categories to recognize vocabulary, not to evaluate a candidate's specific tool choices. Never compare or rank any named tool against another, and never suggest one platform or product is preferable to a competitor — that judgment belongs to the candidate's own experience and, where relevant, a technical SME.

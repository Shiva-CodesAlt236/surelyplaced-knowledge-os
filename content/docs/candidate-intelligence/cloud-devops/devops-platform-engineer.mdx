# DevOps / Platform Engineer

## Purpose

This article describes the DevOps / Platform Engineer working pattern within `ROLE-CLOUD` — candidates centered on automation, deployment pipelines, and the internal tooling other engineers rely on.

## Scope

This article covers what typically distinguishes a DevOps- or platform-focused candidate from the other three working patterns in this collection, for sales-conversation purposes only.

## Core Content

Candidates in this pattern describe their work in terms of automation and enablement — building the pipelines, tooling, and internal platforms that let other engineering teams ship faster and more safely. Many describe their role as sitting between infrastructure and application development, translating operational needs into something self-service for the teams they support.

This pattern often carries a strong efficiency orientation: candidates in this space tend to evaluate almost everything, including a conversation with an advisor, through the lens of "is this the fastest, most direct path to what I need." That's not impatience for its own sake — it reflects a professional habit of eliminating friction wherever they find it, and it tends to show up in how they engage with a sales conversation as much as in how they describe their work.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This pattern gives specific shape to the efficiency and directness theme the [Cloud & DevOps Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/cloud-devops-playbook) names at the industry level, and its automation-and-tooling focus distinguishes it clearly from the reliability focus of [Site Reliability Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/site-reliability-engineer).

## Advisor Guidance

Get to the point quickly and be specific about how a recommendation applies to this candidate's actual situation — this pattern tends to notice and disengage from a longer, more generic pitch faster than most. Frame the conversation itself as an efficient use of the candidate's time, not just the eventual outcome.

## Key Takeaways

DevOps / Platform Engineers focus on automation, pipelines, and internal tooling, bring a strong efficiency orientation that shapes how they evaluate a sales conversation itself, and are best engaged with directness and specificity rather than a longer general pitch.

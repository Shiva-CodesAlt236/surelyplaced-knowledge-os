# Presentation Guide

## Purpose

This guide applies the existing Discussion module to `ROLE-CLOUD`, continuing this collection's four-stage advisor playbook.

## Scope

This guide covers presentation-call approach for Cloud & DevOps candidates broadly, building on [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process).

## Core Content

Consistent with [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed), keep the presentation specific and efficient rather than broad — this population responds better to a recommendation clearly grounded in what discovery surfaced about their actual working pattern than to a general overview of the service. Reference the candidate's own described ownership and responsibilities explicitly, per [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/candidate-behaviour), rather than defaulting to generic "software" or "IT" framing.

When explaining the process per [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), be precise about any technical terminology used and willing to say plainly when a detail should be confirmed with a technical SME rather than guessed at — this population notices imprecision quickly and it can cost credibility fast if handled carelessly.

Where the candidate's working pattern is [Cloud Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/cloud-architect), be prepared to discuss scope and positioning, not just technical fit, since that pattern often evaluates a move partly on influence and breadth per [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/buying-patterns).

## Cross-Module Alignment

This guide applies [Presenting Surely Placed](/docs/discussion/presenting-surely-placed) and [Explaining the Process](/docs/discussion/explaining-the-process), shaped by this collection's ownership-and-precision findings.

## Advisor Guidance

Ground the presentation specifically in what discovery surfaced, use precise technical language or flag uncertainty honestly, and address scope and positioning directly for candidates in more senior working patterns.

## Key Takeaways

Presenting to this role family works best when it's specific and efficient, grounded in the candidate's own described ownership, precise with technical language, and — for more senior candidates — attentive to scope and positioning alongside technical fit.

# Advisor Coaching

> The coaching note below is a labeled, invented illustration only — it does not describe any real advisor, candidate, or call.

## Purpose

This article sets coaching priorities for `ROLE-CLOUD`, extending the schema in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) to this role family's specific risk areas.

## Scope

This article covers what a coach should prioritize when reviewing calls with Cloud & DevOps candidates broadly.

## Core Content

Review should prioritize three areas specific to this role family: whether the advisor used precise, correct technical vocabulary throughout the call, consistent with `COACH-SIGNAL-MISSED` in [Coaching Signals](/docs/candidate-intelligence/COACHING_SIGNALS) when imprecise language went unnoticed or uncorrected; whether a scope-and-fit concern, when present, was actively surfaced rather than left to the candidate to raise on their own, per [Objection Guide](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/objection-guide); and whether the advisor kept the conversation efficient and specific rather than defaulting to a longer, more general pitch this population tends to disengage from.

**Illustrative Only** — a hypothetical coaching note showing format: `coaching_notes: ["COACH-GAP-FILLED — proactively asked whether the recommendation matched the candidate's specific specialization after noticing early signs of quiet disengagement"]`. This describes no real advisor or candidate.

## Advisor Guidance

Coaches should prioritize technical precision, proactive surfacing of scope-and-fit concerns, and conversational efficiency when reviewing calls with this role family, alongside the general framework in [Coach Review Framework](/docs/sales-coaching/coach-review-framework).

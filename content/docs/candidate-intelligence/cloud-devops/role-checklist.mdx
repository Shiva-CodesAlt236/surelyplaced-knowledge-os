# Role Checklist

> This article closes the Cloud & DevOps Role Collection, the fourth complete Role Collection built on the Candidate Intelligence Framework.

## Purpose

This checklist confirms the Cloud & DevOps Role Collection meets the framework's standards and stays consistent with the template the Software Engineering, Data & AI, and Cybersecurity collections established.

## Scope

This checklist covers all fourteen articles in this collection, organized the same way as the three preceding collections: working patterns, technology ecosystem, sales intelligence, advisor guides, and coaching.

## Core Content

**Working patterns and ecosystem**
- [ ] Do [Infrastructure Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/infrastructure-engineer), [Site Reliability Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/site-reliability-engineer), [DevOps / Platform Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/devops-platform-engineer), and [Cloud Architect](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/cloud-architect) stay strictly at the ecosystem level, with no standalone deep dive into a named product?
- [ ] Is every named tool throughout the collection treated purely as an illustrative example, never compared or ranked against another?
- [ ] Is there any point in the collection where one vendor is favored or recommended over a competitor?
- [ ] Does this collection stay clearly distinct from [Cloud Security Engineer](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cybersecurity/cloud-security-engineer) in the Cybersecurity collection rather than duplicating it?

**Sales intelligence**
- [ ] Do [Candidate Behaviour](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/candidate-behaviour) and [Buying Patterns](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/buying-patterns) reflect this role family's particular ownership-and-directness mindset rather than a generic evaluation-style description?

**Advisor guides**
- [ ] Do [Discovery](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/discovery-guide) through [Closing](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/closing-guide) route visa-specific guidance to the matching Visa Playbook?
- [ ] Does each stage guide extend its matching core sales-stage module instead of introducing a new technique of its own?

**Coaching and completeness**
- [ ] Does [Advisor Coaching](/docs/candidate-intelligence/cloud-devops/advisor-coaching) mark its example note as hypothetical only?
- [ ] Does the collection avoid stating any hiring statistic, salary number, guaranteed outcome, or employer-specific expectation anywhere?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the equivalent checklists in the Software Engineering, Data & AI, and Cybersecurity collections and [Profile QA Guidelines](/docs/candidate-intelligence/PROFILE_QA_GUIDELINES), applied to this collection's four working patterns and its overlap-avoidance requirement with the Cybersecurity collection.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this checklist when reviewing this collection, and compare against the three preceding collections' checklists to confirm all four Role Collections remain structurally consistent.

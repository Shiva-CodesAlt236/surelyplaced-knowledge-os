# Presenting Investment Confidently

The same words, delivered two different ways, produce very different reactions. This lesson is about the delivery, not the script.

## Lesson Objective

- State cost at a steady, unhurried pace, without rushing or over-explaining
- Resist the urge to fill the silence right after the number
- Recover composure deliberately if nervousness creeps in mid-conversation

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Notice your own pace right as cost comes up. If it speeds up or your tone shifts, that's the signal to slow down deliberately, not talk faster to get through it.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Priya is analytical and notices congruence — whether Aayush's confidence about the number matches his confidence about everything else in the call. A steady pace with no rushed justification passed that test without either of them naming it directly.
</CoachingNotes>

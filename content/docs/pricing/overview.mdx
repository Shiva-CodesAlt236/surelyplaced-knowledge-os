# Pricing & Investment Psychology — Module Overview

## Purpose

Price is never just a number — it's tied to fear, self-worth, family expectations, and past experience with money. This module goes underneath the objection-handling skills from the previous module to focus specifically on investment psychology: what a candidate actually feels when the cost comes up, and how to present it with genuine confidence and honesty.

## When You'll Use This

This module applies across the entire funnel, not one stage. You'll use it any time investment comes up — introducing cost for the first time in Discussion, handling a specific price objection, discussing payment logistics after a yes in Closing, or recognizing pricing red flags a candidate raises at any point. The skills here are cross-stage on purpose.

## Real-World Examples

- Priya, analytical and direct, wants the actual number early and evaluates it on its own terms rather than being walked slowly toward it.
- Rahul, a student, raises price specifically in the context of a limited budget — a different conversation than a general price objection.
- Neha and her husband weigh the investment against his own H1B-dependent financial picture, a decision that genuinely involves two people.
- Akash, financially strained after a layoff, needs the investment conversation handled with real sensitivity, without either avoiding the number or minimizing what it means for him.

## Skills You'll Build

- Recognizing the psychology behind sticker shock versus a deeper discomfort with self-investment
- Presenting cost at a steady, unhurried pace and holding the silence afterward
- Distinguishing a price objection from a genuine ROI or guarantee question
- Handling payment conversations and discount questions without guessing or overpromising
- Recognizing financial decision-makers and pricing red flags honestly

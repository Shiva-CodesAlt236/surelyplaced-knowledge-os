# Investment Psychology

Spending on your own career isn't a purchase like any other — it's a bet on yourself, and betting on yourself is emotionally loaded for almost everyone.

## Lesson Objective

- Recognize hope, fear, and guilt as normal undercurrents in a spending-on-myself decision
- Never argue guilt or fear away with logic alone
- Ground the conversation in the candidate's own stated goals, not an abstract pitch

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Don't rush to reassure the moment guilt surfaces. Sit with it for a beat, acknowledge it plainly, and only then connect back to the candidate's own goals.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Asking whether investing in herself has felt easy or hard "in the past" let Neha name the real pattern — learned scarcity, not present-day unaffordability. That's a completely different conversation than the one a reassurance-first response would have produced.
</CoachingNotes>

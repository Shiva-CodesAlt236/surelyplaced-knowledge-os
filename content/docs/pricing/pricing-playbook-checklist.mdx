# Pricing Playbook Checklist

A candidate doesn't notice whether you're drawing on "confident delivery" or "ROI discipline" specifically. They notice whether the whole conversation felt steady and honest.

## Lesson Objective

- Run this checklist as a review habit, before and after any pricing conversation
- Confirm confidence and honesty were never in tension with each other
- Know which lesson to return to when a specific gap turns up

# ROI Conversations

A candidate asking for a specific return figure is asking a completely reasonable question. The honest answer is still never a number.

## Lesson Objective

- Redirect a direct ROI question to structural, qualitative value instead of a figure
- Hold that line calmly, even under repeated pressure for a number
- Connect value specifically to the candidate's own goals, not a generic pitch

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Sophisticated evaluators tend to distrust numbers that arrive too easily. Holding the honest line here often builds more credibility than a confident-sounding figure would.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Priya's own analytical instinct meant she recognized and respected the honesty, rather than being frustrated by the lack of a number. That's not guaranteed with every candidate, but it's what honesty consistently earns over time.
</CoachingNotes>

# Discount Guidelines

> **Read this before anything else.** This lesson contains no discount policy, no statement of advisor discretion, and no figure or condition under which a discount might exist. That policy is not documented in this repository. Never improvise, estimate, or infer an answer. If unclear, escalate internally before responding to the candidate — never guess.

Asking for a discount is a completely normal thing to try. The discipline here is in the response, not in the answer, because the answer isn't something to guess at.

**Why this matters:** guessing at discount flexibility, or implying it through soft language, can create an expectation nobody's authorized to honor.
**When to use this:** any time a candidate asks for a discount or special arrangement, at any stage of the funnel.
**What skill this builds:** responding calmly and professionally without guessing, and escalating through the correct internal channel every time.
**What happens next:** the request gets routed properly, and the candidate gets a real answer instead of a guess dressed up as one.

## Lesson Objective

- Respond to a discount request calmly and professionally, without guessing
- Never imply flexibility through tone or soft language
- Escalate through the correct internal channel, every time

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
Sympathy for a genuine situation doesn't change the discipline. Acknowledge the request warmly, then escalate — don't let empathy turn into an informal promise.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Being honest that Aayush couldn't guess, paired with a fast, committed follow-up, respected both Akash's real situation and the integrity of whatever the actual policy turns out to be.
</CoachingNotes>

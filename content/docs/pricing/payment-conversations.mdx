# Payment Conversations

The moment a candidate starts asking practical payment questions is usually a good sign — they're picturing what deciding actually looks like.

## Lesson Objective

- Answer practical payment questions accurately, never from an outdated memory
- Recognize procedural questions as anxiety-management, not just logistics
- Notice when a payment conversation reveals someone else is part of the decision

## Coaching Notes

<CoachingNotes title="Coaching Notes">
A run of very practical, procedural questions can be a way of managing anxiety through control. Respond to the underlying feeling, not just the literal question.
</CoachingNotes>

<CoachingNotes title="Why This Worked">
Naming that understanding the process can feel calming, without assuming that's the whole story, let Rahul confirm it himself rather than being told what he was feeling.
</CoachingNotes>

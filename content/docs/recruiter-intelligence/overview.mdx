# Recruiter Intelligence — Overview

> **Scope note:** "Recruiter" throughout this module means someone working on an employer's side of a hiring process — internal or agency — evaluating candidates for that employer's open role. It does not refer to Surely Placed, to any other program or consultancy a candidate might also be engaged with, or to any competing service. Questions about a candidate's relationship with another program belong to [Already Working With a Consultancy](/docs/objections/already-working-with-a-consultancy), not this module.

## Purpose

A candidate who understands how the recruiter on the other side of the table actually works — what they're evaluating, who they report to, what constraints they operate under — tends to navigate that process with more confidence and less anxiety than one who treats the recruiter as an unpredictable gatekeeper. This module gives advisors a realistic, general picture of employer-side recruiting to share with candidates.

## Scope

This module covers how employer recruiting functions generally operate — roles, workflow, and decision patterns in general terms. It does not describe any specific employer's actual process, does not claim to know how a specific recruiter will behave, and does not promise a particular outcome from any recruiter interaction.

[Recruiter Workflow](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/recruiter-workflow) lays out the general shape of a hiring process from the recruiter's side. [Candidate Screening Process](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/candidate-screening-process) and [Recruiter Decision Framework](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/recruiter-decision-framework) cover what a recruiter is generally doing at each stage. [Recruiter Red Flags](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/recruiter-red-flags) covers what to notice and route, [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide) covers the coaching conversation, and [Recruiter Intelligence Checklist](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/recruiter-intelligence-checklist) closes the module.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This module sits alongside [Recruiter Screening](/docs/interview-intelligence/recruiter-screening), which coaches a candidate through the recruiter-screening call itself — this module instead builds the candidate's broader understanding of the recruiting function behind that call, and of the [Resume Intelligence](/docs/resume-intelligence/overview) and [LinkedIn Intelligence](/docs/linkedin-intelligence/overview) artifacts a recruiter actually reviews.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this module to help a candidate see the recruiting process as a structured, human system rather than an opaque one, without ever claiming certainty about a specific employer's actual process or a specific recruiter's behavior. Route any question about Surely Placed's own relationship to another program or consultancy to the Objection Handling module instead.

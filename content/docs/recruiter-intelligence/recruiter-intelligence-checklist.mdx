# Recruiter Intelligence Checklist

## Purpose

Nine articles precede this one — the general workflow, the internal-versus-agency and technical-recruiter distinctions, hiring-manager collaboration, screening and decision factors, red flags, and the coaching conversation itself. This checklist pulls them into a single working reference.

## Scope

Run through this list as a final gut-check before a recruiter-focused conversation with a candidate, not as a new source of guidance — every item traces back to a specific article above it. This checklist stops short of describing any specific employer's actual recruiting process, since none is documented here.

## Core Content

**General understanding**
- [ ] Can I explain [Recruiter Workflow](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/recruiter-workflow)'s general shape in plain terms a candidate would find useful, without implying it's universal?
- [ ] Have I correctly distinguished internal from agency recruiters, and generalist from technical recruiters, without ranking either pair?

**Process and decision factors**
- [ ] Have I connected [Candidate Screening Process](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/candidate-screening-process) back to the candidate's existing resume and LinkedIn preparation?
- [ ] Have I presented [Recruiter Decision Framework](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/recruiter-decision-framework)'s categories as illustrative, not as a weighted or guaranteed formula?
- [ ] Have I acknowledged the internal coordination factors, per [Hiring Manager Collaboration](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/hiring-manager-collaboration), that sit outside a candidate's control?

**Red flags and escalation**
- [ ] Have I distinguished a genuine concern in [Recruiter Red Flags](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/recruiter-red-flags) from ordinary process friction?
- [ ] Have I routed any payment request, pressure tactic, or authorization-related concern rather than resolving it myself?

**Coaching conversation**
- [ ] Did I ground the conversation in the candidate's actual, specific situation per [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/recruiter-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide)?
- [ ] Does the candidate leave the conversation feeling genuinely more oriented, not just informed?

## Cross-Module Alignment

Treat this as a quick pre-conversation check, and go back to the specific article behind any item that feels uncertain rather than relying on this summary alone.

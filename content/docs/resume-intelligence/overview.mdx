# Resume Intelligence — Overview

> This module covers how an advisor reads, discusses, and coaches around a resume — never a specific Surely Placed resume-writing template, editing service, or guaranteed turnaround this repository doesn't define. Where a candidate needs an actual document produced or reviewed under a specific internal process, follow the latest approved internal process rather than anything implied here.

## Purpose

A resume is often the first concrete artifact an advisor and a candidate work through together, and it keeps resurfacing at nearly every later stage — during discovery while establishing background, during discussion while framing the value of preparation, and again once employer conversations begin. This module gives an advisor a consistent way to read a resume, talk about it with a candidate, and coach improvements without overstepping into work that belongs to a qualified writer or the candidate's own judgment.

## Scope

This module addresses how a resume communicates — structure, clarity, framing, credibility signals — not a verdict on whether a candidate is a strong hire. No article here rewrites a candidate's resume on the page, predicts an employer's reaction to it, or claims a specific format guarantees a callback; resume evaluation is inherently judgment-based, and this module treats it that way throughout.

Treat this module as a way to help a candidate present real experience clearly, not as license to invent or embellish anything the underlying facts don't support.

# Resume Intelligence Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist closes the Resume Intelligence module, gathering its ten articles — overview, the reading framework, red flags, formatting, technical and industry variation, international considerations, storytelling, and coaching — into one working reference an advisor can run through before or during a resume conversation.

## Scope

Use this checklist as a consistency check against the nine articles that precede it, not as a source of new guidance in its own right. It stops short of describing any specific resume-production service, since this repository doesn't define one.

## Core Content

**Reading and framework**
- [ ] Have I read the resume in all three passes from [Resume Reading Framework](/docs/resume-intelligence/resume-reading-framework) — structure, substance, and signal?
- [ ] Have I noted any gap between the resume's overall impression and what I already know about this candidate?

**Red flags and formatting**
- [ ] Have I checked for anything in [Resume Red Flags](/docs/resume-intelligence/resume-red-flags) worth a direct conversation or a routed escalation?
- [ ] Have I avoided treating a non-linear path or unconventional formatting as a defect on its own?
- [ ] Have I stayed at the level of general formatting principle in any ATS discussion, without claiming a guaranteed outcome?

**Content and context**
- [ ] Have I evaluated technical content for clarity and consistency only, routing any genuine skill judgment to a subject matter expert?
- [ ] Have I accounted for this candidate's specific field using [Industry Resume Differences](/docs/resume-intelligence/industry-resume-differences) rather than one universal standard?
- [ ] Where relevant, have I kept any international-student framing strictly non-legal, routing authorization questions to [Visa Discovery Framework](/docs/visa-playbooks/visa-discovery-framework)?

**Narrative and conversation**
- [ ] Can the candidate now state their own throughline, or an honest explanation for a pivot, per [Resume Storytelling](/docs/resume-intelligence/resume-storytelling)?
- [ ] Did the coaching conversation itself follow [Advisor Coaching Guide](/docs/resume-intelligence/advisor-coaching-guide) — candidate-led, balanced, and calm?

## Cross-Module Alignment

This checklist mirrors the closing-checklist pattern used across every other module in this repository, adapted here to Resume Intelligence's ten articles and its own cross-stage scope.

## Advisor Guidance

Run this checklist before any resume-focused conversation as a quick self-check, and return to the specific article behind any item that feels shaky rather than relying on this summary alone.

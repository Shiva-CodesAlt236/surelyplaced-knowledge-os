# Industry Resume Differences

> **Illustrative Only:** This article describes general variation in resume convention across fields. It does not rank fields, does not claim one field's conventions are superior, and does not substitute for the field-specific detail in [Industry Playbooks](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework).

## Purpose

A resume convention that reads as strong in one field can read as unfocused in another. This article gives advisors a general awareness of that variation so a resume conversation accounts for the candidate's actual field rather than applying one universal standard.

## Scope

This article covers general presentation convention — how much detail is typical, what's usually emphasized — not field-specific technical substance, which belongs to the corresponding [Industry Playbook](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework) or [Role Collection](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README). It does not compare fields against each other or imply one is preferable.

## Core Content

Some fields tend to reward a resume with dense, specific technical detail near the top — precise tools, systems, and outcomes stated plainly. Others tend to reward a resume that leads with broader project or program scope before technical specifics, reflecting a role where cross-functional coordination is as central as the technical work itself. Neither approach is universally correct; the right emphasis follows the candidate's actual field and role.

Where a candidate's background spans a regulated or compliance-sensitive field, a resume is generally not the place to state specific regulatory or clinical qualifications with authority the advisor can't verify — that judgment belongs with an appropriate expert, consistent with how this repository already treats regulated-field content elsewhere.

Advisors coaching a candidate toward a specific field's resume conventions should treat the corresponding Industry Playbook or Role Collection as the deeper reference, rather than this article inventing field-specific guidance it isn't positioned to provide in full.

## Cross-Module Alignment

This article is a general awareness layer above the field-specific depth already established in [Industry Playbooks](/docs/industry-playbooks/industry-discovery-framework) and the Role Collections under [Candidate Intelligence](/docs/candidate-intelligence/README), and it defers to both rather than duplicating their content.

## Advisor Guidance

Use this article to calibrate expectations before a resume conversation, then consult the specific Industry Playbook or Role Collection for a candidate's actual field before giving detailed guidance. Never imply that one field's resume convention is objectively better than another's.
